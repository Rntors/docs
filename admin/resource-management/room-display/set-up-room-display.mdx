title: "Set up Room display"
---

Room display shows real-time availability on a tablet outside a room. Customers can identify a free room, scan a QR code to book, and check in when abandoned-booking protection is enabled.

## Prerequisites

- A tablet with a screen of at least 7 inches.
- A supported Android or iPadOS version and access to its app store.
- Rooms configured in RNTOR with photos.
- Room display enabled for the location.

## Install and activate Room display

1. Install the RNTOR Room display app on the tablet.
2. Open the app and select **Log in**.
3. Sign in with an administrator account.
4. Choose the network and location.
5. Select the room assigned to this tablet.

## Exit Room display

Disable Guided Access on iPad or Screen Pinning on Android, then close the app.

## Log out

Close and reopen the app, select **(…) → Log out**, and confirm.

## What Room display provides

- Live availability colors.
- Current and upcoming booking details.
- QR-code access to the booking calendar.
- Check-in for eligible free-room bookings.
- Automatic cancellation of abandoned bookings.
