Room display helps customers understand room availability at a glance and act without administrator assistance.

## Availability indicators

| Display color | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Green** | The room is available long enough for the minimum booking duration. |
| **Yellow** | The room will become available soon. |
| **Red** | The room is occupied or unavailable for the near-term period. |

The display can show the current booking, progress, and upcoming reservations. Booking titles follow each customer’s privacy settings.

## Ad-hoc bookings with QR code

A unique QR code links directly to the room’s booking calendar. The customer scans it, signs in if needed, chooses an available time, and confirms the booking.

## Abandoned-booking protection

For eligible free rooms, enable **Check in** in the room settings.

- **Early check in** controls how soon before the start the button appears.
- **Abandoned threshold** controls how long RNTOR waits after the start before canceling a no-show.
- A successful check-in changes the booking from **Tentative** to **Confirmed**.

## Troubleshoot Room display

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The room does not appear during setup">
    Confirm that the room belongs to the selected location, is enabled, and the administrator has access to it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The display shows outdated availability">
    Check the tablet’s internet connection, reopen the app, and verify that the booking appears in the RNTOR calendar.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The QR code does not open the correct room">
    Confirm the tablet is assigned to the intended room, then log out and activate Room display again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Check in button is missing">
    Verify that the room is completely free, check-in protection is enabled, and the current time falls inside the early check-in window.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A booking was canceled automatically">
    The abandoned threshold elapsed before check-in. Review the room’s check-in timing and the booking activity.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
