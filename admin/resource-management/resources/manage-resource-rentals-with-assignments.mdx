title: "Manage resource rentals with assignments"
description: "Learn how to assign resources to customers for rental and track all rentals using the Assignments timeline"

The Assignments timeline gives you a visual overview of all current and upcoming long-term rentals for your resources—such as private offices or dedicated desks. Assignments are created automatically when a customer subscribes to a rental or when an administrator sets up a subscription on their behalf.

## Assign resources to customers for rental

1. Go to the **Customers** page.
2. Open the customer's profile.
3. [Create](/admin/billing/subscriptions/set-up-subscriptions) or [edit](/admin/billing/subscriptions/edit-a-subscription) their subscription.
4. Add a subscription item.
5. In the ****Product**** field, select a resource (desk, office, etc.).
6. In the ****Tier**** field, choose one of the tiers (pricing options) you added in this resource settings or choose **Custom** to set up the subscription details from scratch.
7. Manage the ****Entire resource**** switch to choose the appropriate assignment type.

![Resource Management Rentals Track Resource Rentals With Assignments 1](/images/resource-management-rentals-track-resource-rentals-with-assignments-1.png)

<Info>
  If you [configured long-term rental pricing](/admin/resource-management/resources/set-up-pricing) in resource settings, customers can also [apply or sign up](/admin/resource-management/resources/rental-applications-or-immediate-sign-ups) for a resource rental by themselves and be automatically assigned to the resource.
</Info>

​

### Entire resource

Use it when:

* The customer rents the whole resource, such as:
  * A private office
  * A meeting room
  * A parking lot rented entirely by one company
* A company rents a full resource (e.g., an office or desk), and their company members book seats internally ([hybrid office setup](/admin/resource-management/resources/hybrid-offices)).

Entire resource assignment makes the resource only visible to the assigned customer and hides it from others.

![Resource Management Rentals Track Resource Rentals With Assignments 2](/images/resource-management-rentals-track-resource-rentals-with-assignments-2.png)

### Quantity of seats/spaces/items

Use it when:

* The customer rents part of a shared resource, such as:
  * 1 dedicated desk in a 10-desk zone
  * 3 parking spaces in a 20-space lot

<Info>
  With this assignment type, customers don't book their seat(s) during their subscription period; they're reserved for the customer.
</Info>

* You want the resource to remain visible to others even when fully occupied.
  * In this case, leave the “Entire resource” option off and assign the maximum quantity instead.
  * The resource will appear with the “Fully occupied” label, allowing others to express interest for future availability.

![Resource Management Rentals Track Resource Rentals With Assignments 3](/images/resource-management-rentals-track-resource-rentals-with-assignments-3.png)

How resources assigned by seats appear to others:

* Users see the the reduced resource availability (e.g., 7/10 available).
* When capacity reaches zero, it remains visible with a “Fully occupied” label.

![Resource Management Rentals Track Resource Rentals With Assignments 4](/images/resource-management-rentals-track-resource-rentals-with-assignments-4.png)

<Info>
  Rooms can only be assigned entirely.
</Info>

## Track all rentals on the Assignments page

1. Go to **Resources** > **Assignments.**
2. The **Assignments** page gives you a consolidated overview of:
   * Resources rented by customers
   * Resources available for rental

### How to read the Assignments timeline

Each resource appears with one or more bars labeled with customer names.

* Thicker bars indicate entire resource assignments.

![Resource Management Rentals Track Resource Rentals With Assignments 5](/images/resource-management-rentals-track-resource-rentals-with-assignments-5.png)

* Thinner bars indicate a [partial assignments](#quantity-of-seatsspacesitems).
  * The number of seats is shown next to the custome name
  * If no seat count appeaars on the thin bar, it means 1 seat is assigned.

![Resource Management Rentals Track Resource Rentals With Assignments 6](/images/resource-management-rentals-track-resource-rentals-with-assignments-6.png)

* Each resource shows its total capacity, so you can quickly assess remaining availability.

![Resource Management Rentals Track Resource Rentals With Assignments 7](/images/resource-management-rentals-track-resource-rentals-with-assignments-7.png)

* Unassigned resources appear without any bars.

**Interactions:**

* Hover over a bar to see the rental period. It is shown based on the customer's [subscription item dates](/admin/billing/subscriptions/set-up-subscriptions).
  * For subscriptions with no end date set, the assignment shows as indefinite.
* Click a bar to open the customer's profile and [manage the resource assignment](/admin/billing/subscriptions/edit-a-subscription) in their subscription.
* Click a resource to view its details.

**Filters:**

* Use the date picker to check availability for a specific period.
* Use the “Resource” dropdown to view all resources or filter resource of a specific type.

![Resource Management Rentals Track Resource Rentals With Assignments 8](/images/resource-management-rentals-track-resource-rentals-with-assignments-8.png)

<Info>
  The **Assignments** page is for tracking long-term rentals only — it does not include bookings.
</Info>

### Floor plan view

* If you have the [Floor plans add-on](/admin/resource-management/floors/use-floor-plans), switch to **Floors** to see the occupancy visually on floor plans.
* Use **Date** or “Resource” filters to check availability for a specific period or a specific resource type.

![Resource Management Rentals Track Resource Rentals With Assignments 9](/images/resource-management-rentals-track-resource-rentals-with-assignments-9.png)

<Info>
  You can also view real-time availability and current assignments for each resource directly on the **Resources** pages.
</Info>

**FAQs**

<Accordion title="Why do I see the error 'Assignments conflict with other assignments' when creating a subscription for the customer?">
  There are several reasons why you see this error message:

  * One or more resources you've added to the customer's subscription are already assigned to another customer for the same dates or with a different assignment type (entire VS by seat).

  To fix this:

  1. Go to **Resources > Assignments.**
  2. Find the resource in question to check:Tip:
     * Which customer it's assigned to
     * The assignment dates
     * The type of assignment
     * Wide bars = Entire resource assignments
     * Narrow bars = Assignments by seats
  3. To fix the conflict:
     * Choose a different resource for your new customer.
     * Go to the profile of the customer who currently rents this resource and remove this resource from their subscription items or adjust its dates.
     * If the resource is assigned by seat, assign the resource to the new customer by seat as well.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I remove resource assignments?">
  1. Go to the **Resources > Assignments** page or to **Rooms, Desks, Offices, Parking,** or **Equipment** pages.
  2. Find the resource in question to check which customer it's assigned to.
  3. Go to the **Customers** page.
  4. Open the profile of the customer who currently rents this resource.
  5. [Edit their subscription](/admin/billing/subscriptions/edit-a-subscription) and remove this resource from their subscription items, or adjust the item's rental dates to assign it to a new customer for other dates.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I view room and desk bookings on the Assignments timeline?">
  One-off bookings, used for short-term or day-specific use, are not displayed on the **Assignments** timeline. These can be tracked on respective resource pages or through [analytics tools.](/admin/analytics-reports/dashboards)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I assign a resource to the customer on specific weekdays?">
  If a customer rents an office, desks, room, or other resource on specific days of the week (for example, only on Mondays and Tuesdays), you can:

  1. [Create a recurring booking](/admin/resource-management/resources/manage-bookings#create-a-recurring-booking) for that resource for the customer with the required schedule.
  2. [Create a subscription](/admin/billing/subscriptions/set-up-subscriptions) to invoice the customer for rental.

  * Do not select this resource as a subscription item — this would assign it to the customer every day.
  * Instead, choose a plan or **Custom** option to  set up the charge only.
</Accordion>
