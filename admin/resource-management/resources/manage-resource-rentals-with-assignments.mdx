---
title: "Manage resource rentals with assignments"
---

The **Assignments** timeline gives administrators one view of current and upcoming long-term rentals for offices, desks, parking, and other resources.

## Assign a resource to a customer

1. Open the customer profile.
2. Create or edit the subscription.
3. Add a subscription item.
4. Choose the resource in **Product**.
5. Select a configured tier or **Custom**.
6. Choose whether the customer rents the **Entire resource** or a quantity.
7. Save the subscription.

### Entire resource

Use this for a full office, meeting room, or complete parking area. The assigned resource becomes exclusive to that customer for the assignment period.

### Quantity of seats, spaces, or items

Use quantity assignments for shared resources, such as three desks in a ten-seat zone. RNTOR reduces the displayed availability by the assigned quantity while keeping the remaining capacity available.

Rooms can only be assigned as an entire resource.

## Track rentals

Go to **Resources → Assignments**. Each timeline bar identifies the customer and assignment period.

- A thicker bar represents an entire-resource assignment.
- A thinner bar represents a quantity assignment.
- The quantity appears with the customer name when more than one unit is assigned.
