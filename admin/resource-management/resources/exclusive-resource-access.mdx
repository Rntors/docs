---
title: "Exclusive resource access"
---

Use exclusive access for premium rooms, trained-member facilities, private company areas, or any resource that should not be available to everyone.

## Configure exclusive access

1. Open the relevant resource settings.
2. Set its **Visibility** and **Booking** access level.
3. Save the resource.
4. Grant access through a plan, subscription item, or individual customer subscription.

<Frame>
</Frame>

### Grant access through a plan

Edit the plan or resource tier, open **Exclusive access**, and select the resource. Customers receive access while their eligible subscription is active.

### Grant access to an individual

Edit the customer’s subscription item and select the resource under **Exclusive access**. Use this for qualifications that apply to one person, such as studio training.

## Customer experience

Eligible customers can see and book the resource according to its normal rules. Other customers either do not see it or cannot complete a booking, depending on the visibility configuration.

Access updates automatically when the qualifying subscription starts, changes, expires, or is canceled.
