---
title: "Dynamic pricing"
---

Dynamic pricing helps maximize resource revenue and occupancy by applying configured price variations to bookings.

## Prerequisites

- Set up the resource and its standard [booking price](/admin/resource-management/resources/set-up-pricing).
- Confirm that money payments are enabled.
- Decide which dates, days, or time ranges need a different rate.

## Configure dynamic pricing

1. Open the resource settings.
2. Go to **Pricing → Dynamic pricing**.
3. Select **Add rule**.
4. Choose the applicable days, dates, and time range.
5. Enter the price adjustment.
6. Save the rule and the resource.

## How rules work

A rule affects only bookings that match its conditions. The customer sees the calculated price before confirming payment. If more than one rule could match the same booking, review their scope carefully to avoid unintended combinations.

## Common uses

- Increase prices during peak hours.
- Offer lower rates during quiet periods.
- Apply weekend or evening pricing.
- Create special prices for seasonal dates.

## Edit or remove a rule

Open the dynamic pricing list for the resource, choose the rule, and edit or delete it. Changes apply to new bookings; previously completed transactions are not recalculated.
