---
title: "Set up pricing"
---

RNTOR lets you offer one resource with multiple pricing options for short-term bookings and long-term rentals.

## Booking pricing

1. Open the resource and select **Edit**.
2. Go to **Pricing**.
3. Enable **Money**, **Credits**, or **Day passes** as needed.
4. Add the amount for each booking duration.
5. Save the resource.

### Money

Set the price in the location currency. When taxes apply, the final amount follows the location’s [tax settings](/admin/billing/taxes/set-up-taxes).

### Credits

Set how many credits are deducted for a booking. Learn how to configure and sell credits in [Credits](/admin/billing/credits-and-day-passes/credits).

### Day passes

Day passes are intended for desk access. Configure how many passes a booking consumes in [Day passes](/admin/billing/credits-and-day-passes/day-passes).

## Price tiers

Use tiers when the same resource is sold with different conditions, such as hourly, daily, monthly, or yearly rental options. Give every tier a clear customer-facing name and configure its amount, tax, billing period, and availability.

## Free resources

Leave paid methods disabled when a resource is completely free. Administrators can also create a booking with the **Free** method when recording a complimentary reservation.

## Pricing precedence

When a customer has an eligible plan or subscription benefit, that assigned price or included allowance is used first. Otherwise, RNTOR shows the resource’s default public price.

## Change existing prices

Price changes apply to new bookings or rentals. Existing payments, invoices, and active subscription terms retain their recorded values unless an administrator edits them separately.
