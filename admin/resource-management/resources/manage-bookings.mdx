Administrators can create, edit, and cancel bookings for customers or themselves in the admin portal.

## Create a booking

1. Open the relevant resource page — **Rooms**, **Desks**, **Offices**, **Parking**, or **Equipment**.
2. Start creating a booking.
3. Choose the booking date, time, and number of seats. Optionally, add a title, memo, or apply a coupon discount.
4. Choose the customer in the **Booker** field.
5. Choose the [payment method](/admin/billing/payments/payment-methods) to charge the customer for the booking.
6. If you book for another customer or add invitees, choose whether to notify them about the booking.

<Info>
  Administrators can also record bookings for past dates.
</Info>

![Resource Management Resources Manage Bookings Create Booking](/images/resource-management-resources-manage-bookings-create-booking.png)

### Create a recurring booking

When a customer needs to book the same resource on a recurring schedule — for example, every Friday from 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM — you can create a recurring booking for them:

1. Start creating a booking.
2. Under the **Repeat** settings, choose one of the suggested recurrence patterns, or select **Custom** to set your own recurrence pattern.
3. In the **End repeat** field, choose:

* **On date** — to set a specific final booking date, or
* **Never** — to set an open-ended recurrence.
* For **Custom** recurrences, you can also choose **After number of occurrences** to schedule a specific number of recurring bookings.

4. Choose the customer for whom you're booking in the **Booker** field.

![Resource Management Resources Manage Bookings Recurring Bookings](/images/resource-management-resources-manage-bookings-recurring-bookings.png)

<Tip>
  Administrators can currently make recurring bookings only with the **Free** payment method. Users can make recurring bookings only for completely free resources.

  If you need to charge the customer, create a recurring booking on their behalf with the **Free** method, then [issue an invoice](/admin/billing/invoices/issue-invoices#manual-one-off-invoices) for it, or deduct from their [credit](/admin/billing/credits-and-day-passes/credits#deduct-credits) or [day pass](/admin/billing/credits-and-day-passes/day-passes#deduct-day-passes) balance manually.
</Tip>

## Edit a booking

To edit a booking, users and administrators follow these steps:

1. Open the relevant booking from the **Resources, Bookings,** or customer profile page.
2. Click **(...)** in the top right corner > **Edit**.
3. Make your changes and save. If the booking was made for another customer or has invitees, choose whether to notify them about the change.

![Resource Management Bookings Edit A Booking 1](/images/resource-management-bookings-edit-a-booking-1.png)

Booking editing permissions differ for users and administrators.

### How users edit bookings

| Payment method/Change               | Credits, day passes, invoice, or free resource                                                                                                             | Payment gateway                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reschedule (change start date/time) | Up to a specific time before the booking starts, based on the resource's [rescheduling policy.](/admin/billing/payments/refunds)                                 | Up to a specific time before the booking starts, based on the resource's [rescheduling policy](/admin/billing/payments/refunds). |
| Extend                              | Anytime before or during the booking, with an extra charge.                                                                                                | Can't extend. The prepaid amount is fixed. Create one more booking instead.                                                |
| Shorten                             | Up to a specific time before the booking starts, based on the resource's [rescheduling policy](/admin/billing/payments/refunds), with a refund.                  | Can't shorten. The prepaid amount is fixed.                                                                                |
| Change number of seats/items        | Up to a specific time before the booking starts, based on the resource's [rescheduling policy](/admin/billing/payments/refunds), with an extra charge or refund. | Can't change. The prepaid amount is fixed.                                                                                 |

### How administrators edit bookings

| Payment method/Change               | Credits, day passes, invoice, free resource                                   | Payment gateway, External                                                   |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reschedule (change start date/time) | Anytime before, during, or after the booking.                                 | Anytime before, during, or after the booking.                               |
| Extend                              | Anytime before, during, or after the booking, with an extra charge.           | Can't extend. The prepaid amount is fixed. Create one more booking instead. |
| Shorten                             | Anytime before, during, or after the booking, with a refund.                  | Can't shorten. The prepaid amount is fixed.                                 |
| Change number of seats/items        | Anytime before, during, or after the booking, with an extra charge or refund. | Can't change. The prepaid amount is fixed.                                  |

**Notes**

* If an administrator created a booking for a user, the user cannot edit it, regardless of the payment method.
* Both users and administrators can edit the booking title, memo, and invitees at any time—before, during, or after the booking.
* Every booking modification triggers a dedicated transaction to handle additional charges or refunds. This approach allows both users and administrators to audit booking history and associated costs with ease.
* When editing or canceling a recurring booking, administrators can choose to apply the change to `This occurrence` only, or to `This and future` bookings.

## Track bookings

There are several ways to track all bookings:

* Enable [admin notifications](/admin/administration/your-account/notifications/set-up-notifications) to receive alerts whenever a new booking is created or canceled.
* Go to the **Resources > Bookings** page to view a comprehensive list of all resource bookings in one place.
  * Filter by date to see all bookings on, before, or after a specific date.
  * Filter bookings by status.
  * Filter by resource type to see bookings for a specific type of resource, such as rooms, desks, offices, parking, or equipment.
  * Filter by a specific resource to see all bookings for that individual resource only.
  * Filter by user to view all bookings of a specific customer.

* To track bookings for specific resource types, go to their dedicated pages. From there, you can filter by date to see bookings for today or any specific day:
  * **Rooms:** Go to **Resources > Rooms >** **Calendar**
  * **Desks:** Go to **Resources > Desks >** **Bookings**
  * **Offices:** Go to **Resources > Offices >** **Bookings**
  * **Parking:** Go to **Resources > Parking >** **Bookings**
  * **Equipment:** Go to **Resources > Equipment >** **Timeline**.

### Booking colors

When viewing room bookings on a calendar, you can identify the user type and booking status by the booking colors and style:

* Blue - the booking of an administrator.
* Green - the booking of a member.
* Yellow - the booking of a nonmember.
* Outlined booking with transparent background - booking in tentative status.

## Cancel a booking

To cancel a booking, users and administrators follow these steps:

1. Open the relevant booking from the **Resources, Bookings,** or customer profile page.
2. Click **(...)** in the top right corner > **Cancel**.

Bookings can be canceled at any time, however, the refund depends on the resource's rescheduling policy and the payment method used.

| Bookings                                                                                      | Monetary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Credit                                                   |
| :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------- |
| Canceled according to the [**resource’s rescheduling policy**](/admin/billing/payments/refunds)     | <ul><li>If [**Stripe**](/admin/billing/payments/refunds) or **KakaoPay** payment gateways are connected and refunds enabled, [**the refund is automatic**](/admin/billing/payments/refunds).</li><li>If another payment gateway is connected, administrators can [**manually refund**](/admin/billing/payments/refunds) bookings via the payment gateway dashboard.</li></ul> | [**The refund is automatic**](/admin/billing/payments/refunds) |
| Canceled not according to the [**resrouce’s rescheduling policy**](/admin/billing/payments/refunds) | The booking is not refunded.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | The booking is not refunded.                             |

<Accordion title="Can I book an office?">
  Office resources are designed primarily for long-term rental assignments, but short-term bookings are also possible in certain cases:

  * **Assigned offices** — If an entire office is assigned to a customer for rental, that member or company can book it for free on a daily basis (e.g. a hybrid office for a company, where company members book seats daily).
  * **Unassigned offices** — If an office is not assigned, you as an administrator can manually book it for any interested customer.
    * Office bookings are always full-day only — hourly booking is not available.
    * Office bookings always use the **Free** payment method — you can issue a separate invoice to the customer for office usage as needed.
    * Customers cannot book unassigned offices for a day — they can only subscribe for a long-term rental. Only administrators can make  office bookings for a day for  customers on request.
    * Customers cannot book unassigned offices for a day — they can only subscribe for a long-term rental. Only administrators can make daily office bookings for  customers on request.
    * Customers cannot book unassigned offices for a day — they can only subscribe for a long-term rental. Only administrators can make daily office bookings for  customers on request.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My customer can't book a desk with day passes">
  This desk must be not a **Hot desk** type or the **Day passes** switch is turned off in its settings. To allow booking it with day passes:

  1. Go to **Resources > Desks.**
  2. Start editing this specific desk.
  3. Verify it is set to the **Hot desk** type. Only hot desks can be booked with day passes.
  4. Verify it has the **Day passes** switch enabled.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why do I get &#x22;Another booking already exists at the same time&#x22; when scheduling a recurring booking?">
  This error means that on one or more dates in the recurring series, there is already a booking scheduled for that resource at that time. To identify the conflicting booking, go to **Analytics**, generate a **Bookings** report, and filter it by the specific resource. This will show all existing bookings for that resource so you can find which one overlaps with your recurring series.
</Accordion>
