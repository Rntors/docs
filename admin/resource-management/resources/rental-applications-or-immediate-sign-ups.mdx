---
title: "Rental applications or immediate sign-ups"
---

Choose whether customers can apply for a resource rental or sign up immediately without administrator approval.

## Prerequisites

- Add the resource and configure long-term [pricing tiers](/admin/resource-management/resources/set-up-pricing).
- Confirm the resource visibility.
- Prepare any plan, contract, deposit, or billing requirements.

## Enable rental applications

1. Open the resource settings.
2. Enable long-term rental pricing.
3. Set the rental action to **Apply**.
4. Add the instructions customers should see.
5. Save.

<Frame>
</Frame>

Customers select an available resource or tier and submit their details. Administrators review the request, agree on terms, and create the subscription and assignment.

## Enable immediate sign-ups

Select **Sign up** when eligible customers may start the rental themselves. Configure the start-date rules, billing interval, payment, and any required agreement.

<Frame>
</Frame>

When sign-up succeeds, RNTOR creates the customer subscription and assigns the resource automatically.

## Availability

Entire-resource assignments block the resource for other customers during the rental. Quantity assignments reduce the remaining capacity. Review [Assignments](/admin/resource-management/resources/manage-resource-rentals-with-assignments) to track occupancy.
