---
title: "Set up resources"
---

Use resources to manage availability, bookings, and rentals across your locations. RNTOR supports **Rooms**, **Desks**, **Offices**, **Parking**, and **Equipment**.

## Enable a resource type

1. Open the menu **(…)** next to the location name.
2. Go to **Location settings → Resources**.
3. Turn on the resource type you want to use.
4. Configure its page name, visibility, and booking rules.

## Add a resource

1. Open the relevant resource page.
2. Select **Add**.
3. Enter the **name**, **description**, **capacity**, and location details.
4. Upload photos and choose the resource visibility.
5. Configure booking access, availability, and notifications.
6. Select **Create**.

### Rooms

Add meeting rooms, phone booths, studios, event spaces, or any area booked for a specific time. Define the minimum and maximum booking duration, booking increment, capacity, and how far ahead customers can book.

### Desks

Use desks for hot-desk, dedicated-desk, half-day, full-day, or hourly access. Set the total number of seats when several equivalent desks share one availability pool.

### Offices

Create offices for short-term bookings or long-term rentals. Add the office capacity and configure pricing for the rental periods you offer.

### Parking

Add individual parking spaces or a shared parking pool. Capacity determines how many simultaneous reservations the resource accepts.

### Equipment

List equipment that customers can reserve, such as monitors, podcast kits, lockers, or presentation devices.

## Availability and visibility

Use **Visibility** to control who can discover the resource and **Booking** to control who can reserve it immediately. If booking access is narrower than visibility, eligible users can be directed to apply instead. See [Applications for bookings](/admin/resource-management/resources/applications-for-bookings).

## Edit, reorder, or delete resources

Open the resource settings to update its details. Reorder resources from the resource list when you want them to appear in a different sequence. Before deleting a resource, export or review its future bookings and active rentals.
