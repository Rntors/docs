---
title: "Hybrid offices"
---

Hybrid offices combine a long-term company rental with daily seat bookings for the company’s members.

## Set up a hybrid office

1. Create an **Office** resource and set its seat capacity.
2. Add the booking availability and duration rules.
3. Create long-term rental pricing.
4. Assign the entire office to the company through its subscription.
5. Enable company members to book seats inside the assigned office.

## Company rental

The company’s active subscription controls the assignment period. Because the office is assigned entirely, it is hidden from unrelated customers while the rental is active.

## Member bookings

Company members open the office calendar and reserve the number of seats they need. RNTOR calculates availability from the office capacity and overlapping member bookings.

</Frame>

## Recommendations

- Set capacity to the actual number of usable seats.
- Define the minimum and maximum booking duration.
- Decide how far ahead members may reserve.
- Use booking titles and notifications to help the company coordinate attendance.
- Review the booking calendar and assignment timeline when changing the rental period.
