---
title: "Manage floor plans"
---

Administrators use floor settings to upload, name, map, update, and remove interactive plans.

Only administrators with the required **Settings** permission can edit floor plans.

## Upload a floor plan

1. Go to **Location settings → Resources → Floors**.
2. Select **Add floor**.
3. Upload the prepared SVG file.
4. Enter the floor name and visibility.
5. Map the resources.
6. Select **Create**.

## Map resources

Select each interactive area and connect it to the corresponding room, desk, or office in RNTOR. Check that names and boundaries match before publishing.

</Frame>

## Update a floor

Open the floor settings to rename it, change visibility, upload a revised plan, or update mappings. Recheck all mapped areas after replacing the SVG.

## Delete a floor

Open the floor menu, choose **Delete**, and confirm. Deleting the floor plan does not delete the underlying resources or their bookings.
