---
title: "Use floor plans"
---

Interactive floor plans help administrators monitor availability and let customers choose resources visually.

## How administrators use floor plans

Floor views are available from resource and assignment pages.

### Resources

1. Open **Resources → Rooms**, **Desks**, or **Offices**.
2. Select the **Floors** tab.
3. Choose a date and floor.

Typical indicators distinguish available, booked, assigned, and unavailable resources.

### Assignments

Open **Resources → Assignments → Floors**, select a date, filter the resource type, and choose a floor. Use the map to identify resources that are available, partially assigned, or fully assigned.

## How customers use floor plans

Customers open **Rooms**, **Desks**, or **Offices** in the member portal or mobile app, choose a floor, and select an available resource.

- Rooms open the booking calendar.
- Desk zones allow the customer to choose a quantity.
- Offices show booking or rental actions according to their configuration.
