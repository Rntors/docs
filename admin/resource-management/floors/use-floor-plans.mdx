Enable real-time visibility into resource availability and streamline bookings with interactive floor plans.

<Info>
  Floor plans come as an add-on and are available on specific plans. To order floor plans, follow [these steps](/admin/resource-management/floors/set-up-floor-plans).
</Info>

## How administrators use floor plans

Floor plans are available to administrators on the following pages:

* **Resources** > **Rooms**, **Desks**, **Offices, Assignments** pages (via the **Floors** tab)
* **Location settings > Resources** > **Floors** tab

### Resources

Floor plans on the **Rooms**, **Desks**, and **Offices** pages let administrators keep track of daily bookings and identify which resources are available for booking.

1. Go to the **Rooms**, **Desks**, and **Offices** page > **Floors** tab.
2. Select **Today** or the necessary future date.
3. Choose the needed floor from the dropdown.

* **Green fill** - available for booking at the moment/on the selected date.
* **Pink fill** - fully booked at the moment/on the selected date.
* **Purple fill** - fully assigned to a certain customer(s) on the selected date.
* **White fill** - unavailable for booking.

### Assignments

Floor plans under the **Resources** > **Assignments** page let administrators keep track of long-term rental assignments and identify which rooms, desks, and offices are available for rent now or on the selected future date.

1. Go to the **Resources** > **Assignments** page > **Floors** tab.
2. Select **Today** or the necessary date in the future.
3. Use the **Resource** dropdown to show only specific resources (only offices or only dedicated desks), or view all.
4. Choose the needed floor from the dropdown.

* **Green fill** - available for rental or partially assigned on a selected date.
* **Purple fill** - fully assigned to a certain customer(s) on a selected date.
* **White fill** - unavailable for assignments.

## How users use floor plans

Users can access floor plans on the **Rooms**, **Desks**, and **Offices** pages in the member web portal and mobile app.

### Rooms

To check available rooms via the floor plan and book, users do the following:

1. Go to **Locations** > **Rooms** page.
2. Choose the needed floor from the dropdown.
3. Tap on the room on the floor plan, which shows up as green > proceed with a room booking.

* **Green fill** — the room is available for booking at the moment.
* **Red fill** — the room is already booked at the moment.
* **White fill** — the room is fully assigned to other customer(s), or not available.

### Desks

To check available desks via the floor plan and book, users do the following:

1. Go to **Locations** > **Desks** page.
2. Select **Today** or the necessary date in the future.
3. Choose the needed floor from the dropdown.
4. Tap on the desk on the floor plan, which shows up as green > proceed with a desk booking.

* **Green fill** — the desk is available for booking on the selected day.
* **Red fill** — the desk is already fully occupied on the selected day.
* **White fill** — the desk is fully assigned to other customer(s), or not available.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="" width="1690" height="944" data-path="images/resource-management-floors-use-floor-plans-5.png" />
</Frame>

### Offices

To check available offices for rental via the floor plan, users do the following:

1. Go to **Locations** > **Offices** page.
2. Choose the needed floor from the dropdown.
3. Tap on the office on the floor plan, which shows up as green > proceed with an application.

* **Green fill** — the office is available for rental.
* **Red fill** — all seats in the office are assigned.
* **White fill** — the entire office is fully assigned to other customer(s), or not available.

<Info>

</Info>

**FAQs**

<Accordion title="Why do I need a floor plan?">
  Floor plans not only let users book resources directly from the floor plan, but also give administrators a clear view of space availability making it easier to monitor and optimize space usage.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is it secure to show all my resources on the floor plan?">
  Users can see the location and availability of resources, but not who is using them. Company or member assignments are not visible, ensuring privacy and security.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can other users see the floor plan on the Assignments page?">
  The **Assignments** page is only accessible to administrators in RNTOR. Users in member web portal or mobile app can view floor plans only on the **Rooms,** **Desks**, and **Offices** pages.
</Accordion>
