title: "Set up floor plans"
---

Floor plans show the live availability of rooms, desks, and offices in an interactive layout.

## Prerequisites

1. Confirm that Floor plans are enabled for your network or location.
2. Calculate the combined area of the floors.
3. Prepare a straight, readable plan for every floor.
4. Clearly mark each resource that should be individually bookable.

</Frame>

## Request floor plans

1. Go to **Location settings → Resources → Floors**.
2. Select **Submit details**.
3. Enter the number of floors and total area.
4. Upload each scheme as an image or PDF.
5. Submit the request.

<Frame>
</Frame>

You can represent desks as individual seats or as zones. Choose the approach that matches how customers book and how administrators track occupancy.

After the interactive SVG files are prepared, follow [Manage floor plans](/admin/resource-management/floors/manage-floor-plans) to upload and map them.
