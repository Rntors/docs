Floor plans provide an interactive visual layout of your space, showing real-time availability of rooms, desks, and offices with intuitive color indicators.

They make it easy for users to book resources directly from the plan while giving administrators a clear view of occupancy.

## Prerequisites

1. If you haven't yet, [sign up for the Floor plans add-on](/admin/administration/billing/make-changes-to-rntor-subscription) from **Network settings** > **Billing**.
2. Calculate the total area of your floors either in square meters or square feet.
3. Prepare a floor plan:
   * Ensure the floor plan is in a standard straight (vertical or horizontal) orientation and not at an angle.
   * Clearly label each resource that should be bookable separately, with its boundaries outlined.

## Get your floor plans created

1. Go to the **Location settings > Resources** page > **Floors** tab.
2. In the **Order floor plans** section, click **Submit details** and fill out the form:
   * **Total floors**: the number of floors to order plans for.
   * **Total area**: the combined area of your floors, in square meters or square feet.
   * **Files**: upload the scheme of each floor as an image or PDF file.

![Resource Management Floors 2](/images/resource-management-floors-2.png)

3. Based on the entered details, you will see the estimated price for your floor plan creation. If you're the network owner, you'll be asked to authorize this one-time setup fee right away. If a non-owner administrator submits the order, our support team will contact the owner separately to authorize the charge.
4. We begin preparing your floor plan(s). It takes up to 14 days per floor.
5. Once created, we provide you with a preview and apply any requested edits.
6. Once approved, [upload the floor plan](/admin/resource-management/floors/set-up-floor-plans) SVG file provided by the RNTOR team, map interactive resources, set its name and visibility.

<Info>
  For any modifications to your floor plan after the initial setup, [contact RNTOR team](/admin/administration/contact-support). Post-setup changes are [subject to a fee](/admin/resource-management/floors/set-up-floor-plans).
</Info>

<Info>
  We only provide support and modifications for floor plans that were originally created by the RNTOR designer.
</Info>

## Use floor plans

Once uploaded and mapped, administrators access floor plans:

* From **Resources** > **Rooms**, **Desks**, or **Offices** pages.
* And under **Resources** > **Assignments** for long-term rental insights.

Users view and interact with floor plans from the **Rooms**, **Desks**, or **Offices** pages.
