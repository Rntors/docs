Use permissions to give each administrator the responsibilities they need in the locations they manage. Some modules remain readable when editing is disabled; turning off a permission does not necessarily hide that module or its prices.

## Customize permissions

Set permissions when you [invite an administrator](/admin/administration/administrators/administrators#invite-an-administrator), or change them later:

1. Open **Settings** from the sidebar and select **Administrators**.

![Administrators selected in Rntor network settings](/images/rntor-administrators-navigation-en-20261024.jpg)

2. Open the administrator's **(...)** menu and select **Edit**.
3. Turn the required permissions on or off.
4. Select **Save**.

![Administrator access form with locations and grouped permission switches](/images/rntor-administrator-edit-en-20261024.jpg)

Owners and super administrators have all permissions in all locations. Granular permission switches apply to the **Administrator** role.

## Understand permissions

| Permission | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **Customers** | Invite or remove members, delete nonmembers, and manage companies and membership requests. Manage customer billing details, payment methods, **Auto-pay**, credit and day-pass balances, and notes, and resend invoices. Removing a member or company with a subscription also requires **Billing**. |
| **Billing** | Create and manage contracts, subscriptions, invoices, and credit notes; mark pending external payments as paid or canceled; edit transaction internal notes; and configure plans, packages, coupons, and promo codes. Without it, plans, subscriptions, and invoices remain readable, including prices. |
| **Resources** | Create, edit, and delete resources; create and manage bookings and their refunds; and create or manage [assignments](/admin/resource-management/resources/manage-resource-rentals-with-assignments). Without it, **Rooms**, **Desks**, **Offices**, **Parking**, and **Equipment**, including resource and booking prices, remain read-only. |
| **Feed** | Create, edit, pin, or delete posts and delete user comments. Without it, the feed remains read-only. |
| **Events** | Create and manage events, manage tickets, and refund tickets. Without it, events remain read-only. |
| **Shop** | Create and manage products and orders, including order refunds. Products and orders remain viewable without it. |
| **Benefits** | Add and manage benefits. Benefits and applications remain viewable without it. |
| **Support** | Open other users' tickets, create and manage tickets and guides, and receive ticket assignments. Without it, administrators can still view the ticket list and all guides, but cannot open tickets created by other users. |
| **Analytics** | Open **Analytics** and see assignments on the **Assignments** page. Both require this permission. |
| **Settings** | Open **Location settings** and **Integrations**. Both are unavailable without this permission. |

### Key considerations

- Each administrator has one permission set, shared across all locations they manage.
- Owners and super administrators always have every permission in every location. If an owner changes to **Administrator**, they retain all permissions and all-location access until you edit that access.
- [Notification preferences](/admin/administration/your-account/notifications/set-up-notifications) do not grant access. The corresponding permission is also required: invoice-payment notifications require **Customers**, and booking notifications require **Resources**.
- Any administrator of a location can [check users in](/admin/check-ins), regardless of their permission switches.
- Managing administrators is controlled by the [staff role](/admin/administration/administrators/administrators#understand-administrator-roles), rather than a separate **Administrators** permission.

## Best practices

Choose permissions according to the person's duties:

| Team responsibility | Suggested permissions |
| --- | --- |
| **Account manager** | All permissions to manage members, companies, subscriptions, and location content. |
| **Event manager** | **Feed** and **Events** to manage posts, events, and registrations. |
| **Accountant** | **Analytics** and **Billing** to use analytics and export payments and invoices from **Transactions** and **Invoices**. |

## FAQs

<Accordion title="Can an administrator view a module when its permission is off?">
  Usually, they retain read-only access. The exceptions are **Analytics**, **Location settings**, and **Integrations**, which need their matching permissions. Opening another user's support ticket requires **Support**. Seeing assignments requires **Analytics**; creating or managing them also requires **Resources**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I hide prices from an administrator?">
  No. Administrators can view plan, subscription, invoice, resource, and booking prices regardless of permission settings. Removing the related write permission restricts actions such as creating, editing, and refunding; it does not hide prices.
</Accordion>
