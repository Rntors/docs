## Who is an administrator

An administrator is a person on your coworking space's staff or team (e.g., a community manager, receptionist, or employee) who is responsible for managing a specific location. Each location can have an unlimited number of administrators.

Users with the **Administrator** role are identified by a shield icon next to their name on the **Customers** page:

![Image](/images/administration-roles-2.png)

To view all administrators across all your locations, go to **Network settings \> Administrators**.

![Administration Roles Who Is An Administrator See All Administrators](/images/administration-roles-who-is-an-administrator-see-all-administrators.png)

## Assign or remove administrator role

Administrators can assign new administrators or remove admin access for any user. For this:

1. Go to **Customers** \> **Users** tab \> select the user from the list.​
2. Press the **(...)** menu top right in their profile \> select **Edit**.
3. Toggle the **Administrator** switch:
   - Enable it to make the user an administrator and [customize administrator permissions.](/admin/administration/administrators/permissions)
   - Disable it to make an administrator a regular member.

![Administration Roles Assign Or Remove Administrator Role](/images/administration-roles-assign-or-remove-administrator-role.png)

<Info>
  If the user is not yet added to RNTOR, [invite them](/admin/customer-management/users/members#invite-a-member) first and toggle the **Administrator** switch.
</Info>

## What an administrator can do

According to the [enabled permissions](/admin/administration/administrators/permissions), administrators can:

- Manage general location settings.
- Add any location content.
- Manage **Customers** page: invite users, create companies, subscriptions, etc.
- Fully access users' contact and membership information.
- Book rooms and other resources for free using the **Free** payment method.
- [View analytics](/admin/analytics-reports/dashboards) and download custom reports.
- [Receive notifications](/admin/administration/your-account/notifications/set-up-notifications) about all users' activities.
- [Invite](/admin/customer-management/users/members#invite-a-member) or assign new administrators, [manage administrators' permissions](/admin/administration/administrators/permissions), or remove [administrators](/admin/customer-management/users/remove-users).

<Info>
  Administrators become active and count towards your subscription only if they [meet the criteria](/admin/administration/billing/active-users) by interacting with RNTOR.
</Info>
