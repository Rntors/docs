## Get instant help

If you have questions about features or need help with setup, tap the **Help** button in the bottom left corner of your admin portal:

- **Ask Noor AI**  — Noor AI knows all of RNTOR's functionality and has access to your specific data, so it can give you a personalized answer or assistance instantly.
- **Docs** — find comprehensive RNTOR documentation about a specific feature or setup.

![Administration Contact Support Get Instant Help](/images/administration-contact-support-get-instant-help.png)

## Contact us

To contact us:

1. Tap the **Help** button in the bottom left corner \> **Support**.
2. If the AI response isn't helpful and you choose to submit your question to our support team, you will receive a response from us via email.

Alternatively, contact us directly at [support@rntor.com](mailto:support@rntor.com) at any time.

<Info>
  In addition to our standard support channel, a dedicated support session is available for purchase.
</Info>

### Office hours

Our regular office hours are _Monday through Friday, 2 a.m. to 5 p.m. Eastern Time (ET)_. Our regular response time is _3 minutes_.

## Check status page

To check the status of our servers:

1. Tap the **Help** button in the bottom left corner \> **RNTOR status**.
2. Press **Subscribe to updates** to subscribe to any news via email, Slack, or Microsoft Teams.

## Share feedback

If you have feature requests, insights to share about the platform, or have faced any challenges tap the **Help** button in the bottom left corner \> **Share feedback**. This is the fastest way to submit feature requests — our team reviews every submission.

## Report a bug

To report an issue or bug, tap the **Help** button in the bottom left corner \> **Report bug**. We will send you updates about the bug resolution to your email.

## Track release notes

To stay informed about all new features, solutions and product improvements implemented in RNTOR, monitor **Release notes** in the bottom left corner of the admin web portal.
