Receive real-time notifications about new bookings, sign-ups, invoices payments, and other users' actions and location updates that may require your attention. Choose how you prefer to get them, and keep track of everything that needs your attention in one place.

## Configure notification preferences

Admin notifications fall into two main categories:

- Activities related to other users' accounts
- Integration errors and disconnections

Administrators can enable and disable notifications they prefer to receive as follows:

1. Open your profile menu in the top bar and select **My Profile**.

![Image](/images/account-menu-en-20261014.jpg)

2. Go to **Notifications**.

![Image](/images/account-language-en-20261014.jpg)

3. Toggle the switches for the notifications you want to receive:

| **Notification category** | **Notifications included** |
| :-- | :-- |
| Customers | Location join requests and company invitations or join requests requiring attention; company members removed; users reported; accounts deleted; invitation delivery failures. |
| Contracts | Contracts signed or viewed, and reminders one month before a contract ends. |
| Subscriptions | <ul><li>New purchases</li></ul> |
| Invoices | <ul><li>Payments succeeded</li><li>Payments failed</li></ul> |
| Credit packages | <ul><li>New purchases</li></ul> |
| Resources | <ul><li>New bookings created by the selected users</li><li>Bookings canceled</li></ul> |
| Visitors | New contacts left on Reception and visitor arrivals recorded at Reception or by an administrator. |
| Check-ins | Member arrivals and departures recorded at Reception or by an administrator, plus automatic departures at midnight (including visitors). Visitor arrivals are controlled under **Visitors**. [Learn about Check-ins](/admin/check-ins). |
| Feed | <ul><li>New posts and comments under the post you created or commented on</li></ul> |
| Events | <ul><li>New attendees</li><li>Tickets canceled</li></ul> |
| Shop | <ul><li>New orders</li><li>Orders canceled</li></ul> |
| Benefits | <ul><li>New applications submitted</li></ul> |
| Support | <ul><li>New tickets</li><li>Reminders about new tickets that await action</li><li>Updates on tickets assigned to you or commented by you</li></ul> |
| Integrations | <ul><li>Failures of the integration connections</li></ul> |
| Chats | Messages and group updates: joining a chat, member additions or removals, changes to the group name or image, and changes to your role. |

For bookings, administrators can also choose whose bookings trigger notifications:

- Administrators
- Location members
- Network members
- Nonmembers

<Info>
  Nonmembers' and invoice activity notifications are enabled by default.
</Info>

<Info>
  Administrators receive notifications only about user activities they can manage according to their [permissions](/admin/administration/administrators/permissions).
</Info>

## Track notifications

Use three channels to follow the activities you enable:

- **Inbox:** view and manage notifications in the admin portal.
- **Push notifications:** enable the channel and allow notifications in your browser.
- **Email:** optionally receive the same enabled notifications at your account email address.

### Push notifications

In **My Profile > Notifications**, scroll to **Channels** and enable **Push notifications**. Allow Rntor notifications when your browser asks. If permission was previously blocked, update the browser's site permissions before trying again.

#### Desktop (admin web portal)

Administrators must grant notification permissions for RNTOR in their browser settings. Follow the guidelines for your browser:

- [Instructions for Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/3220216?hl=en)
- [Instructions for Safari browser](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/change-notification-settings-iph7c3d96bab/17.0/ios/17.0#:~:text=Receive%20Web%20Push%20notifications%20from%20web%20apps)

![Push and email channels in Rntor notification settings](/images/account-channels-en-20261014.jpg)

<Tip>
  Keep your browser updated to the latest version for a seamless push notification experience. You can also add RNTOR to your home screen as a progressive web app for quick access and enable push notifications directly from there.
</Tip>

### Email notifications

In addition to push notifications, you can choose to receive all enabled notifications by email. Go to the **My Profile \> Notifications** page and enable the **Emails** switch at the bottom.

![Email channel at the bottom of notification settings](/images/account-channels-en-20261014.jpg)

<Info>
  If an administrator logs out or disables push notifications in their browser or device, RNTOR cannot deliver push notifications to them. If email notifications are enabled, they will receive notifications by email instead.
</Info>

### Inbox in your admin portal

Enabled admin notifications appear in RNTOR on the **Inbox** page — your main page to stay up-to-date with everything happening in your admin and member portals.

<Info>
  The **Inbox** was previously called **Notifications**. It's the same feature under a new name and interface. All the same notifications still appear here and work exactly as before.
</Info>

![Account Notifications Track Notifications In Your Inbox](/images/account-inbox-en-20261014.jpg)

- Each notification shows the name of the user or company, a description of their action, and the RNTOR service they interacted with (a resource, a plan, a product, and so on).
- Click a notification to preview its item alongside the list; double-click to open the item directly.
- A dot marks unread items. Use **Unread** to show them, and the category menu to narrow the list.
- Select **Mark all as read** to mark your notifications as read.
- Use **Select** to mark or dismiss selected items. Dismissing a notification removes it from your Inbox, without deleting the related item.

<Info>
  Administrators receive notifications only about other users' bookings and actions. You won't receive notifications about your own actions.
</Info>

## FAQs

<Accordion title="I am not getting admin notifications about certain user activity. What should I do?">
  1. Check your notification settings:
     - Open your profile menu in the top bar and select **My Profile**.
     - Go to **My Profile \> Notifications**.
     - Ensure that notifications for the specific user activity and user group you're interested in are enabled.
  2. If you're not receiving push notifications, check in your device or browser settings that you've allowed receiving notifications from RNTOR:
     - In the admin web portal, enable **Push notifications** and [allow push notifications via your browser](/admin/administration/your-account/notifications/set-up-notifications#push-notifications).
  3. If you are not receiving email notifications, go to the **My Profile \> Notifications** page and enable the **Emails** switch at the bottom.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How to set sending notifications about specific user activity to a certain email address?">
  There are two ways to set sending notifications to a certain email address.

  If the employee whose email address you want to notify is an administrator of your space:

  1. [Invite them as an administrator](/admin/administration/administrators/administrators#assign-or-remove-administrator-role) to RNTOR.
  2. [Set their administrator permissions](/admin/administration/administrators/permissions), as needed, if your RNTOR plan allows for this feature.
  3. Ask them to log in to RNTOR, navigate to the [**My Profile > Notifications**](/admin/administration/your-account/notifications/set-up-notifications) page, enable the desired notifications, and then toggle on the **Emails** switch at the bottom of the page.

  If you prefer not to invite the email address as an administrator to RNTOR, you can set up an automation using Zapier:

  1. Create a [Zapier](https://zapier.com/app/assets/zaps) automation designed to send emails.
  2. In the **Trigger** step, select RNTOR and choose the specific user activity event you wish to send notifications about.
  3. In the **Action** step, select your preferred email application (e.g., Gmail, Outlook) and enter the dedicated email address where you want to receive notifications.

  Once set up, this Zapier automation will automatically send an email notification to the specified address every time the chosen user activity occurs in RNTOR.
</Accordion>
