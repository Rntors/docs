Enable **Integrations > Failures** in your [notification preferences](/admin/administration/your-account/notifications/set-up-notifications) to follow connection and synchronization problems. Open **Inbox** and choose **Integrations** to find the alert and the affected item.

![QuickBooks synchronization alerts in the Rntor Inbox](/images/account-integration-failure-en-20261014.jpg)

This page covers **Google Calendar** and **QuickBooks Online**.

| Integration | Alert or problem | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Calendar | A booking conflicts with an existing reservation | Compare the event's time with the existing booking in Rntor, then resolve the overlap before synchronizing again. |
| QuickBooks Online | An invoice is rejected because its sales tax does not match | Check the invoice's tax rate and the active sales-tax rates in QuickBooks. Configure the matching rate under **Taxes** in QuickBooks, or correct the [tax settings in Rntor](/admin/billing/taxes/set-up-taxes) if they are wrong. Retry the synchronization after correcting the configuration. |
| QuickBooks Online | The trial or subscription has ended, or billing prevents new transactions | Check **Subscriptions and billing** in QuickBooks and resolve the account's billing status before retrying. |
| QuickBooks Online | The connection needs authorization again | Reconnect the relevant QuickBooks account in Rntor. |
| QuickBooks Online | A required product or service is missing or inactive | Review the product/service selected in the QuickBooks integration and select an active item. |

<Info>
  Failure alerts concern the administrators of the affected location. Keep **Failures** enabled in your notification preferences and check that your permissions cover that location.
</Info>

## Resolve issues with integration connections

Read the alert in **Inbox** and check the related item before changing the integration. An alert helps identify a problem; it does not mean the failed synchronization has been completed.

### Expired connection

Disconnect the expired connection in Rntor, then reconnect with the correct account and current credentials.

### Account configuration check

Review the provider's account settings and the requirements for [Google Calendar](/admin/integrations/bookings/google-calendar) or [QuickBooks Online](/admin/integrations/accounting/quickbooks-online). For an invoice, also check its synchronization status and the error shown alongside it.

### Contact third-party support

If the provider continues to reject the connection or transaction after you have corrected the settings, contact its support team with the error details. Rntor cannot correct account restrictions or service failures in another product.
