description: "Learn how to change the language of your own RNTOR admin portal interface independently from the location's default language"
---

Choose English or Arabic for your own Rntor admin interface. Your choice does not change the language settings for a location or another administrator.

1. Open your profile menu in the top bar and select **My Profile**.
2. Select **Language**.
3. Choose **English** or **العربية**. The change is saved when you select the language; close the language window to return to your account.
