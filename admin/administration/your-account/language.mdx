---
title: "Language"
---

Administrators can select the language for their own RNTOR account. This setting determines the language of the admin panel interface for you.

<Info>
  By default, RNTOR will be displayed in the administrator’s browser preferred language before/unless the administrator sets a different account language manually.
</Info>

To change their account language, administrators do the following:

1. Tap your network name top right on the left-side panel.
2. Go to the **Account \>** **Language** page.
3. Select the language and press `Save`.
