Welcome to RNTOR, the leading shared space management software. We deeply value our amazing RNTOR community and anticipate helping more shared spaces automate tedious tasks, increase efficiency, and boost member retention.

Your support means a lot to us, and with our referral program, we want to reward you for spreading the word about RNTOR and helping us expand our community.

## How the partner referral program works

1. **Join the program**. [Fill out the form](https://rntor.com/referral-program) to become a referral partner, and let us know how you plan on promoting RNTOR. If it’s a good match, we’ll send you an email confirmation.
2. **Spread the word about RNTOR.** Share the benefits of using RNTOR with other shared spaces. Their objective is to [schedule a demo](https://rntor.com/demo) and mention that it was referred by you.
3. **Earn commissions.** When your referrals sign up, you'll receive a 20% commission on their total payment to RNTOR for 12 months. This includes their subscription plan as well as any add-ons, overages, and upgrades.
4. **Get paid.** When your referral balance hits €100 or more, simply submit an invoice to us, and we will process your payment via bank transfer. Be sure to include all necessary details outlined in our Referral Agreement in the invoice. If you need assistance with the invoicing process, please [contact us](https://rntor.com/contact).

## Who can join the partner referral program

Partner referral program is perfect for our customers as well as others looking to promote our software for shared space management.

1. Coworking & flex spaces.
2. Technical solution providers & integrators.
3. Consulting agencies.

By promoting RNTOR, you’re playing a key role in bringing advanced tech solutions to shared spaces, helping more businesses thrive.
