You can make the following changes to your subscription with RNTOR:

## Upgrade subscription

You may want to upgrade your RNTOR subscription with add-ons to expand your capabilities.

The [owner](/admin/administration/administrators/owner) can add add-ons or upgrade to yearly billing by following these steps:

1. Click the network name in the top left corner of the left-side panel \> go to **Network settings** \> **Billing**.
2. Click **Upgrade** in the **Plan** section. <img src="/images/administration-billing-make-changes-to-rntor-subscription-upgrade-subscription.png" alt="Administration Billing Make Changes To RNTOR Subscription Upgrade Subscription" />
3. On **Your plan** page, choose your preferred add-ons and billing cycle (monthly or annual).
4. Review and agree to RNTOR's terms of use, privacy policy, and data processing addendum.
5. Click **Continue to payment**.

![Image](/images/administration-billing-make-changes-to-rntor-subscription-2.png)

6. Enter your contact and payment details.
   - If you have a promo code, click **Add promotion code** and apply it.
7. Press **Subscribe with obligation to pay**.

![Image](/images/administration-billing-make-changes-to-rntor-subscription-3.png)

Your account updates with new functionality immediately after the payment.

### Important notes

- If you're on RNTOR's legacy Starter, Essential, or Pro plan, you will be automatically switched to the new Business plan during the upgrade. Review its pricing and conditions on the upgrade page.
- Before ordering the Member Mobile App add-on, make sure to have an [Apple Developer account for your organization](/admin/general/member-mobile-app/prepare-your-apple-developer-account) ready. We will publish your member mobile apps with the upcoming mobile release.
- Upgrading to the Member mobile app add-on starts a new 6-month commitment term for your RNTOR subscription from the upgrade date.
- If you upgrade mid-cycle, you only pay the prorated amount for the remaining days of the current month. The system automatically calculates the amount you've already paid for this month and charges you only the difference for the remaining days.

## Update your payment or billing information

You can easily manage your payment and invoicing information for subscription to RNTOR. For example, change a credit card for payments, update the email for receiving bills, or add a VAT number.

The [owner](/admin/administration/administrators/owner) follows these steps:

1. Click the network name in the top left corner **\>** **Network settings \> Billing** page.
2. In the **Billing details** section, click **Edit**.

![Administration Billing Make Changes To RNTOR Subscription Update Your Payment Or Billing Information](/images/administration-billing-make-changes-to-rntor-subscription-update-your-payment-or-billing-information.png)

3. Proceed with the necessary updates
   - Update your default payment method
   - Change billing address
   - Update the email address to receive invoices and notifications
   - Update your Tax ID, which appears on invoices.

## View and pay invoices from RNTOR

The [owner](/admin/administration/administrators/owner) follows these steps:

1. Click the network name in the top left corner **\>** **Network settings \> Billing** page.
2. Find the **Invoices** section.
3. Locate the necessary invoice. <img src="/images/administration-billing-make-changes-to-rntor-subscription-view-and-pay-invoices-from-rntor.png" alt="Administration Billing Make Changes To RNTOR Subscription View And Pay Invoices From RNTOR" />

- Click the invoice title to pay it.
- Click **(...)** \> **Download PDF** to download it.

<Info>
  If the payment attempt for your RNTOR subscription fails, the system will automatically retry the payment up to 4 times within 1 week.
</Info>

## Switch from monthly to yearly billing

The [owner](/admin/administration/administrators/owner) needs to follow these steps:

1. Click the network name in the top left corner of the left-side panel.
2. Go to **Network settings** \> **Billing**.
3. Click **Upgrade** in the **Plan** section.
4. On **Your plan** page, select the **Yearly** billing option.
5. Select the desired add-ons if necessary and [continue to payment.](/admin/administration/billing/make-changes-to-rntor-subscription)

## Cancel or downgrade your subscription

Canceling your RNTOR subscription involves a few steps to ensure a smooth transition and proper handling of your account and data:

1. Ensure that all your past-due invoices with RNTOR are paid.
2. Contact us at [support@rntor.com](mailto:support@rntor.com) as an [owner](/admin/administration/administrators/owner) to cancel or downgrade your RNTOR subscription. Confirm the cancellation date.
3. Export necessary data (customers' contacts, invoices, etc.) using [custom reports](/admin/analytics-reports/reports) to avoid losing important information.
4. Plan for service termination by notifying team members and migrating any dependent workflows.

### Notes

Make sure your request complies with the RNTOR Terms of Use you agreed to: [https://rntor.com/terms](https://rntor.com/terms). Key terms for cancellation include:

- You can cancel your subscription only after your Minimum Subscription Term has ended.
- You must provide us with a written notice of non-renewal at least 3 days before your current Subscription Period ends.
- Your RNTOR account will remain active until the confirmed cancellation date.
- After this point, access to the admin portal, member web portal and mobile app, and any associated data will be permanently terminated.

**FAQs**

<Accordion title="Is data associated with my RNTOR account deleted after my trial ends, or I terminate my subscription?">
  As part of RNTOR's commitment to data protection, privacy, and information security, all data associated with your account is scheduled for permanent deletion 60 days after your trial expires or your subscription is terminated. You will receive email reminders 60 days and 30 days before the permanent deletion occurs to ensure you have time to reactivate your plan or export necessary reports.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where can I find a link to payment for RNTOR subscription?">
  To complete the payment for your unpaid invoice for the RNTOR subscription, the [owner](/admin/administration/administrators/owner) needs to follow these steps:

  1. Click the network name in the top left corner **\>** **Network settings \> Billing** page.
  2. Find the **Invoices** section.
  3. Locate the unpaid invoice and click its title to make the payment.

  The invoice covers plan fees for the upcoming month and active user charges for the period stated under "Period."

  <Info>
    The **Download** button and specific billing periods are unavailable for initial invoices or those generated via a plan upgrade.
  </Info>
</Accordion>
