You can make the following changes to your subscription with RNTOR:

## Upgrade subscription

The [owner](/admin/administration/administrators/administrators) can add features or switch to annual billing.

1. Open **Settings** > **Billing** for your network.
2. Select **Upgrade** in the **Plan** section.

![Plan and Upgrade button](/images/administration-billing-make-changes-to-rntor-subscription-upgrade-subscription.png)

3. On **Your plan**, select your add-ons and billing cycle.
4. Accept the terms, privacy policy, and data processing addendum if prompted.
5. Review the amount and select **Pay**.

![Subscription upgrade options](/images/administration-billing-make-changes-to-rntor-subscription-2.png)

![Subscription payment](/images/administration-billing-make-changes-to-rntor-subscription-3.png)

The prorated charge uses your [saved payment method](#change-your-payment-method). The new features become available after payment.

### Important notes

- If you're on RNTOR's legacy Starter, Essential, or Pro plan, you will be automatically switched to the new Business plan during the upgrade. Review its pricing and conditions on the upgrade page.
- Before ordering the Member Mobile App add-on, make sure to have an [Apple Developer account for your organization](/admin/general/member-mobile-app/prepare-your-apple-developer-account) ready. We will publish your member mobile apps with the upcoming mobile release.
- Upgrading to the Member mobile app add-on starts a new 6-month commitment term for your RNTOR subscription from the upgrade date.
- If you upgrade mid-cycle, you only pay the prorated amount for the remaining days of the current month. The system automatically calculates the amount you've already paid for this month and charges you only the difference for the remaining days.
- Visitors, API & webhooks, Floor plans, and Member mobile app apply to all network locations. Their fees depend on the number of locations.

## Update your billing details

Your business name, billing email, phone, address, and tax ID appear on your RNTOR invoices.

1. As an [owner](/admin/administration/administrators/administrators), open **Settings** > **Billing**.
2. Select **Edit** under **Billing details**.
3. Update the information and select **Save**.

![Billing details](/images/administration-billing-make-changes-to-rntor-subscription-update-your-payment-or-billing-information.png)

## Change your payment method

1. Open **Settings** > **Billing** as an owner.
2. Under **Payment method**, select **Change**, or **Add payment method** if none is saved.
3. Enter the new details and select **Save**.

If a bank account requires micro-deposit verification, verify the amounts sent by Stripe before it becomes the subscription's payment method.

## View and pay invoices from RNTOR

The [owner](/admin/administration/administrators/administrators) follows these steps:

1. Open **Settings** > **Billing** for your network.
2. Find the **Invoices** section.
3. Locate the necessary invoice. <img src="/images/administration-billing-make-changes-to-rntor-subscription-view-and-pay-invoices-from-rntor.png" alt="Administration Billing Make Changes To RNTOR Subscription View And Pay Invoices From RNTOR" />

- Click the invoice title to pay it.
- Click **(...)** \> **Download PDF** to download it.

## Pay an outstanding invoice

The owner has 7 days to settle a failed subscription payment before suspension.

1. Open **Settings** > **Billing**. If access is already suspended, select **Renew** on the suspension page.
2. If necessary, use **Update payment method** in the **Plan** section to replace the failed method.
3. Open the unpaid invoice and complete payment.

Access is restored after successful payment. **Update payment information** in the failed-payment email opens the payment update page.

<Info>
  Failed subscription payments are retried automatically up to 4 times within one week.
</Info>

## Switch from monthly to yearly billing

The [owner](/admin/administration/administrators/administrators) needs to follow these steps:

1. Open **Settings** from the sidebar.
2. Select **Billing** for your network.
3. Click **Upgrade** in the **Plan** section.
4. On **Your plan** page, select the **Yearly** billing option.
5. Select any add-ons you need and select **Pay**.

For yearly-to-monthly billing, [request a downgrade](#cancel-or-downgrade-your-subscription).

## Cancel or downgrade your subscription

Contact our team to cancel, remove an add-on, or switch from yearly to monthly billing:

1. Ensure that all your past-due invoices with RNTOR are paid.
2. Contact us at [support@rntor.com](mailto:support@rntor.com) as an [owner](/admin/administration/administrators/administrators) to cancel or downgrade your RNTOR subscription. Confirm the cancellation date.
3. Export necessary data (customers' contacts, invoices, etc.) using [custom reports](/admin/analytics-reports/reports) to avoid losing important information.
4. Plan for service termination by notifying team members and migrating any dependent workflows.

### Cancellation terms

Make sure your request complies with the RNTOR Terms of Use you agreed to: [RNTOR Terms of Use](https://rntor.com/en/terms). Key terms for cancellation include:

- You can cancel your subscription only after your Minimum Subscription Term has ended.
- You must provide us with a written notice of non-renewal at least 3 days before your current Subscription Period ends.
- Your RNTOR account will remain active until the confirmed cancellation date.
- After this point, access to the admin portal, member web portal and mobile app, and any associated data will be permanently terminated.
- Any remaining network balance is forfeited.

## FAQs

<Accordion title="Is data associated with my RNTOR account deleted after my trial ends, or I terminate my subscription?">
  As part of RNTOR's commitment to data protection, privacy, and information security, all data associated with your account is scheduled for permanent deletion 60 days after your trial expires or your subscription is terminated. You will receive email reminders 60 days and 30 days before the permanent deletion occurs to ensure you have time to reactivate your plan or export necessary reports.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where can I find a link to payment for RNTOR subscription?">
  To complete the payment for your unpaid invoice for the RNTOR subscription, the [owner](/admin/administration/administrators/administrators) needs to follow these steps:

  1. Open **Settings** > **Billing** for your network.
  2. Find the **Invoices** section.
  3. Locate the unpaid invoice and click its title to make the payment.

  The invoice covers plan fees for the upcoming month and active user charges for the period stated under "Period."

  <Info>
    The first invoice and invoices generated by an upgrade have no billing period or **Download active users** option.
  </Info>
</Accordion>
