If you are running multiple locations, you can add them to RNTOR. Each location has a separate community, currency, language, settings, and administrators.

## Add a location

The [owner](/admin/administration/administrators/administrators) needs to follow these steps:

1. Open **Settings** > **Locations** for your network.
2. Press **Add location**, and enter the details:
   - Name
   - Country
   - Timezone
   - Language — the default [location language](/admin/general/language) used for invoice and receipt PDFs, visit invitations, and in a few more places.
   - Currency — a currency used for all prices in the location.

![Image](/images/administration-locations-1.png)

3. Press **Create**.

<Info>
  A new location increases the subscription fee and any add-on fees charged per location. Review [pricing](https://rntor.com/en/pricing).
</Info>

## Edit a location

To rename a location, modify your location's language or timezone, administrators follow these steps:

1. Select the location, then open **Location settings** > **General**.
2. Make your changes and leave the page. The changes will apply immediately.

![Locations Edit A Location](/images/administration-locations-5.png)

<Warning>
  The location currency cannot be changed after the location has been created.
</Warning>

## Delete a location

The [owner](/admin/administration/administrators/administrators) needs to follow these steps:

1. Open **Settings** > **Locations** for your network.

![Image](/images/administration-locations-3.png)

2. Press **(...)** \> **Delete** next to the location you'd like to delete, and confirm your choice.

![Image](/images/administration-locations-4.png)

<Warning>
  Deleting a location is permanent and irreversible. Even our engineers can't recover your data.
</Warning>

The current billing period is not refunded. The location and its per-location add-ons stop being charged from the next renewal.

## Multi-location management tips

Help members discover, book, and pay for services across your network using credits and day passes.

### Configure visibility

[Set visibility](/admin/location/visibility) for all your resources and services in all locations to **Network members** or **Public**. This way, members can see all your locations and services in them and book and purchase services across your entire network.

**Public** visibility helps not only your existing members to discover other locations but also first-time customers.​

![Locations Multi Location Management Tips Configure Visibility](/images/administration-locations-5.png)

### Enable cross-location credits and day passes

By [enabling cross-location credits and day passes](/admin/billing/credits-and-day-passes/cross-location-credits-and-day-passes), you allow members to use credits and day passes received in one location in other network locations.

Members can spend their personal or company credits and day passes across locations, giving them more flexibility and a consistent experience wherever they go.

![Locations Multi Location Management Tips Cross Location Credits And Day Passes](/images/administration-locations-5.png)

## FAQs

<Accordion title="How are locations ordered for customers?">
  On the [landing page](/admin/general/member-web-portal/customize-landing-page), locations appear alphabetically.

  In the member portal and mobile app's **Locations** list, locations are grouped in this order:

  1. Locations where the customer is a member.
  2. Locations with a pending request to join.
  3. Other locations.

  Each group is sorted by distance when geolocation is allowed, or alphabetically otherwise.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Which location opens when customers open the member portal?">
  The portal and mobile app return to the **Home** page of the location opened last. Customers can switch from **Locations**.

  On a first visit, customers see **Locations**, or **Home** when the network has one location. After signing out of the mobile app, it opens **Locations** again.
</Accordion>
