# Information

> Configure the Information page with your location's general details and cover photos or videos

The **Information** page for each location helps your customers recognize what location and application they're using, get your address, Wi-Fi password, schedule, answers to common questions, and other general data. ​ Your **Information** page shows users the following data:

- Your location's name and description
- Photos and videos of the location
- Schedule, address, and contact details
- [Location guides](/admin/support/guides)
- Wi-Fi details.

![Information Page](/images/location-information-1.png)

## Manage Information page

1. Click the menu **(...)** next to the location name on the left-side panel.
2. Go to **Location settings** \> **General.**
3. Upload location photos or videos.
4. Fill out description, address, contact and Wi-Fi details.
5. [Configure the location schedule](/admin/location/schedule).

Once done, leave the page. The changes will save automatically.

<Info>
  The visibility of the **Information** page is defined by the highest [visibility settings](/admin/location/visibility) of other pages. For example, if **Rooms** has **Public** visibility, the **Information** page will be automatically visible to the public, too.
</Info>

<Tip>
  To enhance the security of your Wi-Fi access, take advantage of our [integration with IronWiFi](/admin/integrations/wifi/ironwifi). This feature automates the Wi-Fi access granting and revoking process, ensuring that only those with a membership, or those who have booked a room or desk, can access the Wi-Fi.
</Tip>

### Location media

The location photos and videos you upload appear to users in the following places:

1. [**Information**](/admin/location/information) page.

Location cover photos or videos appear on the ⓘ **Information** page of your location, where users can learn more about your space, find contact details, etc.

2. [Landing](/admin/general/member-web-portal/customize-landing-page) page.

- All location cover media added across all locations are included in the slideshow on the landing page of your member web portal.
- The first cover image or video will appear under the specific location in the **Our locations** section.

3. [Reception app](/admin/service/visitors/set-up-visitors)

If you have the [Visitors](/admin/service/visitors/set-up-visitors) add-ons, the last uploaded cover photo will be automatically shown as a background on the Reception app.

<Info>
  The media are updated across your apps immediately.
</Info>

### Location address

The location address determines the order of locations on the main screen when there are multiple locations. If a user is a member of a particular location, that location will be displayed first, followed by others arranged based on the user's geolocation, from nearest to furthest. [Understand sorting of the app content](/admin/general/content-sorting)
