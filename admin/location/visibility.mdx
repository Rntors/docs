The **Visibility** property lets you control who can see the content and pages.

## Visibility levels

There are four visibility levels:

| **Administrators** | Only the administrators of the selected location. |
| --- | --- |
| **Location members** | Members see the object in their location of membership. |
| **Network members** | Any member of any location within your network can view the information. |
| **Public** | Any user who opened the member web portal and mobile app. |
| **Members with exclusive access** | Members who are subscribed to a plan that explicitly includes these resources (via the [**Exclusive access** setting](/admin/billing/plans/set-up-plans)). This visibility setting is available for resources only. |

## How to set up visibility

To configure the page visibility, follow these steps:

1. Go to the RNTOR \> tap the **(...)** button next to the location name \> **Locations** **settings** page.
2. Choose a desired module \> locate the **Visibility** dropdown \> select the desired value. The visibility settings for certain pages are limited to only a few options.

![Image](/images/location-visibility-1.png)

The following pages have visibility controls:

- **Community**
- **Feed**
- **Visitors**
- **Support**

<Info>
  The visibility of pages such as **Rooms**, **Desks**, **Offices**, **Parking**, **Equipment**, **Events**, **Shop,** and **Benefits** depends on the visibility settings of resources added to these pages.
</Info>

![Image](/images/location-visibility-2.png)

<Info>
  Sharing your app content through the **Share** button on **Rooms**, **Feed**, **Events**, **Benefits**, and **Shop** does not change the visibility property of the object.
</Info>

**FAQ**

<Accordion title="What is the difference between network members and location members?">
  Network members exist when you have multiple locations—they may belong to one location but not another. Location members belong to a specific location. They can access member-only areas in the member web portal and mobile app, have subscriptions, and can view content marked for **Location members** and above.

  <Info>
    Location members and network members can be found on the **Customers**page in the app.
  </Info>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why can't I see some resources in the member web portal or mobile app?">
  If a resource isn't visible on its dedicated page (for example, you don't see your room on the **Rooms** page), it may be [assigned](/admin/resource-management/resources/manage-resource-rentals-with-assignments) to a member or company. Navigate to RNTOR \> **Resources** \> open the necessary module and locate the resource you are looking for to see the assignment. If the resource should be available to all, [remove the assignment](/admin/resource-management/resources/manage-resource-rentals-with-assignments).
</Accordion>
