The Member web portal is a branded web application that enables your customers to book resources, make purchases, access all services, get in touch with your team, and more from a browser of their choice.

It is a convenient, all-in-one platform that provides your customers with self-sufficiency and superior service without the need for manual administrative intervention.

## How to get member web portal

Every RNTOR plan includes access to the member web portal at no additional cost. Simply [sign up for RNTOR](/admin/administration/billing/rntor-subscription#sign-up-for-rntor) to activate your own branded member web portal.

## How to access member web portal

### As an administrator

To access the member web portal, click **Member web portal** at the bottom of the left-side panel in admin web portal. You will be redirected to the portal that your customers will see and interact with.

![Image](/images/member-web-portal-1.png)

<Tip>
  Utilize the command palette via Ctrl \+ K or ⌘ \+ K for instant navigation to the member web portal in a new browser tab.
</Tip>

Once you open the member web portal, you can experience it from the member's perspective, view and interact with it exactly as a member would.

<Info>
  Admin features are accessible only through the admin web portal.
</Info>

<Tip>
  You can copy and save the link to the member web portal right from the browser tab for sharing with your customers and promoting via your marketing channels.
</Tip>

### As a customer

Your customers can get access to the member web portal in several ways:

1. **Via invitation**: [invite a user](/admin/customer-management/users/members#invite-a-member) to the location, and they will receive an email invitation with a link to access the member web portal.
2. **Via marketing channels**: Share the member web portal link through your website, social media, advertisements, and other marketing efforts. Check out RNTOR’s tips on [promoting member web portal and mobile app.](/guides/promotion/promote-member-web-portal-and-mobile-app)
3. **Direct sharing**: Provide the link directly to new customers who want to book a resource or access your services, or [generate QR code](/guides/promotion/use-qr-codes) to place in your space.

![Image](/images/member-web-portal-2.png)

<Info>
  Learn how various users [navigate member web portal](/member/onboarding).
</Info>

## How member web portal works

- **Streamlined customer service:** The portal offers everything your customers need in one place - purchase memberships, book resources, and participate in your community anytime.

![Image](/images/member-web-portal-3.png)

- **Accessible across all devices:** Users can access the member web portal from both desktop and mobile browsers. The web app delivers a smooth, app-like experience - no downloads needed.
- **Branded and customizable:** The member web portal is fully white-labeled, allowing you to reflect your brand’s identity with custom colors, logos, and a personalized landing page that highlights your brand.
- **Multi-language friendly:** offer a seamless experience with a [multilingual interface](/admin/general/language), making it more inclusive.

## Troubleshoot issues with access to member web portal

If you're experiencing issues accessing the member web portal, follow these steps to identify and troubleshoot the problem:

<Accordion title="1. Check your Internet connection">
  You may be experiencing issues accessing the member mobile app due to a poor internet connection. To resolve this, we recommend reconnecting to Wi-Fi on your device.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="2. Custom third-level domain is not working">
  If you've recently set up a custom domain for your member web portal and are now unable to access it, follow these troubleshooting steps:

  - **Confirm availability of custom domain feature**: Ensure that your [current plan with RNTOR](https://rntor.com/pricing#comparison-table) includes the option to set up a custom domain.
  - **Double-check domain setup accuracy:** Verify that the custom domain setup has been completed correctly according to the [Manage custom domain](/admin/general/member-web-portal/custom-domain-for-your-member-web-portal) article.
  - **Troubleshoot custom domain configuration**: If the issue persists after checking your plan and verifying the setup, refer to [this article](/admin/general/member-web-portal/custom-domain-for-your-member-web-portal#troubleshoot-issues-with-custom-domain) for further troubleshooting steps.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="3. Wrong URL used">
  Another common reason for being unable to access the member web portal is using the wrong URL. It's easy to mistype or select the incorrect link, which can lead to a "page not found" error or other issues.

  To resolve this, double-check the URL you're using to [access the member web portal](/admin/general/member-web-portal/get-started).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="4. Outdated URL used">
  If you're having trouble accessing your member web portal, it may be due to using a link with an outdated `andcards.com` domain.

  ![Image](/images/member-web-portal-troubleshoot-issues-with-accessing-member-web-portal-1.png)

  This legacy domain is no longer supported, as it has been replaced by the RNTOR branding and now contains `rntor.com`. Ensure you're using the updated link to avoid access issues.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="5. Member web portal app is suspended">
  If your subscription with RNTOR has been suspended, you will see the **Suspended Subscription** message when accessing the member web portal and RNTOR.

  According to our [terms of use](https://rntor.com/terms), the suspension occurs automatically if a payment is not received within the grace period of 7 days after the invoice issuance.

  <Info>
    Due to the automated nature of RNTOR subscriptions, we are unable to manually adjust suspensions resulting from non-payment within the 7-day grace period.
  </Info>

  To unsuspend and regain access to the member web portal and RNTOR, [the owner](/admin/administration/administrators/owner) needs to [complete the outstanding payment](/admin/administration/billing/make-changes-to-rntor-subscription#update-your-payment-or-billing-information) with RNTOR.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="6. Subscription with RNTOR is no longer active">
  If your subscription with RNTOR was canceled, the member web portal will be restricted on the subscription cancellation date.
</Accordion>
