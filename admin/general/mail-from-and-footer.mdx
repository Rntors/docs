Set a custom sender address for your network and manage your email footer in network settings.

## Set up custom MAIL FROM

Custom MAIL FROM lets supported network emails use your own sender address. Until that address is verified and enabled, Rntor uses its default sender.

<Info>
  Custom MAIL FROM is a subscription add-on. Its renewal cycle follows your network's subscription. Review the amount due now and the renewal terms before confirming the purchase. [Pricing](https://rntor.com/pricing)
</Info>

The network owner can configure the sender in a Live workspace:

1. Open **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Under **Merchant wide (Network)**, open **General** > **Information**.
3. In **MAIL FROM**, select **Subscribe** if the add-on is not already enabled. Review the purchase details, then select **Confirm and pay**.
4. Enter your sender email address and select **Save**.
5. Copy the DNS records displayed below the address to your domain registrar. Use the names, types, values, and priorities shown in Rntor.
6. Select **Check verification**. When the status becomes **Verified and active**, the verified address is available for sending.

If verification is still pending, allow time for your DNS changes to become available and check again. Payment alone does not verify your domain. MAIL FROM settings in Test workspaces are read-only.

Changing only the part before **@** on an already verified domain does not require new DNS records. Save the new address to apply it.

### Improve deliverability for your custom address

<span id="improve-email-deliverability" />

Use a sender address your customers recognize, such as **members@** or **hello@**. Keep the DNS records used to verify the domain in place.

You can also configure DMARC with your domain provider. A monitoring record can use the following values:

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Type | TXT |
| Name | `_dmarc` |
| Value | `v=DMARC1; p=none; sp=none; pct=100; rua=mailto:reports@yourdomain.com;` |
| TTL | Your provider's default |

Replace `reports@yourdomain.com` with an address you manage for receiving DMARC reports.

### How custom MAIL FROM works

The sender address belongs to the network. Supported emails use the verified address while the add-on is active.

<Info>
  Some network notifications still use Rntor's default sender. A custom MAIL FROM address does not currently apply to every type of email.
</Info>

**Migrate custom MAIL FROM to a different hosting provider**

1. Open the DNS settings with your current provider and copy the records originally used to verify your sending domain.
2. Add the same records with your new DNS provider.
3. Keep the old records until the new records are available.

**Migrate custom MAIL FROM to a different domain name**

1. Open **Settings** > **Merchant wide (Network)** > **General** > **Information**.
2. Enter the address on the new domain in **MAIL FROM** and select **Save**.
3. Add the new DNS records shown in Rntor to that domain.
4. Select **Check verification**.

Your current verified sender remains in use while the replacement domain is awaiting verification.

## Customize email footer

The network owner can save the business details to use as an email footer:

1. Open **Settings** > **Merchant wide (Network)** > **General** > **Information**.
2. Under **Email**, enter your business name and postal address in **Footer**.
3. Use **EN** and **AR** to enter the text in each language. Click outside the field to save.

<Info>
  The saved footer is not currently included in every email. Booking confirmations, for example, still use their standard footer.
</Info>

## Troubleshoot email delivery issues

If a customer cannot find a message, look for it in [Email activity](/admin/general/email-activity) and check its status. **Delivered** means the receiving mail server accepted it; it does not guarantee that the message appears in the inbox. A missing record or the absence of an error does not prove delivery.

<Accordion title="Invitations to join">
  1. Check that the customer's email address is correct.
  2. Look for the invitation in **Email activity**. If it bounced or failed, check the address and ask the recipient's email administrator whether the sender is blocked.
  3. If it was delivered, ask the recipient to check spam, inbox rules, and any quarantine managed by their email administrator.
  4. Once the cause is resolved, use **Resend invite** on the customer's page.

  Ask the recipient to allow the sender address used by your network. You can also try another email address the customer provides.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Invoices">
  - Confirm that the customer is checking the email address registered on their **Account** page.
  - Check that the invoice was sent. [Send an invoice](/admin/billing/invoices/issue-invoices)
  - For a company invoice, check that the company manager is selected and has a valid email address. [Select company managers](/admin/customer-management/companies/set-up-companies#add-company-members-and-managers)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Login codes">
  Login-code emails are not included in **Email activity**.

  Ask the user to check their spam folder and inbox filters, confirm the email address they entered, and contact their email administrator if messages are blocked. If the problem continues, [contact support](/admin/administration/contact-support).
</Accordion>

## FAQs

<Accordion title="How quickly is my custom MAIL FROM configured after subscribing?">
  After adding the DNS records, select **Check verification**. The time depends on when your DNS provider makes the records available. The sender is ready when Rntor shows **Verified and active**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I add multiple custom MAIL FROM email addresses?">
  One custom sender address can be active for the network. When changing domains, the previous verified address remains active until the replacement is verified.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to my sender address when the add-on is no longer active?">
  Emails that use custom MAIL FROM return to Rntor's default sender.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What should I do if my customer still does not receive an email?">
  [Contact support](/admin/administration/contact-support) with the recipient's email address, the message type, when it was expected, its status in **Email activity** if available, and the steps you have already tried.
</Accordion>
