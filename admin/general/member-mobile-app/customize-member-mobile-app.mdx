# Customize member mobile app

> Customize your member mobile app's appearance and manage your branded app store listings and screenshots in App Store and Google Play

RNTOR allows you to customize the member mobile app, including colors, icons, and listings on the App Store and Google Play.

Such customizations help improve user experience, strengthen brand identity, and boost engagement with member mobile app.

## Customize the member mobile app appearance

| Change of the background color | ✓ |
| --- | --- |
| Change of the accent color | ✗ (requires mobile release) |
| Font | ✗ (applies only to the member web portal) |

Learn more about [how to customize](/admin/general/member-web-portal/customize-member-web-portal) the background and accent colors through the member web portal.

<Info>
  The theme of the member mobile app follows the device's theme. To change it, users go to the device's settings, find the **Personalization** section, and switch between light or dark mode. The app theme will update automatically.
</Info>

## Update icons for member mobile app

To update icons for the member mobile app, follow the steps below:

1. Log in as an [owner](/admin/administration/administrators/owner) and go to **Network settings** \> **Member** **mobile** **app**.

![Image](/images/member-mobile-app-customize-member-mobile-app-1.png)

2. Tap on **Foreground Graphic** and upload your SVG logo. Ensure that the file meets the following requirements.
   - The logo must be in **SVG format**.
   - Dimensions should be **72 x 72 pixels**.
   - Include only vector shapes (no fonts or raster elements).
   - Ensure the background is transparent and use foreground graphics only.
3. Check the preview to ensure everything seems fine for you. If any image on the preview doesn't display your icon properly, double-check [the requirements](/admin/general/media-formats) and upload the corrected logo.
4. **For Android:** Follow [these steps](#google-play-console) to upload a new icon in Google Play.
5. **For iOS:** Wait for the next mobile app release, as the new icon will be updated with the new app version once it's published on the App Store.

Icons of low-quality or non-compliant icons may result in app rejection.

## Update member mobile app listing

You can update your App Store or Google Play application page's name, custom screenshots, and privacy policy.

### App Store Connect

1. Log in to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/) as the account owner.
2. Open **My Apps** and select your app.
3. Create a new app version: Click the **"\+"** button in the top-left corner and enter a version number that follows standard versioning conventions. For example, if the current version is **4.8.12**, enter **4.8.13**.
4. Update your app information:
   1. App name and subtitle on the **App Information** page under the **General** section of the left navigation panel.
   2. Application screenshots, description, by selecting the new app version under the **iOS App** section. [Screenshot rules and specifications](https://help.apple.com/app-store-connect/#/dev910472ff2)
   3. Privacy Policy on the **App Privacy** page below the **General** section.
5. Always press **Save** top right.

<Info>
  Your changes will go live once the new version is released in the regular cycle.
</Info>

<Warning>
  All changes must comply with Apple's [App Store Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/). Failure to do so may result in your app being rejected by Apple.
</Warning>

### Google Play Console

1. Log in to the [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console/) using the Google Account that's been granted access to your Play Store listing. If you don't have access yet, email [support@rntor.com](mailto:support@rntor.com) to request admin permissions.
2. Select your app in **All apps**.
3. Go to the **Main store listing** under the **Grow** section of the left navigation pane.
4. Make your changes:
   1. App name
   2. Short description
   3. Long description
   4. Application icon. [Google Play icon design specifications](https://developer.android.com/distribute/google-play/resources/icon-design-specifications)
   5. Feature graphics according to the specifications mentioned on the page.
   6. Phone screenshots according to the specifications mentioned on the page.
5. Press **Save** on the bottom right. Your app store listing should update within a few minutes.

### Notes

- If you encounter display issues when uploading an SVG file, consider the following steps:
  1. Inspect the SVG file to confirm it was created correctly and is not corrupted. This includes consulting the designer if necessary.
  2. Ensure the SVG file has a transparent background and includes only foreground graphics. This is essential for compliance and proper display.
  3. Once corrections are made, re-upload the file and review the visual preview across all icons to confirm it displays as intended. Proper troubleshooting can prevent display inaccuracies and maintain the app's professional appearance.
