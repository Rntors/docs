The member mobile app is a white-label application for your customers on Google Play and the App Store. It gives members easy, mobile access to bookings, payments, and all your services.

A modern, smartphone experience increases member engagement, elevates your brand, and helps you stay connected with members in real-time.

## Prerequisites

1. Open an Apple Developer account as an organization and [prepare it for new app submissions](/admin/general/member-mobile-app/prepare-your-apple-developer-account).
2. [Generate App Store Connect API Key](/admin/general/member-mobile-app/prepare-your-apple-developer-account) and copy the Key ID and Issuer ID from your Apple Developer account.
3. Prepare mobile app icon according to [Google and Apple requirements](/admin/general/media-formats),-Format):
   - **Format:** SVG format.
   - **Size:** 72x72 px.
   - **Background:** No background. The logo must be **transparent.**
4. If you haven't yet, [sign up for the Member mobile app add-on](/admin/administration/billing/make-changes-to-rntor-subscription).

## Get your member mobile apps created

1. [Invite RNTOR](/admin/general/member-mobile-app/prepare-your-apple-developer-account) ([publisher@rntor.com](mailto:publisher@andcards.com)) to your Apple Developer organization account.
2. Go to the **Network settings** \> **Member mobile app** page as an [owner](/admin/administration/administrators/owner) and submit all the required details for your app creation:
   - Upload your foreground graphic, select the background color.
   - Pass your Apple Developer account details.
   - Upload your API key file, enter Key ID, and Issuer ID.

Once submitted, we'll start preparing your iOS and Android mobile apps. Once they're live, we'll inform you via email. If the mobile app has been rejected, we'll resubmit it to Apple and Google within 48 hours.

<Info>
  When publishing apps, we use our template descriptions, keywords, and screenshots for the initial app listing. Once published under your brand, you can [update the listing](/admin/general/member-mobile-app/customize-member-mobile-app) as needed.
</Info>

### Prepare for submission

Apple and Google review your app's content and functionality. Missing content is a common cause for rejection and can significantly delay your launch.

To ensure smooth approval, complete the following before submission:

1. Ensure your [privacy policy](/admin/general/privacy-policy) and [terms of use](/admin/general/terms-of-use) are compliant and include your network name.
2. Add bookable resources ([rooms](/admin/resource-management/resources/set-up-resources#rooms), [desks](/admin/resource-management/resources/set-up-resources#desks), [offices](/admin/resource-management/resources/set-up-resources#offices), etc.) with cover images. If you don't have the final images yet, use temporary ones and update them later.
3. [Create a post](/admin/engagement/feed) on the **Feed** page. This could be a message to welcome your first members.
4. [Create an upcoming event](/admin/engagement/events/set-up-events) on the **Events** page with a description and cover photo. The event can be scheduled for any future date, and you can always [edit](/admin/engagement/events/set-up-events#edit-an-event) or [delete](/admin/engagement/events/set-up-events#delete-an-event) it after your apps are published.
5. [Complete the](/admin/location/information) [**Information**](/admin/location/information) [page](/admin/location/information) with photos of your space, a description, contact details, location schedule, and any other relevant information.

## Access member mobile apps

- Once your member mobile apps are published, your [landing page](/admin/general/member-web-portal/customize-landing-page) will automatically be updated with App Store and Google Play badges with direct links for customers to download your member mobile app.

![Image](/images/member-mobile-app-1.png)

- You can also copy and share direct links to download your member mobile apps through your website, QR codes, social media, or advertisements. [Promote mobile apps](/guides/promotion/promote-member-web-portal-and-mobile-app)

<Info>
  To download the member mobile app, the device must have Android 8.0 or higher, or iOS 17.0 or later.
</Info>

## How member mobile app works

- **White-labeled and customizable:** The member mobile app is fully white-labeled, allowing you to enhance brand presence with custom logos, colors, and [push notifications](/admin/administration/your-account/notifications/set-up-notifications#push-notifications).
- **Accessible on Google Play and App Store:** The app is available for download on both major platforms, making it effortless for customers to download it and access your services.
- **Mobile-optimized experience:** Designed specifically for mobile devices, the app mirrors the member web portal, offering the same functionality, but optimized for smartphone use.
- **Better user experience:** Users can make bookings, payments, join events, and access all your services faster and easier.

**FAQ**

<Accordion title="How can I test the member mobile app before the release?">
  To get a firsthand experience of how the mobile apps look and function, we recommend downloading and testing the apps of our customers. You can find the download links at the bottom of their respective landing pages. For example, explore the mobile apps for [FoundrSpace Coworking](https://app.foundrspace.com/) or [Hatch Coworking](https://app.hatchcoworking.com/).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a limit on the app name length?">
  Yes, the limit for app names on both Apple's App Store and Google's Play Store is **30 characters**. If your space name is longer, consider alternative names.
</Accordion>
