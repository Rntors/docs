Rntor connects bookings and memberships to door access through [Tapkey](/admin/integrations/access-control/tapkey). Customers manage their bookings in Rntor and use the Tapkey app when they arrive at your space.

## Integration comparison

| Integration | Unlock in Rntor | Access basis | App invitation | Maintained by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Tapkey](/admin/integrations/access-control/tapkey) | No | Bookings and subscriptions | No | Rntor |

Customers install the Tapkey app themselves; Rntor does not email an invitation to install it. Access requires a confirmed booking or an active subscription.

## Main difference

Rntor manages **who can enter and when**. Tapkey handles **opening the door**.

- Link Tapkey doors or available user groups to resources, membership plans, or subscription items.
- Rntor updates the access it manages when an eligible booking or subscription changes, expires, or is canceled.
- If another eligible booking or subscription still covers the same access, that access is retained.

### Unlocking outside Rntor

**Supported integration: Tapkey**

1. The customer has a confirmed booking or active subscription linked to Tapkey access.
2. Rntor synchronizes their access with the connected Tapkey account.
3. The customer installs the Tapkey app and signs in with the email address used in Rntor.
4. At the door, the customer uses Tapkey to unlock it.

<Info>
  Tapkey access is configured separately for each location in a Live workspace. Customers open doors in Tapkey; there is no Tapkey unlock button in the Rntor member portal.
</Info>

See the [Tapkey setup guide](/admin/integrations/access-control/tapkey) for prerequisites, access settings, customer instructions, and disconnecting.
