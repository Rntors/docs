Rntor supports [Tapkey](/admin/integrations/access-control/tapkey) for automatic door access based on bookings and subscriptions. Customers use Rntor to book your services and the Tapkey app to enter your space.

## Integration comparison

| Integration | Unlocking in Rntor | Access granted for | Email invitation to install the app | Developed and maintained by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Tapkey](/admin/integrations/access-control/tapkey) | No | Bookings and subscriptions | No; customers download Tapkey themselves | Rntor |

## Main difference

Rntor manages access automatically:

- Eligible bookings and subscriptions grant the customer the access configured for the resource, plan, or subscription item.
- When eligibility ends, Rntor removes that access unless another active booking or subscription still provides it.

Tapkey handles unlocking through its own app.

### Unlocking outside Rntor

**Supported integration: Tapkey**

1. A customer makes a confirmed booking or starts an eligible subscription in Rntor.
2. Rntor synchronizes the customer's access with Tapkey.
3. The customer installs Tapkey, signs in with the same email address used in Rntor, and unlocks the door when they arrive.

Customers install the app themselves; they should not wait for a Tapkey invitation email. See [Tapkey](/admin/integrations/access-control/tapkey) for setup and customer instructions.
