Use Tapkey with Rntor to manage door access for confirmed bookings and active memberships. Choose the doors or user groups associated with each resource, plan, or subscription item, then let Rntor synchronize eligible customers.

<Info>
  Tapkey supplies its own locks, apps, and account services. Contact Tapkey for hardware compatibility, account features, and pricing.
</Info>

## Prerequisites

1. Prepare a Tapkey administrator account with the locks for your location.
2. If you will use **Tapkey groups**, enable user groups on that account and configure each group's door permissions in Tapkey.
3. Use a **Live** Rntor workspace and an administrator account authorized to manage the location's integrations and the resources or billing settings you will edit.
4. Make sure customers have an active Rntor customer record and a valid email address.

<Warning>
  Use groups dedicated to Rntor. Access added manually in Tapkey is preserved by Rntor and may continue after a Rntor booking or subscription ends.
</Warning>

## Connect Tapkey integration

1. Select the location, open **Integrations**, and choose **Tapkey**.
2. Click **Connect Tapkey**.
3. Sign in to Tapkey as an administrator and authorize the connection.
4. If asked, select the Tapkey account that owns the location's locks and click **Save**.
5. Check the **Connected account** before configuring access.

<Info>
  Each location has its own connection and access settings. Tapkey cannot be connected from a Test workspace.
</Info>

## Configure access for Tapkey

Configure access from the **Access** section of a saved resource, membership plan, or subscription item:

- **Tapkey doors:** select individual doors.
- **Tapkey groups:** select existing user groups when the connected Tapkey account supports them. The group's door permissions are managed in Tapkey.

Selections are saved when you change them. Synchronization runs automatically; a saved selection does not mean the customer's access has finished synchronizing.

### Access for subscriptions

#### For future new members

1. Open [Billing > Plans](/admin/billing/plans/set-up-plans) and edit the membership plan.
2. In **Access**, select the required **Tapkey doors** or **Tapkey groups**.
3. [Create the customer's subscription](/admin/billing/subscriptions/set-up-subscriptions), or let the customer [sign up for the plan](/admin/billing/plans/immediate-plan-sign-ups).

Create and save a new plan first, then reopen it to configure Access. For an individual resource rental, configure the saved subscription item that covers the rental.

#### For existing members

1. [Open the subscription](/admin/billing/subscriptions/edit-a-subscription).
2. Open the relevant saved subscription item.
3. Select its doors or groups under **Access**.

Plan access and item access can both apply. When removing access, check whether another plan, item, or booking still grants it.

### Access for resource bookings

1. Go to **Resources** and open **Rooms**, **Desks**, **Offices**, **Parking**, or **Equipment**.
2. Edit the [saved resource](/admin/resource-management/resources/set-up-resources).
3. Find **Access** and select the doors or groups that customers need for that resource.

A resource selection controls access for its confirmed bookings. Set subscription access separately on the plan or subscription item.

### Access for plans with day passes

To give access only on booked days:

1. Leave Tapkey doors and groups unselected on the plan and its subscription items.
2. Configure access on the hot desk resource.
3. Have the customer book the desk using their day pass.

The confirmed booking then determines the access period, including the location's configured time before and after bookings.

<Warning>
  Selecting access on a plan or subscription item can grant access throughout its eligible subscription period, including days without a desk booking.
</Warning>

## How Tapkey integration works

### Access based on subscriptions

- Access follows active, unsuspended subscriptions and their eligible items.
- Each item's dates are limited by the subscription's dates. Access starts at midnight on the start date and covers the end date, using the location's time zone.
- An eligible company subscription covers active customers belonging to that company at the same location, including customers added later.
- Ending, canceling, or suspending the subscription removes the access it provides unless another entitlement still grants it.

Invoice payment status is not a separate access rule. Manage eligibility through the subscription's status and dates. You can [edit its end date](/admin/billing/subscriptions/edit-a-subscription) or [cancel the subscription](/admin/billing/subscriptions/cancel-a-subscription).

### Access based on bookings

- Only **confirmed** bookings grant access; pending bookings do not.
- Access covers the booking customer and room invitees linked to eligible customer records at that location.
- Rescheduling, extending, shortening, or canceling a booking updates the access it provides.
- Overlapping access periods are combined. Separate bookings do not grant access during the gap between them.

In **Integrations > Tapkey > Access window**, set **Minutes before a booking** and **Minutes after a booking**, then click **Save**. Both default to **15 minutes** and can be set from **0 to 240 minutes**.

With the default settings:

| Booking time | Access window |
| --- | --- |
| 10:00 AM–12:00 PM | 9:45 AM–12:15 PM |
| 2:00 PM–3:30 PM | 1:45 PM–3:45 PM |

<Note>
  Access changes synchronize in the background. Check **Access synchronization** for **Synced**, **Pending**, and **Failed** counts. **Sync now** requests another synchronization; it does not open a door.
</Note>

### How users unlock doors

1. Install the Tapkey mobile app.
2. Sign in using the same email address as the Rntor customer account.
3. Allow the app to synchronize the available access.
4. Stand near the relevant lock and unlock it through Tapkey during the permitted period.

<Warning>
  Tapkey locks can operate offline. A revoked key may remain usable until the phone or lock synchronizes, so a completed removal is not a guarantee of immediate revocation at an offline door.
</Warning>

#### Inform customers how to unlock doors

Include the Tapkey app and matching-email instructions in your customer onboarding:

- Add them to the resource's [booking confirmation email](/admin/general/customize-emails).
- Publish a [guide explaining how to enter the space](/admin/support/guides).

Ask customers to install Tapkey before arrival rather than wait for an automatic app invitation. Direct customers to Tapkey for unlocking.

## Disconnect Tapkey

1. Open the location's **Integrations > Tapkey** page.
2. Click **Disconnect** and confirm.
3. Wait while Rntor removes the access it created. The page shows the disconnecting status during cleanup.

Access created manually in Tapkey is kept. Disconnecting finishes after managed access has been removed.

<Warning>
  If **Access needs review in Tapkey** appears, review the affected group memberships in Tapkey and remove access that should no longer exist. Rntor keeps checking and finishes disconnecting once the unresolved access is gone. Offline-lock synchronization limits still apply.
</Warning>

## FAQs

<Accordion title="How should I organize access for different doors?">
  Create dedicated groups in Tapkey, then select the matching groups in Rntor. For example:

  - A shared entrance can be assigned to the resources and plans that require it.
  - A private office group can contain the entrance and that office's door.
  - A meeting room group can contain the entrance and the booked room's door.

  Configure any group schedule in Tapkey. You can also use **Tapkey doors** to map individual doors directly.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why are no Tapkey groups available?">
  The selected Tapkey account must support user groups and allow Rntor to read and manage membership. If there are no groups, create them in Tapkey. If Rntor requests reconnection, sign in again with the required administrator permissions. Reconnecting does not enable a missing account feature; contact Tapkey about enabling it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why has access not synchronized?">
  Check the connected account, customer's email and active status, booking confirmation or subscription eligibility, and the selected doors or groups. Resolve any reconnection message, review **Access synchronization**, and use **Sync now** if needed. A pending or failed synchronization is not confirmation that a key is ready.
</Accordion>
