Paystack integration allows you to receive payments in RNTOR.

<Info>
  🌍 Paystack is available in Côte d'Ivoire, Egypt, Ghana, Kenya, Nigeria, Rwanda, and South Africa.
</Info>

</Info>

## Connect Paystack

To activate Paystack, go to RNTOR and do the following:

1. Go to the **Integrations** page by pressing the menu **(...)** next to the location's name.

![Image](/images/integrations/payments-paystack-1.png)

2. Find the **Paystack** integration page. _On this page, you'll also see two helpful buttons:_
   - **About**: For general information about the integration.
   - **Docs**: For detailed instructions on connecting and configuring.

![Image](/images/integrations/payments-paystack-2.png)

3. Press **Connect**.

To connect the integration, you need to find a Secret Key from the settings of your Paystack account. Generate your Secret key from Paystack following this [guide](https://support.paystack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360011508199-How-do-I-generate-new-API-keys-).

## Configure Paystack

You can activate payments for the following:

- Bookings
- Event tickets
- Shop products
- Invoices
- Credit packages

## How Paystack works

The integration adds **New payment method** at the checkout section around member web portal and mobile app. Users selecting the **New payment method** are redirected to the Paystack payment page and can use any of the payment methods enabled.

## Identify users' transactions in Paystack

You can easily find users' transactions in the Paystack dashboard using the following unique identifiers:

- ID — coincides with `Transaction ID` in Paystack dashboard. You can copy the ID of the transaction from the [**Transactions** custom report](/admin/analytics-reports/reports).
- Time — coincides with the date and time of the transaction in the Pastak dashboard.
- Description — the types of purchases users make, such as bookings, **Shop** orders, etc.

## Disconnect Paystack

To disconnect Paystack integration, follow these steps:

1. Go to RNTOR \> **Integrations** page by pressing the menu **(...)** next to the location's name \> **Paystack.**
2. Press **Disconnect** and confirm your choice.
