The **Feed** page is your go-to place for updates, announcements, and community engagement. Keep your customers informed about upcoming events, important news, and activities happening in your workspace.

## Set up Feed

1. In the left-side panel, click the menu **(...)** next to your location name.
2. Go to **Location settings** > **Engagement** page > **Feed** tab and toggle the **Feed** switch on.
3. Configure **Feed** permissions: ​
   * **Visibility** — control who can see your **Feed** page. The visibility of the **Feed** page determines the visibility of all posts.
   * **Posting** — choose who is allowed to create new posts. Typically, this includes administrators who share updates or announcements.
   * **Viewing comments** — control who can see comments below each post. To encourage high engagement, set comments to be public. Alternatively, you can limit comment visibility to a selected group, such as administrators, location members, or network members.
   * **Commenting** — define who can reply under posts.

![Feed image 1](/images/engagement-feed-1.png)

<Info>
  These settings define permissions for all posts on the **Feed** page.
</Info>

## Create a post

1. Go to **Engagement > Feed** and press **Create post** top right.

![Feed image 2](/images/engagement-feed-2.png)

2. Add the contents of your post.
3. Press **Post**. All members of the location will immediately get notified with a push notification.

### Content formatting and attachments

Format your text using a visual editor and shortcuts:

* Floating toolbar: Select text and format it (bold, italic, inline code, highlight, code block, hyperlink) with a quick-access menu.

![Feed image 3](/images/engagement-feed-3.png)

* Keyboard shortcuts:
  * **Command + B** / **Ctrl + B** → **Bold**
  * **Command + I** / **Ctrl + I** → *Italic*
* Use `#` to create a hashtag.

You can attach one or multiple images and videos. Plain links added in text will automatically show a preview with a cover image.

### Use hashtags in posts

* Add hashtags to your feed posts to organize them by topics and make it easier for interested users to find related posts. When a user clicks on a hashtag, the **Feed** page is filtered to show all posts that contain the same hashtag.
* The 5 most frequently used hashtags in the last 6 months are prominently shown at the top of the **Feed** page. This allows quick and convenient access to popular or important categories of posts for all users.

## Edit a post

Administrators can edit their own posts as well as those created by other administrators and users. For this:

1. Go to **Engagement** > **Feed**.
2. Hover over the post > click the menu **(...)** next to it > **Edit**.
3. Change the post and press **Save**.

Users do not receive a notifications when you edit a post.

## Pin a post

Feed posts are sorted from the most recent to the oldest. To pin a certain post:

1. Go to **Engagement** > **Feed**.
2. Click the empty **Pin** icon next to the necessary post.

To unpin a pinned post, click the filled **Pin** icon next to the necessary post.

<Info>
  Only administrators can pin and unpin posts.
</Info>

## Delete a post

1. Go to **Engagement** > **Feed**.
2. Hover over the post > click the menu **(...)** next to it > press **Delete** and confirm your choice.

<Info>
  Administratora can delete any post at any time.  Users can only delete their own posts.
</Info>

<Warning>
  Deleting a post is irreversible and can not be undone.
</Warning>

## Report a post

Users can report feed posts if they consider the content to be offensive or objectionable. For this, users:

1. Go to **Locations > Feed** and find a post.
2. Click the **(...)** menu next to this post > **Report**.
3. Once a user reports a post, administrators receive a support ticket about it.
