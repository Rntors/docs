The **Feed** page is your go-to place for updates, announcements, and community engagement. Keep your customers informed about upcoming events, important news, and activities happening in your workspace.

## Set up Feed

1. In the left-side panel, click the menu **(…)** next to your location name.
2. Go to **Location settings > Engagement > Feed** and turn on the **Feed** switch.
3. Configure Feed permissions:
   - **Visibility** — control who can see your Feed page. The visibility of the Feed page determines the visibility of all posts.
   - **Posting** — choose who can create posts. This typically includes administrators who share updates and announcements.
   - **Viewing comments** — control who can see comments below each post. To encourage engagement, make comments public, or limit visibility to administrators, location members, or network members.
   - **Commenting** — define who can reply under posts.

These settings define permissions for all posts on the Feed page.

## Create a post

1. Go to **Engagement > Feed** and click **Create post** in the top-right corner.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="Create a post from the RNTOR Feed page" />
</Frame>

2. Add the contents of your post.
3. Click **Post**. All members of the location immediately receive a push notification.

### Content formatting and attachments

Format your text using the visual editor and shortcuts:

- **Floating toolbar:** Select text and format it with **bold**, *italic*, inline code, highlight, code block, or hyperlink.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="Format Feed post content with the RNTOR visual editor" />
</Frame>

- **Keyboard shortcuts:**
  - **Command + B / Ctrl + B** → Bold
  - **Command + I / Ctrl + I** → Italic
- Use `#` to create a hashtag.

You can attach one or multiple images and videos. Plain links added in the text automatically display a preview with a cover image. Review the supported [media formats](/admin/general/media-formats).

### Use hashtags in posts

- Add hashtags to Feed posts to organize them by topic and help interested users find related posts. When a user clicks a hashtag, the Feed is filtered to show every post containing it.
- The five most frequently used hashtags from the last six months appear at the top of the Feed, giving users quick access to popular or important topics.

## Edit a post

Administrators can edit their own posts and posts created by other administrators or users:

1. Go to **Engagement > Feed**.
2. Hover over the post and click **(…) > Edit**.
3. Update the post and click **Save**.

Users do not receive a notification when you edit a post.

## Pin a post

Feed posts are sorted from newest to oldest. To pin a post:

1. Go to **Engagement > Feed**.
2. Click the empty **Pin** icon next to the post.

To unpin it, click the filled **Pin** icon. Only administrators can pin and unpin posts.

## Delete a post

1. Go to **Engagement > Feed**.
2. Hover over the post, click **(…) > Delete**, and confirm.

Administrators can delete any post. Users can delete only their own posts.

<Warning>
  Deleting a post is irreversible and cannot be undone.
</Warning>

## Report a post

Users can report Feed posts they consider offensive or objectionable:

1. Go to **Locations > Feed** and find the post.
2. Click **(…) > Report**.
3. RNTOR creates a [support ticket](/admin/support/tickets) for administrators.
