title: "Chats"
description: "Enable in-app Chats so administrators and members can message each other one-on-one or in groups."
---

Use in-app Chats to centralize communication and encourage member networking. Message people one-on-one or create group chats that bring several members together. With Chats, you can:

- Replace external messaging tools by letting members connect and message each other through your member web portal and mobile app.
- Centralize communication between customers and administrators.
- Bring members together in group chats to reach a company, share announcements, or connect people with shared interests.

## Enable Chats

1. Click **(…)** next to the network name and go to **Network settings > Chats**.
2. Turn on the **Chats** switch.

  <img src="" alt="Enable Chats in RNTOR network settings" />
</Frame>

Once enabled, **Chats** appears in the main navigation for administrators and users.

Chats work at the network level. When enabled, they are available in all locations.

## One-on-one chats

### How administrators chat with customers

Administrators can chat with any customer to provide support or follow up on requests.

1. Go to **Chats**.
2. Open an existing conversation, or click **New chat** in the top-right corner and search for a customer.

  <img src="" alt="Start a one-on-one chat with a customer in RNTOR" />
</Frame>

You can also start a chat directly from the customer profile or by opening their profile from another page.

### How users chat with other users

Members can chat with other community members depending on your [visibility settings](/admin/location/visibility).

- If the **Community** page visibility is **Location members** or **Network members**, members can browse the community and start a chat with another member of that location or network.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="Start a chat with another community member in RNTOR" />
</Frame>

- If Community visibility is **Administrators**, members can only start chats with people who deliberately made themselves discoverable elsewhere. For example, they can click the name of someone who published a Feed post.

## Group chats

Group chats bring several members together in one conversation. Use them to:

- Message an entire company or a selected set of members, such as founders from different companies.
- Group members by shared interest, such as a yoga group, speaking club, or film club.

### How administrators manage group chats

Only administrators can create group chats:

1. Go to **Chats** and click **New chat > New group**.
2. Search for and select participants, then click **Next**.
   - An administrator can add members from locations they manage.
   - An owner can add anyone in the network, including members and nonmembers across all locations.
3. Enter a group title and click **Create**.

To manage a group, open it and click **(…)** in the top-right corner:

- **Information** — view the group title and participants.
- **Edit** — rename the group or add and remove participants.
- **Delete** — permanently delete the group for everyone.

### What members can do

When added to a group, a member can:

- Send messages to everyone in the group.
- Open **(…) > Information** to see the participants.
- Select **(…) > Leave** to leave the group at any time.

A member added to an existing group sees the full message history, including messages sent before they joined.

## Manage messages

- **Attachments:** To send files, contracts, or images, click the **Attachment** (paperclip) icon. Review the allowed [media and file formats](/admin/general/media-formats).
- **Delete messages:** Users and administrators can unsend a message by hovering over it and selecting **(…) > Delete**.

## What's next?

- [Set up administrator notifications](/admin/administration/your-account/notifications/set-up-notifications) to stay alerted to new messages.
- Review [visibility settings](/admin/location/visibility) to control how users discover each other.
