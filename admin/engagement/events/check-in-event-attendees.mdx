---
title: "Check in event attendees"
description: "Learn how to check in attendees, track event attendance, and export attendee lists for follow-up."
---

Administrators can check in event attendees to keep track of who has arrived. RNTOR offers two convenient methods.

## Check in by scanning attendee tickets

1. On your mobile device, log in to RNTOR in your browser or PWA (Progressive Web App).
2. Ask the attendee to open their ticket from **Account > Tickets** on their device.
3. Scan the QR code on the ticket using your phone camera.
4. Tap **Check in**.

![Scan an event attendee ticket and check them in with RNTOR](/images/engagement-event-check-in-1.png)

## Check in directly from your RNTOR account

1. Log in to RNTOR on your mobile device or desktop.
2. Go to **Engagement > Events**, open the event, and select the attendee.
3. Tap **Check in** at the bottom of the page.

![Check in an attendee directly from an RNTOR event](/images/engagement-event-check-in-2.png)

Checked-in event attendees have a green checkbox next to their name in the attendee list.

![Checked-in event attendee indicator in RNTOR](/images/engagement-event-check-in-3.png)

## Export event attendees

1. After your event, go to **Engagement > Events** and open the relevant event.
2. Click **(…)** in the top-right corner and select **Export attendees as CSV**.

![Export event attendees as CSV from RNTOR](/images/engagement-event-check-in-4.png)

The CSV includes attendee contacts, creation date, check-in date, and payment details.

Use this information to tailor your outreach. For example, send a personalized thank-you and share event highlights with attendees to improve future event planning and conversions.
