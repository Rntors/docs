Monitor occupancy for all resource types on the **Occupancy** page to coordinate with your sales team and lease available spaces.

## Track occupancy rates

1. Go to **Analytics \> Occupancy**.
2. Review:
   - **Overall occupancy rate** — occupied desk and office seats, through bookings or long-term assignments, as a percentage of all available seats.
   - **Occupancy rate for each resource type** — how each type is utilized today.

![Occupancy dashboard](/images/analytics/track-occupancy-dashboard.png)

| Resource type | Calculated by | Calculation details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rooms | Hours per day | Booked hours as a percentage of all available hours for the day |
| Desks | Seats | Booked or assigned seats as a percentage of all desk seats |
| Offices | Seats | Booked or assigned seats as a percentage of all office seats |
| Parking | Lots | Booked or assigned lots as a percentage of all parking lots |
| Equipment | Items | Booked items as a percentage of all equipment items |

For rooms, seating capacity is not used. Occupancy is based on booked time versus all available hours in the [default or public location schedule](/admin/location/schedule). Custom room schedules are not applied.
