The **Analytics** page serves as a comprehensive dashboard that provides key performance indicators and metrics for your workspace. This powerful tool enables tracking and analyzing important data points such as invoices, volume, bookings, and much more.

## View Analytics

1. Go to RNTOR \> **Analytics** page.
2. Get a quick overview of all dashboards for the current month's data.

![Analytics View Analytics](/images/analytics/analytics-view-analytics.png)

## Analytics overview

By digging into each dashboard, you can get a consolidated view of the following statistics:

- **Customers**: provides the number of individual users and companies within your workspace community.
- **Contracts**: shows the quantity of the contracts of different statuses within different periods of time (a week, a month, half a year, or a year).
- **Subscriptions**: gives the number of customers with active subscriptions and identifies popular subscription periods; provides statistics on the number of subscriptions for different products (plans and resources), as well as revenue from subscriptions to each product.

![Image](/images/analytics/analytics-2.png)

- **Invoices**: shows the quantity and volume of the invoices of different statuses within different periods of time (a week, a month, half a year, or a year).

![Image](/images/analytics/analytics-3.png)

- **Payments**: gives detailed statistics on the payments made for each resource you offer during a selected period (a week, a month, half a year, or a year).

![Image](/images/analytics/analytics-4.png)

- [**Occupancy**](/admin/analytics-reports/track-occupancy): displays the real-time overall occupancy rate of your space and the occupancy rate for each resource type.
- **Bookings**: provides insights into the number of bookings for each resource type, statistics by payment method, and volume generated from bookings during a selected period (a week, a month, half a year, or a year).
- **Feed**: showcases statistics on posts, likes, and comments, and provides a graphical representation of activity trends during a selected time period (a week, a month, half a year, or a year).
- **Events**: provides insights into attendees by payment method and displays the overall volume generated during a selected time period (a week, a month, half a year, or a year).
- **Shop**: offers information on orders made by payment method, the overall volume gained during a selected time period (a week, a month, half a year, or a year), and provides information on orders by status.
- **Benefits**: quantifies the number of benefits and applications during a selected time period (a week, a month, half a year, or a year).
- **Visitors**: offers insights into visits by type, and requests by status with the graphical representations of these data points during a selected time period (a week, a month, half a year, or a year).
- **Support**: allows you to track tickets by type and status during a selected time period (a week, a month, half a year, or a year).

<Tip>
  Use the date range and granularity filters to compare data across different periods.
</Tip>

## Export data from Analytics

For a deeper analysis, you can export the data and tailor it to your specific needs with [custom reports](/admin/analytics-reports/reports).
