The **Analytics** page is a comprehensive dashboard with key performance indicators for invoices, payment volume, bookings, occupancy, and other workspace activity.

## View Analytics

1. Go to **RNTOR \> Analytics**.
2. Review the overview, which initially shows the current month's data.

![Analytics overview](/images/analytics/dashboards-overview.png)

## Analytics overview

Open each dashboard for a consolidated view:

- **Customers** — individual users and companies in your community.
- **Contracts** — contracts by status across week, month, half-year, or year.
- **Subscriptions** — active customers, popular periods and products, and subscription revenue by product.
- **Invoices** — invoice count and volume by status and period.
- **Payments** — payment statistics by the resources and services offered.
- [**Occupancy**](/admin/analytics-reports/track-occupancy) — real-time overall and per-resource-type occupancy.
- **Bookings** — booking count, payment methods, and volume by resource type.
- **Feed** — posts, likes, comments, and activity trends.
- **Events** — attendees by payment method and generated volume.
- **Shop** — orders by payment method and status, plus generated volume.
- **Benefits** — benefits and applications.
- **Visitors** — visits by type and requests by status.
- **Support** — tickets by type and status.

![Analytics dashboards](/images/analytics/dashboards-modules.png)

Use date-range and granularity filters to compare periods.

## Export data from Analytics

For deeper analysis, export the data and tailor it with [custom reports](/admin/analytics-reports/reports).
