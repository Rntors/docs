---
title: "Track sales"
description: "Track and analyze your space sales by monitoring daily or weekly revenue trends on the Analytics dashboards, and download detailed reports for transactions, subscriptions, and resource bookings"
---

Analytics in RNTOR allows you to track sales from all business activities within the space, such as bookings, subscriptions, payments, and others.

Visit the **Analytics**page to monitor your sales by period or download custom reports to get comprehensive sales reports.

## Track all sales

### Quick daily/weekly tracking

To check all your successfully processed payments go to **Analytics** \> **Payments** page. Use the **Volume by source** graph to see revenue sources (e.g., memberships, bookings) and choose a relevant timeline:

- 7D/28D - bookings by days.
- 26W - bookings by weeks.
- 12M - bookings by months.

![Image](/images/analytics/analytics-track-sales-1.png)

### Generate a report

To get a report of all transactions go to **Analytics** \> press **Create report** and select the **Transactions** report type.

Set the necessary start and end date, select **Money** to include only monetary transactions, keep **All** in the **Users & companies** dropdown, and only the **Paid** filter for transaction status.

![Image](/images/analytics/analytics-track-sales-2.png)

The report will allow you to view the total revenue, breakdowns by source, and other insights.

## Track membership plan sales

### Quick daily/weekly tracking

To check your current membership plan sales go to the **Analytics** page \> **Subscriptions** tab.

- The **Subscriptions** tab: displays the current number of active subscriptions.
- The **Subscription items by plan**: shows active subscriptions for the most popular membership plans.
- The **Subscription volume by plan**: shows recurring revenue.

![Image](/images/analytics/analytics-track-sales-3.png)

### Generate a report

##### **Subscriptions custom report**

To get full insight into subscriptions in your space, you can download a report of the **Subscriptions** type by going to the **Analytics** page \> press **Create report**. Configure the statuses to filter out the needed information and press the **Download** button.

![Image](/images/analytics/analytics-track-sales-4.png)

##### **Invoice Items custom report**

To get a report about invoice payments for each membership plan, select the **Invoice Items** report type, set the necessary create date and issue date, filter the **Status** field as required, and press the **Download** button.

![Image](/images/analytics/analytics-track-sales-5.png)

The report will show the revenue of the issued invoices and what membership plan is the most popular among the customers.

## Track room and desk booking sales

### Quick daily/weekly tracking

To check all your revenue go to **Analytics** \> **Rooms** or **Desks** tab for this month's booking stats and select a timeline.

- **Bookings by payment method**: shows the number of bookings.
- **Volume**: shows the revenue from bookings.

![Image](/images/analytics/analytics-track-sales-6.png)

### Generate a report

To get a report on bookings, go to **Analytics** \> **Create report** and select **Bookings**, set the necessary create and start dates. In the **Type** section choose your resource type, enable **Resource** and choose the individual resources if required.

![Image](/images/analytics/analytics-track-sales-7.png)

These reports will allow you to get insights into the number of bookings made, purchased subscriptions, as well as the revenue earned.
