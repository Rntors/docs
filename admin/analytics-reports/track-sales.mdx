---
title: "Track sales"
description: "Monitor sales performance"
---

Analytics in RNTOR lets you track sales from bookings, subscriptions, payments, and other activities by period, or download custom reports for deeper analysis.

## Track all sales

### Quick daily/weekly tracking

Go to **Analytics \> Payments** and use **Volume by source** to see revenue sources. Choose:

- **7D/28D** for daily figures.
- **26W** for weekly figures.
- **12M** for monthly figures.

![Payment volume by source](/images/analytics/sales-payment-volume.png)

### Generate a report

Go to **Analytics \> Create report \> Transactions**. Select a start and end date, choose **Money**, leave **All** under users and companies, and keep only **Paid** status.

![Transactions report](/images/analytics/sales-transactions-report.png)

## Track membership plan sales

### Quick daily/weekly tracking

Go to **Analytics \> Subscriptions**:

- **Subscriptions** shows the current number of active subscriptions.
- **Subscription items by plan** shows active subscriptions for popular plans.
- **Subscription volume by plan** shows recurring revenue.

![Subscription sales](/images/analytics/sales-subscriptions.png)

### Generate a report

#### Subscriptions report

Go to **Analytics \> Create report \> Subscriptions**, choose the needed statuses, and download.

![Subscriptions custom report](/images/analytics/sales-subscriptions-report.png)

#### Invoice items report

Select **Invoice items**, set creation and issue dates, filter by status, and download. The report shows issued-invoice revenue and plan popularity.

![Invoice items report](/images/analytics/sales-invoice-items.png)

## Track room and desk booking sales

### Quick daily/weekly tracking

Go to **Analytics \> Rooms** or **Desks** for current-month statistics:

- **Bookings by payment method** shows booking count.
- **Volume** shows booking revenue.

![Room and desk sales](/images/analytics/sales-bookings.png)

### Generate a report

Go to **Analytics \> Create report \> Bookings**, set creation and start dates, choose the resource type, and optionally select individual resources.

![Bookings report](/images/analytics/sales-bookings-report.png)

These reports reveal booking counts, subscriptions sold, and earned revenue.
