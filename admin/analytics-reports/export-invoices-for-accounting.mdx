---
title: "Export invoices for accounting"
description: "Learn how to export and bulk download invoices from RNTOR for your accounting software"
---

If [automated integrations](/admin/billing/invoices/accounting) between RNTOR and your accounting platform don't meet your needs, you can easily export all invoices from RNTOR using custom reports and import them into your accounting software.

RNTOR lets you export invoice data in CSV reports and download invoice PDFs.

## Export CSV reports

CSV reports are ideal for bulk uploading invoice data into accounting software.

| **Report type** | **Purpose** |
| --- | --- |
| Invoices | Export consolidated invoice data with total amounts per invoice. |
| Invoice items | Export detailed data for each individual line item within invoices. |

<Tip>
  Learn more about the content of each report [here](/admin/analytics-reports/reports).
</Tip>

To download a CSV report:

1. Go to the **Analytics** page \> click **Create report** top right.
2. Choose the necessary report type, time range, and payment status (e.g., filter to export only **Paid** invoices).
3. Press **Download**.
4. Upload or copy-paste invoice data into your accounting software.

![Image](/images/analytics/analytics-export-invoices-for-your-accounting-app-1.png)

## Download invoice PDFs

1. Go to **Analytics** \> press **Create report** top right.
2. Choose the **Invoices** report type, select the necessary time range, and payment status \> press **Download**.
3. Open the downloaded report in Google Sheets, Excel, or any other app that supports the CSV file format.
4. Locate the **PDF** column.

![Image](/images/analytics/analytics-export-invoices-for-your-accounting-app-2.png)

5. To download individual invoices, click on the URL in this column. The invoice PDF will be downloaded to your device.
6. To mass download all invoices, select a range of links in the **PDF** column. Then, click the **(...)** \> **View more cell actions** \> use the **Open links** feature (available in [Google Sheets](https://support.google.com/docs/thread/5466717/keyboard-shortcut-to-open-a-spreadsheet-hyperlink-to-a-new-tab-but-stay-in-current-sheet-alt-ent?hl=en) and other apps that handle hyperlinks). All selected links will open simultaneously, and all invoices will be downloaded.
7. Once exported, upload all invoice PDFs to your accounting software.
