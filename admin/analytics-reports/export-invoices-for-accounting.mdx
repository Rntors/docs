---
title: "Export invoices for accounting"
description: "Export invoices for your accounting workflow"
---

If an automated accounting integration does not meet your needs, export invoice data as CSV and download invoice PDFs for import into your accounting software.

## Export CSV reports

| Report type | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Invoices | Consolidated invoice data with totals per invoice |
| Invoice items | Detailed data for each invoice line item |

See [Reports](/admin/analytics-reports/reports) for the exact fields.

1. Go to **Analytics \> Create report**.
2. Select **Invoices** or **Invoice items**, the time range, and payment status.
3. Press **Download**.
4. Upload or copy the invoice data into your accounting software.

![Export invoice CSV](/images/analytics/export-invoices-csv.png)

## Download invoice PDFs

1. Go to **Analytics \> Create report**.
2. Choose **Invoices**, select the time range and status, then press **Download**.
3. Open the CSV in Google Sheets, Excel, or another compatible app.
4. Locate the **PDF** column.

![Invoice PDF column](/images/analytics/export-invoices-pdf-column.png)

5. Click a URL to download one invoice.
6. For a bulk download, select links in the PDF column and use your spreadsheet app's **Open links** action.
7. Upload the downloaded PDFs to your accounting software.
