title: "Track membership growth"
description: "Monitor membership growth over time"
---

Track customer-base growth and new subscription purchases with custom **Subscriptions** and **Users** reports.

## Track subscription purchases

1. Go to **Analytics** and press **Create report**.
2. Select the [Subscriptions report](/admin/analytics-reports/reports) and choose statuses.
3. Filter by **Create date** to count purchases or by **Start date** to count subscriptions that started in the period.

![Subscription growth report](/images/analytics/membership-subscriptions.png)

You can also use the **Start date** and **Create date** filters on **Billing \> Subscriptions**.

![Subscription filters](/images/analytics/membership-subscription-filters.png)

## Track customer base growth

1. Go to **Analytics** and press **Create report**.
2. Select **Users** and include members, nonmembers, or both.
3. Filter by **Create date** to count new customers during a period.

![Customer growth report](/images/analytics/membership-users.png)

Use these results to identify trends and improve your membership offering.
