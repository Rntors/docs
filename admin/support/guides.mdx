Location guides form a knowledge base that consolidates helpful information about your space for customers.

## Create a guide

1. Go to **Support \> Guides**.
2. Press **Create**, add a title and description, and use [rich text formatting](/admin/general/media-formats).
3. To add photos or videos, place the cursor in the description and paste an image or use the gallery icon to select media from your device. Review [supported media formats](/admin/general/media-formats).

![Create a guide](/images/support/guides-create.png)

4. In **Visibility**, select who can see the guide. See [visibility settings](/admin/location/visibility).
5. Press **Create**.

If any guide is **Public**, the Information page also becomes publicly visible.

## Where guides appear to users

Users can discover guides on the **Information** and **Support** pages.

![Guides on Information and Support](/images/support/guides-user-view.png)

The Search field lets users browse guides and quickly find answers. Guides are sorted alphabetically by title.

![Search guides](/images/support/guides-search.png)

## Recommended guides to create

- **How to find us** — detailed directions to your space.
- **How to become a member** — how to sign up for a plan or office.
- **Parking** — availability and instructions.
- **House rules** — room etiquette, kitchen rules, and noise regulations.
- **Refund policy** — rules and timelines for bookings, products, and events.

![Recommended guide topics](/images/support/guides-recommended.png)

## Edit a guide

1. Go to **Support \> Guides**.
2. Hover over the guide, press **(…)**, then **Edit**.
3. Make changes and press **Save**.

![Edit a guide](/images/support/guides-edit.png)

## Delete a guide

1. Go to **Support \> Guides**.
2. Hover over the guide, press **(…)**, select **Delete**, and confirm.

![Delete a guide](/images/support/guides-delete.png)

<Warning>
  Deleting a guide is irreversible and cannot be undone.
</Warning>
