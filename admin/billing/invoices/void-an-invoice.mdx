Delete a draft invoice or void an unpaid invoice you no longer need.

As an administrator, you can remove an invoice you no longer need. Which action is available depends on the invoice’s status: delete a [**Draft**](/admin/billing/invoices/invoice-statuses) invoice, or void an [**Unpaid**](/admin/billing/invoices/invoice-statuses) invoice that’s already been issued.

## Delete a draft invoice

You can delete a [**Draft**](/admin/billing/invoices/invoice-statuses) invoice—one created but not yet issued—at any time.

To delete a draft invoice:

1. Go to **Billing > Invoices**.
2. Locate the draft invoice you want to delete.
3. Tap the **(...)** menu in the top-right corner, click **Delete**, and confirm your choice.

<Warning>
  Deleting an invoice is irreversible. It is not possible to recover a deleted invoice.
</Warning>

## Void an unpaid invoice

Deleting an issued invoice isn’t possible, but you can void it instead. Voiding cancels the invoice and its payment requirement while keeping a record of it, rather than erasing the data.

<Info>
  Only invoices with the [**Unpaid**](/admin/billing/invoices/invoice-statuses) status can be voided. When an invoice payment has **Pending**, **Failed**, or **Processing** status, you can’t void it.
</Info>

To void an unpaid invoice:

1. Go to **Billing > Invoices**.
2. Locate the unpaid invoice you want to void.
