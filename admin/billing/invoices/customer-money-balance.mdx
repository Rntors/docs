Manage customer money balances to record prepayments, overpayments, or refunds to their next invoice.

The customer’s money balance provides an easy way to record the customer’s prepayments, over- and underpayments, refunds, or specific charges and automatically apply them towards their next invoice.

## Manage customer’s money balance

Administrators can view and manage the customer’s money balance in the customer’s profile.

There are two ways to make adjustments to the customer’s money balance.

### Automatic increase by issuing a credit note

- If you [**issue a credit note**](#replace-issue-credit-note-link) on an invoice with **Paid** status and choose **Customer balance** in the **Add credit option** property, it will automatically add the credit amount to the customer’s money balance, which will be applied to their next invoice.
- If you [**issue a credit note**](#replace-issue-credit-note-link) for an invoice that already has an applied balance, and the total credit amount *(credit note amount + previously applied balance)* becomes greater than the invoice’s total, the difference is automatically added to the customer’s **Money balance** to be applied to their next invoice.

### Manual adjustments by administrator

Administrators can also manually adjust the customer’s balance to add or deduct funds.

1. Navigate to the customer’s profile.
2. Next to the **Money balance**, click **Adjust balance** and select the adjustment type.
3. Enter the **Amount** to be added or deducted.
4. Type a reason in the **Internal note** field. This helps your administrator team track and recognize this adjustment later.

Money balance can be positive or negative.

| Balance | Positive (>0) | Negative (<0) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Meaning** | The customer has a credit with you. | The customer owes you money. |
| **Use when** | • A customer makes a prepayment or accidentally overpays an invoice *(e.g., via bank transfer).*<br /><br />• You need to apply a refund or discount to the customer’s next invoice for canceled services or service issues. | • A customer underpays an invoice.<br /><br />• You need to apply a penalty or a specific charge to the customer’s next invoice. |
| **Impact on next invoice** | Decreases the amount due on their next invoice. | Increases the amount due on their next invoice. |

<Info>
  Customers don’t receive notifications about adjustments made to their balance.
</Info>

## How money balance works

When the customer’s next invoice is issued, the money balance is automatically applied to its total post-tax amount due. It is reflected on the invoice as **Applied balance**.

{/* Replace with the RNTOR invoice applied balance screenshot */}

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/billing/customer-money-balance/applied-balance-invoice.png"
    alt="Customer money balance applied to an invoice in RNTOR"
  />
</Frame>

If the money balance is higher than the invoice total, the invoice will be fully covered, and the remaining balance will roll over to cover subsequent invoices.

<Warning>
  Applied balances on invoices do not currently sync to [**QuickBooks Online**](#replace-quickbooks-online-link) or [**Xero**](#replace-xero-link). To ensure your records are accurate, please adjust these invoices manually within your QuickBooks Online or Xero dashboard.
</Warning>

## Track balance adjustments

All changes to the customer’s money balance are recorded on the **Adjustments** page. To locate it, press the **(...)** menu button > **View adjustments**.

## How customers view their money balance

1. Customers go to the **Membership** page in the member web portal or mobile app.
2. In the personal or company section, open the **Billing** page.
3. Check **Money balance**.
