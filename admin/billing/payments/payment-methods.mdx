When making a booking or purchase, users can choose from different payment methods. The options available to administrators differ slightly because administrators can also use **Free** and **External** payments for specific scenarios.

| Payment method | Admin portal | Member portal |

| **External** | ✓ | |
| **Credits** | ✓ | ✓ (members only) |
| **Day passes** | ✓ | ✓ (members only) |
| **Invoice** | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Saved payment method** (card or bank account) | ✓ | ✓ |

## Free payment method

- **Free** is available only in the admin portal to administrators with permission to manage bookings.
- It lets an administrator book a resource for themselves or a customer without collecting money, credits, or day passes.

## External payment method

- [External](/admin/billing/payments/external) is available only to administrators booking on behalf of customers.
- Use it to record a payment collected outside RNTOR.

## Credits

- [Credits](/admin/billing/credits-and-day-passes/credits) appear for bookings, event tickets, and shop orders when:
  - Credits are enabled for the location.
  - The service has a credit price.
  - The member has personal or company credits.
- Administrators can also spend a member’s available credits while booking for them.

There are two credit balances:

- **Personal credits** belong to an individual member.
- **Company credits** may be used by company managers and permitted company members, subject to [company payment method permissions](/admin/customer-management/companies/company-payment-method-permissions).

## Day passes

- [Day passes](/admin/billing/credits-and-day-passes/day-passes) appear for eligible hot-desk bookings when:
  - Day passes are enabled for the location.
  - The selected desk accepts day passes.
  - The member has personal or company day passes.
- Administrators can also use a member’s available day passes while booking for them.

Day passes can be **personal** or **company** balances. Company-member access follows the company’s payment method permissions.

## Invoice

- **Invoice** adds the payment to an invoice instead of collecting it immediately.
- Customers can use it for eligible bookings and purchases only when [Pay with invoice](/admin/billing/payments/pay-with-invoice) is enabled.
- Administrators can select Invoice when booking for a customer.

The available invoice types are:

- **Personal invoice** for an individual customer.
- **Company invoice** for company managers and permitted company members.

## New payment method

- **New payment method** appears in the member portal when:
  - A [payment gateway](/admin/billing/payments/payment-gateway) is connected.
  - The resource or service has a monetary price.
- It lets the user enter new payment details for an immediate transaction.

## Saved payment method

### Saved credit card

- A saved card appears after the user has paid with it or added it manually, provided the connected gateway supports reusable payment methods.
- Administrators can use an eligible saved card to book for the customer, [pay an invoice](/admin/billing/invoices/record-invoice-payments), or [create a one-off payment](/admin/billing/payments/create-one-off-payments).

### Saved bank account

- A saved bank account can appear for invoice payments when the connected gateway supports bank debits and the customer has linked an account.
- Administrators can use an eligible saved bank account to charge the customer’s invoices.

## FAQs

<Accordion title="Which payment methods can an administrator use when booking for a customer?">
  For a **member**, the available methods can include **Free**, **External**, personal or company credits, personal or company day passes, personal or company invoice, and saved payment methods.

  For a **nonmember**, the available methods can include **Free**, **External**, **Personal invoice**, and saved payment methods. The exact list depends on permissions, balances, pricing, and connected integrations.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I let employees or staff book resources for free?">
  Add them as administrators and grant the permission required to manage bookings. When they create a room, desk, parking, or equipment booking in the admin portal, **Free** appears as a payment method.
</Accordion>
