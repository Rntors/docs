RNTOR generates receipts for successful online and external payments, including:

- One-off bookings, shop orders, and event tickets.
- Invoice payments.

Online-payment receipts are emailed to users and remain available in the member portal. Administrators can access receipts from the admin portal and export transaction data for accounting.

## What a receipt looks like

A receipt confirms a successful payment.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="full RNTOR receipt PDF." />
</Frame>

It can include:

- **Receipt number**
- **Invoice number**, when applicable
- **Date paid**
- Location logo, legal business name, and address
- Customer name, legal business name, and address
- Item description, quantity, and unit amount
- Tax rate and tax amount
- Discount
- Subtotal and total
- Surcharge
- Credit note
- **Amount paid**
- Refund date and refunded amount
- **Total refunded**
- **Payment method**
- Location footer

Tax presentation follows [tax settings](/admin/billing/taxes/set-up-taxes). Once created, a receipt is not changed; updates to billing details or item names affect future receipts only.

## How users get and access receipts

After an online payment, the user receives an email with the available invoice and receipt PDFs.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="email with invoice and receipt attachments." />
</Frame>

Users can also:

1. Go to **Membership > Billing > Transactions**.
2. Open the successful transaction.
3. Select **View receipt PDF** or **Download receipt PDF**.

  <img src="" alt="receipt actions in member billing." />
</Frame>

Alternatively, users can open a booking, ticket, or order from **Account** and download its receipt from **Payment**.

</Frame>

## How administrators access receipts

1. Go to **Billing > Transactions > Money**.
2. Find and open the transaction.
3. Select **Send receipt**, **View receipt PDF**, or **Download receipt PDF**.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="transaction receipt actions in admin portal." />
</Frame>

Administrators can also open the related invoice, booking, ticket, or order and use its **Payment** section.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="Payment section with receipt actions." />
</Frame>

Use [Reports](/admin/analytics-reports/reports) to export transactions for accounting.

## Receipts for external payments

When an administrator creates a paid [External](/admin/billing/payments/external) booking or [records an external invoice payment](/admin/billing/invoices/record-invoice-payments), RNTOR also generates a receipt. It is not emailed automatically merely because the external payment was recorded.

### Users

1. Go to **Membership > Billing > Transactions**.
2. Open the transaction.
3. Select **View receipt PDF** or **Download receipt PDF**.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="external transaction receipt in member portal." />
</Frame>

### Administrators

1. Go to **Billing > Transactions > Money**.
2. Open the transaction.
3. Send, view, download, or open the related invoice.
