RNTOR generates receipts for successful online and external payments, including:

- One-off purchases such as bookings, event tickets, and shop orders.
- Invoice payments.
- One-off payments created by administrators.

Customers receive receipts for online payments by email and can access them later from their billing history. Administrators can view, send, download, and export receipts.

## What a receipt contains

A receipt confirms a successful payment and can include:

- Receipt number
- Related invoice number
- Payment date
- Location logo, legal name, and address
- Customer name, legal name, and address
- Item description
- Quantity and unit amount
- Tax rate and tax amount
- Discounts
- Subtotal and total
- Surcharge, if applicable
- Credit note, if applicable
- Amount paid
- Refund date and refunded amount, if applicable
- Payment method
- Location footer information

Receipt amounts follow the location's tax configuration. Review [Taxes](/admin/billing/taxes/set-up-taxes) and [Billing details](/admin/customer-management/billing-details) to configure the information used for future documents.

<Note>
  A receipt is a record of the completed payment. Later changes to billing details, names, or service settings affect future receipts only.
</Note>

## Customer access

After an online payment, the customer receives an email with the available invoice and receipt PDFs.

Customers can also:

1. Go to **Membership > Billing > Transactions**.
2. Open the successful transaction.
3. Select **View receipt PDF** or **Download receipt PDF**.

For a specific purchase, they can open the related booking, ticket, or order and download the receipt from its **Payment** section.

## Administrator access

1. Go to **Billing > Transactions**.
2. Open the **Money** tab and find the customer or transaction.
3. Open the transaction.
4. Select **Send receipt**, **View receipt PDF**, or **Download receipt PDF**.

Administrators can also open the related invoice, booking, ticket, or order and use its **Payment** section.

Use [Reports](/admin/analytics-reports/reports) to export transaction records for accounting.

## Receipts for external payments

When an administrator marks a booking or invoice as paid externally, RNTOR still creates a receipt.

External receipts are not automatically emailed when the external payment is merely recorded. The customer can access the receipt from their billing history, and an administrator can send it manually from the transaction.

Learn more about [External payments](/admin/billing/payments/external).
