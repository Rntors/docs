---
title: "Pay with invoice"
description: "Let eligible customers add bookings and purchases to a personal or company invoice instead of paying immediately"
---

**Pay with invoice** lets customers complete eligible bookings and purchases without immediate payment. RNTOR saves the charges as invoice items and bills them later.

For companies, team purchases can be consolidated into a company invoice.

## Enable Pay with invoice

1. Go to **Customers** and open a member, nonmember, or company profile.
2. Find **Payment methods** in the profile panel.
3. Open the menu beside **Personal invoice** or **Company invoice** and select **Edit**.
4. Choose which services can be paid by invoice.
5. For a company, choose who can use the company invoice for each service:
   - **None** — invoice payment is unavailable.
   - **Company managers** — only company managers can use it.
   - **Company members** — company managers and members can use it.
6. Save the changes.

Pay with invoice is disabled by default. Company permissions can also affect which members may use the company payment method.

## Configure how invoice payments are billed

For customers without a subscription, payments by invoice remain [pending invoice items](/admin/billing/invoices/pending-invoice-items) until an administrator invoices them.

For a customer with a subscription:

1. Open the customer profile.
2. Open the subscription.
3. Configure **Add to subscription invoices**.

### Add to subscription invoices

When enabled, eligible payments are consolidated into subscription invoices.

- Purchases and bookings in the current billing period can appear on the upcoming subscription invoice.
- Future-dated bookings are assigned according to their booking date and the subscription invoicing schedule.
- The invoice combines subscription items and eligible additional purchases.

Review the result in [Upcoming invoices](/admin/billing/invoices/upcoming-invoices).

### Bill separately

When **Add to subscription invoices** is disabled, payments remain pending invoice items until an administrator creates a separate invoice.

Use this option when subscription charges and one-off purchases must appear on different invoices.

## How customers pay with invoice

1. The customer selects an eligible booking, ticket, shop product, or package.
2. At checkout, they select **Personal invoice** or **Company invoice**.
3. RNTOR confirms the purchase according to its rules and creates the related invoice item.
4. The item is either added to an upcoming subscription invoice or kept as pending, based on the customer's configuration.

## Book for a customer with Invoice

Administrators can select a customer's available personal or company invoice when creating a booking on their behalf.

The customer's invoice-payment permissions still determine whether a personal or company invoice is appropriate.

## Manage pending invoice items

1. Go to **Billing > Invoices > Pending invoice items**.
2. Filter by customer if needed.
3. Select the items to bill.
4. Create the invoice.
5. Review or [edit the invoice](/admin/billing/invoices/edit-invoices).
6. Select **Issue** to send it for payment, or **Issue and charge** when an eligible saved payment method is available.

Learn more in [Pending invoice items](/admin/billing/invoices/pending-invoice-items).
