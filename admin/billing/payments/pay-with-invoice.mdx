---
title: "Pay with invoice"
description: "Enable customers to include purchases in personal or company invoices instead of paying immediately"
---

**Pay with invoice** lets customers complete eligible bookings and purchases without paying immediately. Charges are collected and billed later. For companies, team expenses can be consolidated into one company invoice.

## Enable Pay with invoice

1. Go to **Customers > Members** or **Nonmembers**.
2. Open a user or company profile and find **Payment methods**.
3. Press **(…) > Edit** beside **Personal invoice** or **Company invoice**.
4. For an individual, choose the services that can be paid by personal invoice.
5. For a company, choose who can use the company invoice for each service:
   - **None**
   - **Company managers**
   - **Company members**

</Frame>

Pay with invoice is disabled by default. Company managers may later manage permissions that RNTOR allows them to control.

## Configure how payments with invoice are billed

Payments by nonmembers and members without subscriptions become [pending invoice items](/admin/billing/invoices/pending-invoice-items). For a member with subscriptions:

1. Open the customer profile.
2. Open the relevant subscription.
3. Manage **Add to subscription invoices**.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="subscription billing settings." />
</Frame>

If the customer has multiple subscriptions, enable the switch only on the subscription that should collect these purchases, or disable it everywhere to bill them separately.

### Add to subscription invoices

When enabled:

- Purchases and bookings within the current subscription period are added to the [upcoming invoice](/admin/billing/invoices/upcoming-invoices).
- Future bookings are assigned to the appropriate future subscription invoice.
- The resulting invoice combines subscription items and eligible extra purchases.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="subscription invoice with additional purchases." />
</Frame>

### Bill separately

When disabled, payments remain as **Pending invoice items** until an administrator creates a separate invoice. They are not invoiced automatically.

</Frame>

## How Pay with invoice works

1. The customer chooses the service.
2. At checkout, they select **Personal invoice** or **Company invoice**.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="personal and company invoice payment methods." />
</Frame>

Only customers for whom this method is enabled can see and use it.

## Book for customers with the Invoice payment method

1. Go to **Resources > Rooms, Desks, Parking**, or **Equipment**.
2. Select the resource and complete the booking details.
3. Find the customer in **Booker**.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="Booker dropdown." />
</Frame>

4. For a new customer, select **New nonmember** and enter their details.
5. Under **Payment**, select **Personal invoice** or **Company invoice**, then book.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="booking modal with Invoice selected." />
</Frame>

6. If **Add to subscription invoices** is enabled, the booking is assigned automatically.
7. Otherwise, it becomes a pending invoice item.

### Manage pending invoice items

1. Open the customer profile and add any required [billing details](/admin/customer-management/billing-details).
2. Find **Pending invoice items**.
3. Press **Create invoice**.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="Create invoice button below pending items." />
</Frame>

4. [Edit the draft](/admin/billing/invoices/edit-invoices) if needed.
5. Press **Issue**, or **Issue and charge** when a saved method is available.

<Info>
  Pending items accumulated for the customer are issued together. Review the full set before creating the invoice.
</Info>

## FAQs

<Accordion title="Can a user book multiple dates with one payment?">
  Users cannot select several dates as one checkout. Enable **Pay with invoice** so multiple bookings accumulate on a consolidated invoice, or create the bookings for the customer and choose **Invoice** each time.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can an administrator purchase an event ticket or package for a customer?">
  Administrators can create resource bookings on a customer’s behalf. Availability of admin-created event tickets or packages depends on the corresponding RNTOR workflow.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I book and issue an invoice for a nonmember company?">
  Create the company manager as an individual nonmember, enter the company’s billing details in that profile, then create the booking and select **Personal invoice**. The invoice uses the billing details saved for that nonmember.
</Accordion>
