title: "Create one-off payments"
description: "Quickly charge custom one-off payments or record external ad-hoc transactions"
---

When you need to charge outside standard checkout and recurring subscription invoices, you can:

- [Create and issue a one-off invoice](/admin/billing/invoices/issue-invoices) for the customer to pay later.
- Create a one-off payment to charge a saved method immediately or record money already collected externally.

## Create a one-off payment

1. Go to **Billing > Transactions** and press **Create payment**.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="Create payment button on Billing > Transactions." />
</Frame>

2. Select the customer.
3. Enter:
   - **Description**
   - **Quantity**
   - **Unit price**
   - **Currency**, including an [alternative currency](/admin/billing/currencies) when applicable
   - **Tax rate**, including a [custom tax rate](/admin/billing/taxes/custom-tax-rates) when applicable
   - **Payment method** — an eligible saved method or [External](/admin/billing/payments/external)
4. Enter required [billing details](/admin/customer-management/billing-details).
5. Confirm the payment.

## How creating one-off payments works

After confirmation:

1. RNTOR creates the transaction in the customer profile and creates its invoice.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="customer transaction and related invoice." />
</Frame>

- For a saved method, RNTOR initiates the charge and displays whether it succeeds or fails.
- For **External**, RNTOR records an externally collected payment.
2. Analytics and reports identify the transaction as a **Custom** payment.

## Best practices

A one-off payment is useful when:

1. A disputed payment must be charged again.
2. An urgent service or last-minute upgrade requires immediate payment.
3. You need to record a small ad-hoc fee or supplemental service not covered by normal invoicing.
