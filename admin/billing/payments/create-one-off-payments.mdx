---
title: "Create one-off payments"
description: "Charge a customer for an ad hoc product or service without creating a booking, order, or subscription"
---

Create a one-off payment when you need to charge a customer for an item that is not represented by a booking, event ticket, shop order, package, or subscription.

Examples include printing, damages, replacement access cards, additional services, and other ad hoc charges.

## Create a one-off payment

1. Go to **Customers**.
2. Open the customer profile.
3. Open the actions menu and select **Create payment**.
4. Enter a description of the product or service.
5. Enter the quantity, unit price, tax, and discount if applicable.
6. Choose the payment method:
   - A saved payment method to charge the customer online.
   - **External** when payment was collected outside RNTOR.
   - **Invoice** when the charge should be billed later.
7. Review the total and confirm.

The available methods depend on the customer's payment settings and your connected [payment gateway](/admin/billing/payments/payment-gateway).

## Charge a saved payment method

Select an eligible saved card or bank account to charge the customer immediately.

After a successful charge:

- RNTOR records a paid transaction.
- An invoice and [receipt](/admin/billing/payments/receipts) are generated.
- The customer can access the documents from their billing history.

If the payment fails, review the transaction status and the gateway response before retrying.

## Record an external one-off payment

Choose **External** when you already collected the amount outside RNTOR. RNTOR records the transaction and generates the related invoice and receipt without processing money through the connected gateway.

## Add the charge to an invoice

Choose the customer's personal or company invoice when the amount should be paid later.

Depending on the customer's configuration, the item is either:

- Added to an upcoming subscription invoice, or
- Saved under [Pending invoice items](/admin/billing/invoices/pending-invoice-items).

See [Pay with invoice](/admin/billing/payments/pay-with-invoice) for configuration details.

## Review one-off payments

Administrators can review the transaction from **Billing > Transactions** or the customer's profile. Use [Reports](/admin/analytics-reports/reports) to export one-off payment data for reconciliation.
