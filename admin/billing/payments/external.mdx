Use **External** when you create a booking for a customer and collect payment outside RNTOR, such as cash, bank transfer, or an external terminal.

The member portal is intended for self-service booking, so consider promoting self-served bookings whenever administrator assistance is unnecessary.

## Make bookings with the External payment method

External is available only to administrators for room, desk, parking, and equipment bookings.

1. Go to **Resources** and choose **Rooms**, **Desks**, **Parking**, or **Equipment**.
2. Select the resource and booking time.
3. Find the customer in **Booker**.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="Booker field in the booking modal." />
</Frame>

4. If the user has no profile, select **New nonmember**, enter their details, and create the profile.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="New nonmember form." />
</Frame>

5. Complete the booking details.
6. Select an eligible coupon if needed.
7. Choose **External** and set the status:
   - **Paid** — the customer has already paid outside RNTOR.
   - **Pending** — the customer has reserved the resource but has not paid.
8. Add required [billing details](/admin/customer-management/billing-details).
9. Confirm the booking.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="External payment selection with Paid and Pending statuses." />
</Frame>

### Understand paid external bookings

When you select **Paid**, RNTOR treats the payment as completed:

- The booking is confirmed and secured on the calendar.
- RNTOR immediately generates an [invoice](/admin/billing/invoices/track-and-manage-invoices) and [receipt](/admin/billing/payments/receipts).
- The customer can see the booking in their account.

### Manage pending external bookings

A **Pending** external booking remains tentative while the resource is held.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="tentative booking displayed in the calendar." />
</Frame>

After the customer pays—or if payment will not be received—open the booking and change **Payment > Status** to **Paid** or **Canceled**.

<Frame>
  <img src="" alt="booking payment status control." />
</Frame>

- **Paid:** RNTOR confirms the booking and generates the invoice and receipt.
- **Canceled:** RNTOR cancels the transaction and booking and releases the reserved time.

## How external bookings reflect in Analytics

External bookings are included in the relevant invoices, payments, rooms, desks, equipment, and parking analytics. Related [reports](/admin/analytics-reports/reports) identify the payment type as **External** and the payment status as **Paid** or **Pending**.
