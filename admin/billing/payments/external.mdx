---
title: "External"
description: "Record payments collected outside RNTOR and manage paid or pending external bookings"
---

Use the **External** payment method when an administrator creates a booking for a customer and the payment is collected outside RNTOR, such as by cash, bank transfer, or an external card terminal.

The External payment method is available only to administrators for room, desk, parking, and equipment bookings.

## Create a booking with External

1. Go to **Resources** and open the relevant Rooms, Desks, Parking, or Equipment page.
2. Select the resource and booking time.
3. Select the customer in **Booker**. Create a nonmember profile first if necessary.
4. Complete the booking details and apply a coupon if applicable.
5. Select **External** as the payment method.
6. Choose the payment status:
   - **Paid** — payment has already been collected outside RNTOR.
   - **Pending** — the resource is reserved while payment is still outstanding.
7. Complete any required [billing details](/admin/customer-management/billing-details).
8. Confirm the booking.

## Paid external bookings

When the status is **Paid**:

- The booking is confirmed and secured on the calendar.
- RNTOR generates an invoice and [receipt](/admin/billing/payments/receipts).
- The customer can view the booking and its payment documents in their account.
- The transaction is included in billing and analytics.

## Pending external bookings

When the status is **Pending**:

- The booking remains tentative while you wait for payment.
- The transaction remains pending until an administrator changes it.
- The held time appears on the relevant resource schedule.

After collecting payment:

1. Open the booking.
2. In **Payment**, change the status to **Paid**.
3. RNTOR confirms the booking and generates the invoice and receipt.

If the customer does not pay, change the status to **Canceled**. RNTOR cancels the transaction and removes the booking from the resource calendar.

## External payments in analytics

External bookings appear in the relevant booking, invoice, and transaction analytics. Use the [Reports](/admin/analytics-reports/reports) page to export detailed records and filter by payment method or status.
