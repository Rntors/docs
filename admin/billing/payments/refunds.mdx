RNTOR supports automatic and administrator-initiated refunds according to the payment method and connected gateway.

| Payment method | Automatic refund | Admin-initiated in RNTOR |
| --- | :---: | :---: |
| **Credits** | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Day passes** | ✓ | ✓ |
| **External** | — | ✓ |
| **Supported gateway payment** | If enabled | ✓ |
| **Other gateways** | — | Use the gateway dashboard |

## Configure automatic refunds

1. Open **Integrations** for the location.
2. Open the connected payment gateway.
3. Enable **Automatic refunds**, when supported.

<Frame caption="Image placeholder — Automatic refunds">
  **Image placeholder:** gateway Automatic refunds switch.
</Frame>

4. Configure:
   - **Rescheduling policy** for rooms, desks, parking, and equipment.
   - **Refund policy** for events and shop products.

### Set rescheduling policy

1. Go to **Resources** and add or edit a resource.
2. Under **Rescheduling policy**, select:
   - **Before the start**
   - A **specific time** before the booking
   - **No refund**

<Frame caption="Image placeholder — resource rescheduling policy">
  **Image placeholder:** resource rescheduling policy options.
</Frame>

Customers see the policy before purchase and when canceling.

### Set refund policy

For an event, open **Engagement > Events**, add or edit the event, and configure **Refund policy**.

  **Image placeholder:** event refund policy.
</Frame>

For a shop product, open **Service > Shop**, add or edit the product, and choose the applicable refund rule.

## Issue refunds manually via RNTOR

Administrators can issue eligible full or partial refunds to override a policy, refund a past purchase, or refund an invoice.

For a booking, event ticket, or shop order:

1. Open the purchase from the customer profile or its module.
2. Open **Payment > Refund**.
3. Enter the amount and confirm.

For an invoice:

1. Go to **Billing > Invoices** and open the invoice.
2. Open the payment and select **Refund**.
3. Enter the amount and confirm.

## Issue refunds via the payment gateway dashboard

If the gateway cannot initiate refunds from RNTOR:

1. Find the transaction under **Billing > Transactions**.
2. Copy its gateway reference.
3. Locate it in the gateway dashboard.
4. Issue the refund there.
5. Confirm whether the gateway synchronizes the result back to RNTOR.

## See refunded transactions

### Monetary transactions

Open **Billing > Transactions > Money** and filter by customer. A completed refund appears as **Refunded** or **Partially refunded**, and the receipt shows the refunded amount.

### Credit and day pass transactions

Open the corresponding **Credits** or **Day passes** transaction tab. The refund appears as a dedicated transaction and restores the eligible balance.

## Tips

- Consider refunding with [credits](/admin/billing/credits-and-day-passes/credits) when appropriate.
- Offer a [coupon or promo code](/admin/billing/discounts/coupons-and-promo-codes) to encourage a future purchase.

## FAQs

<Accordion title="How can I check whether a customer will receive a refund after cancellation?">
  Customers see the applicable policy during purchase and again before they confirm cancellation. After cancellation, administrators can check **Billing > Transactions** and open the Money, Credits, or Day passes tab. Users can check the corresponding transaction section under **Membership**.

  Credit and day-pass returns are normally immediate. Monetary refund timing depends on the gateway and the customer’s bank.
</Accordion>
