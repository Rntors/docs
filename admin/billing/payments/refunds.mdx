---
title: "Refunds"
description: "Configure automatic refunds, issue full or partial refunds, and track refunded transactions"
---

Refund behavior depends on the service policy, transaction type, and connected payment gateway.

RNTOR can return money, credits, or day passes when an eligible booking or purchase is canceled. Administrators can also issue full or partial monetary refunds manually.

## Configure automatic refunds

Where supported by the connected gateway:

1. Go to **Integrations** and open the connected payment gateway.
2. Enable **Automatic refunds**.
3. Configure the cancellation, rescheduling, and refund conditions for the relevant resources and services.
4. Save the settings.

Automatic refunds apply only when the cancellation meets the configured policy. Gateway support may vary.

## Issue a refund manually in RNTOR

Administrators can issue a refund as an exception to the automatic policy, for a past purchase, or for an invoice that customers cannot cancel themselves.

### Booking, ticket, or shop order

1. Open the customer's booking, ticket, or order from their profile or the relevant module.
2. To cancel it, select **Cancel** and confirm whether to refund.
3. To refund without canceling, open **Payment**, select **Refund**, enter the amount, and confirm.

### Invoice payment

1. Go to **Billing > Invoices**.
2. Open the invoice and its **Payment** section.
3. Open the payment menu and select **Refund**.
4. Enter the amount and confirm.

You can also open the payment from **Billing > Transactions** and select **Refund**.

Partial refunds are available for eligible monetary transactions. Credit and day-pass adjustments are recorded as separate refund transactions.

## Refund through the gateway dashboard

If a gateway does not support initiating refunds from RNTOR:

1. Find the transaction in **Billing > Transactions**.
2. Open its receipt and copy the transaction or receipt reference.
3. Find the payment in the gateway dashboard.
4. Issue the refund there.
5. Confirm whether the gateway automatically synchronizes the refund back to RNTOR.

For Stripe configuration and troubleshooting, see [Stripe](/admin/integrations/payments/stripe/stripe).

## Track refunded transactions

A monetary transaction displays **Refunded** or **Partially refunded** after RNTOR records the refund. The receipt is updated with the refunded amount.

- Customers can review refunds from their billing transactions.
- Administrators can review them under **Billing > Transactions**.
- [Reports](/admin/analytics-reports/reports) include refund amounts for reconciliation.

Credit and day-pass refunds create dedicated transactions so the original use and the returned balance remain visible.

<Note>
  The payment gateway completes the transfer of funds. Processing time and final success depend on the gateway and the customer's bank.
</Note>
