PCI DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard) is a global standard for protecting credit and debit card data. It does not govern ACH, SEPA, or other non-card payment methods.

RNTOR uses connected third-party payment processors to collect and process sensitive payment details. The exact PCI DSS scope and validation requirements depend on the gateway, your transaction volume, and how your organization handles payment data.

## RNTOR and payment card data

RNTOR does not expose full card numbers or CVV/CVC values to location administrators. Sensitive account data is entered through and managed by the third-party payment processor you connect.

RNTOR may retain limited non-sensitive references returned by the processor, such as:

- Card brand
- Last four digits of the Primary Account Number
- Expiration month and year
- Processor payment-method reference

These details let users recognize a saved method without revealing the full card number.

<Warning>
  Do not state that your organization or RNTOR has a specific PCI certification level unless you have the current supporting assessment or attestation.
</Warning>

## Determine your PCI compliance level

Your responsibilities can depend on:

- Annual card transaction volume
- Card-present versus card-not-present transactions
- The connected payment processor
- Whether staff ever receive or handle card data outside the processor’s secure flow
- Other terminals, websites, or systems used by your organization

Follow the current compliance instructions from your connected processor and consult a qualified security assessor when necessary. See the [PCI Security Standards Council](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/) for official standards and resources.

## Practical compliance steps

- Limit billing and customer-data access to essential personnel.
- Use named administrator accounts rather than shared logins.
- Remove administrator access immediately when a person leaves the organization.
- Do not use administrator accounts on public or shared devices.
- Use strong authentication for RNTOR and the payment processor.
- Never place full card numbers or CVV/CVC values in notes, support tickets, custom fields, or messages.
- Document payment-data retention and incident-response procedures.
- Include relevant payment handling and retention practices in your terms and privacy policy.
- Complete any self-assessment questionnaire required by your payment processor.
