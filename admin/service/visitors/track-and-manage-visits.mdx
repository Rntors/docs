---
title: "Track and manage visits"
description: "Track and manage all visits, monitor visit statuses and analytics"
---

Once the **Visitors** module and the Reception app are [set up](/admin/service/visitors/set-up-visitors), members can create and manage visits on the **Visitors** page, while administrators can track all visits there.

## How members manage visits

### Create visits

To create visits, members [preregister visitors or approve visit requests](/member/visitors/preregister-and-approve-visit-requests) on the **Visitors** page.

### View visits

* Members can view all their past and upcoming visits and pending visit requests on the **Visitors** page.

![Track and manage visits image 1](/images/service-visitors-track-and-manage-visits-1.png)

* They can click on a specific visit to check its details, contact the visitor, or [register this visitor again](/member/visitors/preregister-and-approve-visit-requests).

![Track and manage visits image 2](/images/service-visitors-track-and-manage-visits-2.png)

### Edit visits

To update or cancel a visit, members follow these steps:

1. Go to the **Visitors** page and open the necessary visit.
2. Click the menu **(...)** top right > **Edit** to change the visit date or time.
3. Or press **Cancel** at the bottom of the visit page to cancel the visit.

![Track and manage visits image 3](/images/service-visitors-track-and-manage-visits-3.png)

<Info>
  The visitor is notified by email if the visit is changed or canceled.
</Info>

## How administrators manage visits

### Create visits

Administrators can [create visit requests](/member/visitors/check-in-and-check-out) on behalf of visitors upon their arrival, if assistance is needed, or if the visitor cannot self-register.

### Track all visits

* Administrators can see all the visits, visit requests, and contacts on the **Service** > **Visitors** page in RNTOR. Each visit shows the visitor's name, host's name, visit date, and visit status at a glance:
  * Empty circle - visitor hasn't checked in.
  * Green circle - visitor has checked in.
  * Purple circle - visitor has checked in and checked out.

![Track and manage visits image 4](/images/service-visitors-track-and-manage-visits-4.png)

* Filter by **Date** to see visits scheduled on, before, after, or between specific dates.
* Filter by **Create date** to see visits created on, before, after, or between specific dates. On the **Requests** tab, you can filter visit requests by **Create date** the same way.
* Click on any visit to view more details, such as the visit code, check-in/out time, visit duration, or to contact the host or visitor.

![Track and manage visits image 5](/images/service-visitors-track-and-manage-visits-5.png)

* Administrators can check the visit statistics on the **Analytics** page > **Visitors** dashboard.

![Track and manage visits image 6](/images/service-visitors-track-and-manage-visits-6.png)

* For a comprehensive overview of all visits in the selected time range, including the visit date and time, check-in and check-out time, visit durations, and the visitor's and host's contact information, generate the [**Visitors** custom report](/admin/analytics-reports/reports) on the **Analytics** page.

### Edit visits

If necessary, administrators can edit visits by following these steps:

1. Go to the **Visitors** page and open the necessary visit.
2. Click on the menu **(...)** top right > **Edit** to change the visit date or time or add an internal note.

<Info>
  Internal notes are only visible to your team of administrators.
</Info>
