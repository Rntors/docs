The Visitors add-on provides access to the **Visitors** module and **Reception** app.

It is designed to register and track your members' visitors, streamline visitor check-in and check-out at your front desk, instantly notify hosts about their visitors' arrival, and collect contact details of walk-ins — all without a receptionist's involvement.

This creates a professional first impression for visitors in your space and reduces your front-desk workload.

## Who are visitors

A visitor is anyone a member invites to your space — typically for meetings or personal visits. We recommend setting a policy in your space that requires members to register all their visitors for better tracking and a smoother arrival experience.

Common cases:

- Guests attending a meeting with a member.
- Friends or family coming to see a member.

<Warning>
  Reception is only for checking in members' visitors — guests who come to see a member for free, without a booking. It is not designed for members, or customers and tenants who have a booking or subscription, to check in.
</Warning>

## Prerequisities

1. Have a supported tablet device:
   - A minimum screen size of 7 inches.
   - Support Google Play (for Android) or the App Store (for iPadOS).
   - Android 8.0 or above, iOS/iPadOS 15.1 or later
2. If you haven't yet, [sign up for the Visitors add-on](/admin/administration/billing/make-changes-to-rntor-subscription) from **Network settings** \> **Billing**.

## Set up the Visitors module

1. Press the menu **(...)** near the location name \> go to **Location settings** \> **Service** \> **Visitors** tab.
2. Enable the switch and [manage the visibility](/admin/location/visibility) of the **Visitors** page.
3. **Host search** — configure how unregistered visitors can find their host on the Reception tablet:
   - Full name (mandatory)
   - Email address
   - Phone number
   - Company name (if the host is a member of any company)
4. **Check out** — enable if you want visitors to check out and track visit durations.
5. **Leave contacts** — enable if you want to use the Reception app for [collecting contacts of walk-ins](/admin/service/visitors/collect-contacts-of-walk-ins-with-reception), too.
6. **Invitation** — enable if you want to [customize the visit invitation email](/admin/general/customize-emails) that visitors receive.

![Visitors image 1](/images/service-visitors-1.png)

Once enabled, your members will discover the **Visitors** page and can start [registering their visitors and approving visit requests](/member/visitors/preregister-and-approve-visit-requests).

## Set up the Reception app

### Log in to and set up Reception

1. Download the Reception app to your tablet by following these links:
   - Google Play: [https://app.rntor.com](https://app.rntor.com)
   - App Store: [https://app.rntor.com](https://app.rntor.com)
2. Open the app and press **Log in**.

![Visitors image 2](/images/service-visitors-2.png)

3. Log in with the account of an administrator for the location where you want to enable Reception.
4. Select your network and relevant location.

### Customize Reception

The Reception app automatically reflects your network's branding to create a consistent and professional experience. The following branded elements are displayed:

- The member web portal logo
- The member web portal name
- Accent color
- The last [cover photo](/admin/location/information) of the location where the Reception app is used

<Info>
  If no cover photo is uploaded for the location, background color applies instead.
</Info>

To customize the appearance of the Reception app, you need to update your network settings.

1. To change the name, logo, or accent color, the [owner](/admin/administration/administrators/owner) needs to go to **Network settings** \> **General** \> **Information** and **Branding** tabs.

<Info>
  Changes will apply to the member web portal and/or mobile app as well.
</Info>

2. To change the background of the Reception app, as an administrator, [upload a new cover photo](/admin/location/information) for the location where the Reception app is used.

## How Visitors solution works

- **Preregistration of visitors and express check-in**: Members can [preregister their visitors](/member/visitors/preregister-and-approve-visit-requests) via the member web portal or mobile app. Visitors receive an invitation with a visit code by email, with which they [check in](/member/visitors/check-in-and-check-out) on the Reception app upon arrival.
- **Self-service registration**: Unregistered visitors can register themselves on the tablet and [request a visit](/member/visitors/check-in-and-check-out). This prevents reception bottlenecks and allows members to approve or decline the visit from their own device.
- **Automated host notifications**: When a visitor checks in or requests a visit, the host member receives an immediate push notification or email, ensuring they meet their guest promptly without a receptionist's manual coordination.
- **Visitor check-out and duration tracking**: Visitors can [check out](/member/visitors/check-in-and-check-out) through the Reception app, allowing you to track visit durations and enforce visitor limits.
- **Lead generation for walk-ins**: Apart from visitors, people interested in your space can leave their details using the [**Leave contacts**](/admin/service/visitors/collect-contacts-of-walk-ins-with-reception) feature on the Reception app. This helps you grow your lead base without relying on separate tools or manual follow-ups.
- **Custom branded experience**: The Reception app [reflects your brand](/admin/service/visitors/set-up-visitors#customize-reception) by displaying your logo and location photo, delivering a professional experience from the moment a visitor arrives.

## Troubleshoot issues with access to Reception app

If you can't enable or access the Reception app, here are the most common reasons and how to fix them:

<Accordion title="1. Poor Internet connection">
  Try reconnecting your tablet to Wi-Fi or switching to a different network.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="2. Logging in with the wrong email address">
  Make sure you're logging in as an [administrator](/admin/administration/administrators/administrators) for the location where you want to use Reception. Only administrators can choose a network and location when logging in.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="3. Outdated Reception app version">
  Ensure you've installed the latest version of the Reception app from the [Google Play Store](https://app.rntor.com) or [App Store](https://app.rntor.com).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="4. Your RNTOR subscription is not active or doesn't include the Visitors add-on">
  [Check your subscription](/admin/administration/billing/rntor-subscription) status and the availability of the [Visitors add-on](/admin/service/visitors/set-up-visitors).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="5. Device doesn't meet technical requirements">
  Ensure your tablet meets the [requirements](/admin/service/visitors/set-up-visitors) for installing the Reception app.
</Accordion>
