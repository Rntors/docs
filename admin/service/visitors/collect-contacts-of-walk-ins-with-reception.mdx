Once the **Visitors** module and the Reception app are [set up](/admin/service/visitors/set-up-visitors), you can collect contact information from all walk-ins coming to your space with the help of Reception app. This helps grow your lead and marketing base without missing valuable connections. You can later convert these walk-ins into nonmembers or members in just one click.

## Who are contacts

Walk-ins are people who visit your space without a prior booking or registration — often to:

* Request a tour
* Learn more about your pricing or membership plans
* Explore your space

It would be a pity to miss collecting their contact information to reach out later. Reception helps you collect their contact details on the spot through the **Leave contacts** feature.

<Tip>
  If a person already has a booking or is coming to a member as a visitor, they don't need to leave their contacts — they're already registered through the member portal or mobile app.
</Tip>

## Enable leaving contacts

To enable the **Leave contacts** feature, do the following:

1. Go to RNTOR > click the **(...)** menu next to the location name on the left-side panel > **Location settings.**
2. Go to the **Visitors** module and [enable the **Leave contacts** switch.](/admin/service/visitors/set-up-visitors)
3. [Enable the Reception app](/admin/service/visitors/set-up-visitors) on your tablet.
4. The **Leave contacts** button will appear by default on the screen.

## How users leave contacts

1. A walk-in person taps the **Leave contacts** button on the Reception app.
2. They enter their contact information and click **Submit**.

## View and export all contacts

As an administrator, you can view and manage all collected contacts:

* Go to **Service** > **Visitors** on the left-side panel > open the **Contacts** tab.

![Contacts image 4](/images/service-visitors-collect-contacts-of-walk-ins-with-reception-4.png)

* Filter contacts by **create date** to see contacts created on, before, after, or between specific dates.
* Click a certain contact to invite them as a member or add them as a nonmember, if you convert them and need to create a booking or assign a subscription.

![Contacts image 5](/images/service-visitors-collect-contacts-of-walk-ins-with-reception-5.png)

* To export contacts, go to **Analytics** > generate the **Visitors** [custom report](/admin/analytics-reports/reports) > choose the **Contacts** filter.
