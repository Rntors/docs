Connect RNTOR to ChatGPT to retrieve and analyze your coworking and flexible workspace data in conversation.

After you authorize a network, ChatGPT can use RNTOR's read-only tools to answer operational questions without changing your RNTOR data.

## Connect RNTOR in ChatGPT

### 1. Open RNTOR in ChatGPT

In ChatGPT, open **Settings → Plugins** and find RNTOR.

</Frame>

### 2. Install the plugin

Select **Install plugin**, then choose **Connect** when ChatGPT asks you to authorize RNTOR.

<Frame caption="Upload a screenshot showing the Install plugin button for RNTOR.">
  **Screenshot placeholder**
</Frame>

### 3. Authorize your network

Sign in to RNTOR, select the network you want ChatGPT to access, and choose **Allow**.

### 4. Start asking

Open a ChatGPT conversation, select RNTOR when needed, and ask questions in plain language.

## What you can ask ChatGPT

- **Network and locations:** “List our locations and their addresses.”
- **Customers:** “Which members do not have an active subscription?”
- **Plans and subscriptions:** “Which subscriptions renew this month?”
- **Resources and bookings:** “What is booked in the Boardroom this week?”
- **Invoices and contracts:** “List unpaid invoices due this week.”
- **Shop and orders:** “What shop orders were placed today?”
- **Benefits and applications:** “Which benefit applications are waiting for review?”
- **Guides:** “Show me the published Wi-Fi guide.”

See the full list on the [RNTOR MCP overview](https://docs.rntor.com/docs/mcp-server#supported-tools).

You can also:

- Combine categories in one request, such as reviewing a customer, their subscription, and recent invoices together.
- Use RNTOR results alongside other ChatGPT plugins available in the same conversation.

## Disconnect RNTOR from ChatGPT

Disconnecting removes ChatGPT's access to your RNTOR network.

1. Open **Settings → Plugins** in ChatGPT.
2. Find RNTOR in your installed plugins.
3. Select **Uninstall** or **Disconnect**.

You can reconnect at any time. You can also use RNTOR with [Claude](https://docs.rntor.com/docs/mcp-server/claude) or another compatible assistant through the [RNTOR MCP overview](https://docs.rntor.com/docs/mcp-server).
