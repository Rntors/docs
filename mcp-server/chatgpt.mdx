Connect RNTOR to ChatGPT and access your coworking and flexible workspace information directly from a conversation.

Once installed and authorized, ChatGPT can look up members, subscriptions, invoices, bookings, and more without opening the RNTOR dashboard.

## Connect RNTOR in ChatGPT

### 1. Open the RNTOR plugin

Open **Settings → Plugins** in ChatGPT and search for RNTOR.

### 2. Install the plugin

Select **Install plugin**, then select **Connect** when prompted.

<Frame caption="Upload a screenshot showing the Install plugin button for RNTOR.">
  **Screenshot placeholder**
</Frame>

### 3. Authorize your network

Sign in to RNTOR, choose the network you want ChatGPT to access, and select **Allow**.

### 4. Start asking

Open a ChatGPT conversation and ask for the RNTOR information you need.

## What you can ask ChatGPT

Ask ChatGPT about:

- **Network and locations**
- **Members and companies**
- **Plans and subscriptions**
- **Resources and bookings**
- **Invoices and contracts**
- **Shop products and orders**
- **Benefits and applications**
- **Guides**

See every available tool and example on the [RNTOR MCP overview](https://docs.rntor.com/docs/mcp-server#supported-tools).

ChatGPT can also:

- Combine several categories in one request, such as a member's subscription, recent invoices, and bookings.
- Pass findings to other plugins, such as drafting reminder emails for overdue invoices.

## Disconnect RNTOR from ChatGPT

1. Open **Settings → Plugins** in ChatGPT.
2. Find RNTOR under your installed plugins.
3. Select **Disconnect** or **Uninstall**.

You can reconnect at any time.
