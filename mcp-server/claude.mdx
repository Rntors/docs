Connect RNTOR to Claude to give it access to the RNTOR networks permitted by your account and get answers directly in your Claude conversation.

RNTOR uses a remote MCP server with OAuth sign-in. Add it to Claude as a custom connector, authorize your RNTOR account, and then ask about your account, networks, locations, resources, and recent bookings without opening the RNTOR admin dashboard.

## Connect RNTOR in Claude

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your connector settings">
    In Claude, go to **Settings → Connectors** and choose to add a custom connector.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the RNTOR connector">
    Enter **RNTOR** as the connector name and use this server URL:

    ```text
    https://iwdewzswethsnvwvpind.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Authorize your network">
    When prompted, sign in to RNTOR and authorize access to your network. RNTOR follows your existing roles, permissions, and network scope.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start asking">
    Enable the RNTOR connector in your Claude conversation. Then describe the information you need in plain language.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can ask Claude

Ask Claude about any of the following:

- Your connected RNTOR account
- Networks and locations
- Resources and recent bookings

For the complete list of available tools and example prompts, see [Supported tools](/mcp-server#supported-tools).

Claude can also combine the available information in one request. For example, ask it to find your accessible locations and then list the meeting-room bookings for one of them.

## Disconnect RNTOR from Claude

You can disconnect RNTOR from Claude at any time to end its access.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your connectors">
    In Claude, go to **Settings → Connectors**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find RNTOR">
    Locate RNTOR in your connected or custom connectors.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Disconnect">
    Disconnect the RNTOR connector to end its access to your RNTOR network.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Claude connector is powered by the RNTOR MCP server. You can also [connect RNTOR to ChatGPT or another MCP-compatible assistant](/mcp-server#connect-in-another-ai-assistant).
