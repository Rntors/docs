RNTOR is available as a published **Community connector** in the Claude Directory. Connect it to ask Claude about your authorized workspace operations without leaving the conversation.

<Card title="Open RNTOR in the Claude Directory" icon="sparkles" horizontal href="https://claude.ai/directory/rntor">
  View the public listing and connect RNTOR for yourself or your Claude organization.
</Card>

## Connect RNTOR in Claude

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the RNTOR directory listing">
    Go to [claude.ai/directory/rntor](https://claude.ai/directory/rntor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to connect">
    Select **Connect** or **Connect for your team**, depending on your Claude plan and organization permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to RNTOR">
    Claude starts the RNTOR OAuth flow. Sign in with an active RNTOR account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a network">
    Choose the RNTOR network you want this connection to access. The selection is required and is limited to networks available to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and allow">
    Review the read-only categories, then choose **Allow**. Claude returns to the conversation after authorization succeeds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  RNTOR is currently a Community connector. Community connectors undergo Anthropic’s automated directory review but are not marked as verified by Anthropic.
</Note>

## Connect manually

If your organization cannot yet see the directory listing, add RNTOR as a custom connector:

1. Open **Settings → Connectors** in Claude.
2. Add a custom connector named **RNTOR**.
3. Enter:

```text
https://mcp.rntor.com/mcp
```

4. Keep **Always required** authentication and automatic OAuth client registration selected when Claude detects them.
5. Connect, sign in, select a network, and allow read-only access.

## Authorization behavior

- The consent page requires an explicit network.
- OAuth connections are fail-closed: without an active network binding, network tools return no data.
- The selected network and environment remain enforced across refreshed access tokens.
- Cross-network identifiers, internal locations, and live/test mismatches are denied safely.
- Claude receives only the fields exposed by the read-only tool schemas.

## Disconnect or change networks

To end access, open **Settings → Connectors**, find RNTOR, and disconnect it.

To authorize another network, disconnect and reconnect RNTOR, then choose the new network during consent.

## Troubleshooting

### I do not see a network to select

Confirm that your RNTOR user has an active membership in at least one live network. Platform administrators can select from eligible live networks.

### I returned to Claude without connecting

Restart the connection and complete the RNTOR consent screen. Do not reuse an expired authorization page.

### A record is not found

The record may belong to another network, a different environment, or an internal location. RNTOR intentionally returns the same safe response for inaccessible and nonexistent records.

### I need help

Contact [support@rntor.com](mailto:support@rntor.com) and include the time of the connection attempt. Never send access tokens or authorization codes.
