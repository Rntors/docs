Connect RNTOR to Claude to retrieve and analyze your coworking and flexible workspace data in conversation.

RNTOR is available in the Claude connector directory. After you authorize a network, Claude can use RNTOR's read-only tools without changing your RNTOR data.

## Connect RNTOR in Claude

### 1. Open the RNTOR connector

Open [RNTOR in the Claude connector directory](https://claude.ai/directory/rntor), or go to **Settings → Connectors** and search for RNTOR.

### 2. Connect RNTOR

Select **Connect** or **Connect for your team**, depending on your Claude workspace.

</Frame>

### 3. Authorize your network

Sign in to RNTOR, select the network you want Claude to access, and choose **Allow**.

### 4. Start asking

Open a Claude conversation and ask questions about your workspace in plain language.

## What you can ask Claude

- **Network and locations:** “List our locations and their addresses.”
- **Customers:** “Which members do not have an active subscription?”
- **Plans and subscriptions:** “Which subscriptions renew this month?”
- **Resources and bookings:** “What is booked in the Boardroom this week?”
- **Invoices and contracts:** “List unpaid invoices due this week.”
- **Shop and orders:** “What shop orders were placed today?”
- **Benefits and applications:** “Which benefit applications are waiting for review?”
- **Guides:** “Show me the published Wi-Fi guide.”

See the full list on the [RNTOR MCP overview](https://docs.rntor.com/docs/mcp-server#supported-tools).

You can also:

- Combine categories in one request, such as reviewing a customer, their subscription, and recent invoices together.
- Use RNTOR results alongside other connectors available in the same Claude conversation.

## Disconnect RNTOR from Claude

Disconnecting removes Claude's access to your RNTOR network.

1. Open **Settings → Connectors** in Claude.
2. Find RNTOR in your connected services.
3. Select **Disconnect**.

You can reconnect at any time. You can also use RNTOR with [ChatGPT](https://docs.rntor.com/docs/mcp-server/chatgpt) or another compatible assistant through the [RNTOR MCP overview](https://docs.rntor.com/docs/mcp-server).
