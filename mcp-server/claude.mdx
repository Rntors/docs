Connect RNTOR to Claude so you can look up coworking and flexible workspace information directly in your conversations.

RNTOR is listed in Claude's connector directory. After you authorize a network, Claude can use RNTOR's read-only tools without changing your RNTOR data.

## Connect RNTOR in Claude

### 1. Open the RNTOR connector

Open [RNTOR in the Claude connector directory](https://claude.ai/directory/rntor), or search for RNTOR under **Settings → Connectors**.

</Frame>

### 2. Connect RNTOR

Select **Connect** or **Connect for your team**, depending on your Claude workspace.

<Frame caption="Upload a screenshot showing the Connect button for RNTOR.">
  **Screenshot placeholder**
</Frame>

### 3. Authorize your network

Sign in to RNTOR, choose the network you want Claude to access, and select **Allow**.

### 4. Start asking

Open a Claude conversation and ask for the RNTOR information you need in plain language.

## What you can ask Claude

- **Network & locations:** “List our locations and their addresses.”
- **Members & companies:** “List all active companies.”
- **Plans & subscriptions:** “Which subscriptions renew this month?”
- **Resources & bookings:** “What is booked in the Boardroom this week?”
- **Invoices & contracts:** “List unpaid invoices due this week.”
- **Shop & orders:** “What shop orders were placed today?”
- **Benefits & applications:** “Which benefit applications are waiting for review?”
- **Guides:** “Open our published Wi-Fi guide.”

See every available tool and example on the [RNTOR MCP overview](https://docs.rntor.com/docs/mcp-server#supported-tools).

## Disconnect RNTOR from Claude

1. Open **Settings → Connectors** in Claude.
2. Find RNTOR in your connected services.
3. Select **Disconnect**.

You can reconnect at any time. To use RNTOR with another assistant, see [ChatGPT](https://docs.rntor.com/docs/mcp-server/chatgpt) or the [RNTOR MCP overview](https://docs.rntor.com/docs/mcp-server).
