Connect RNTOR to Claude to give it access to your RNTOR network, and get answers and information instantly by asking in the Claude conversation.

Once connected, Claude can access your RNTOR network, so you can ask about your members, subscriptions, invoices, and more — right in the conversation, without opening the RNTOR admin dashboard.

## Connect RNTOR in Claude

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your connectors">
    In Claude, go to **Settings → Connectors** and choose to add a custom connector.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add RNTOR">
    Enter **RNTOR** as the connector name and use this server URL:

    ```text
    https://iwdewzswethsnvwvpind.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Authorize your network">
    When prompted, sign in and authorize access to your RNTOR network.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start asking">
    Claude can now access your RNTOR network. Just name what you’re looking for in plain language, and Claude will answer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can ask Claude

Ask about any of the following, in plain language, and Claude finds the answer for you:

- Network and locations
- Members and companies
- Plans and subscriptions
- Resources and bookings
- Invoices and contracts
- Shop products and orders
- Benefits and applications
- Guides

For the full list of tools Claude can use, see [Supported tools](/mcp-server#supported-tools).

Claude can also:

- Combine these in a single request — for example, ask for a member’s subscription, recent invoices, and bookings together, and Claude pulls up all three.
- Pass what it finds to your other connectors — for example, draft reminder emails for overdue invoices.

## Disconnect RNTOR from Claude

You can disconnect RNTOR from Claude at any time to end its access.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your connectors">
    In Claude, go to **Settings → Connectors**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find RNTOR">
    Locate RNTOR in your list of connectors.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Disconnect">
    Disconnect the connector to immediately end its access to your RNTOR network.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Claude connector is powered by the RNTOR MCP server. You can also [connect RNTOR to ChatGPT or any other MCP-compatible assistant](/mcp-server#connect-in-another-ai-assistant) manually.
