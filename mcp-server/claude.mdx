Connect RNTOR to Claude and access your coworking and flexible workspace information directly from a conversation.

Once connected and authorized, Claude can look up members, subscriptions, invoices, bookings, and more without opening the RNTOR dashboard.

## Connect RNTOR in Claude

### 1. Open the RNTOR connector

Open [RNTOR in the Claude connector directory](https://claude.ai/directory/rntor), or search for RNTOR under **Settings → Connectors**.

<Frame caption="RNTOR in the Claude connector directory">
  ![RNTOR in the Claude connector directory](/images/mcp/claude/rntor-connector-directory.png)
</Frame>

### 2. Connect RNTOR

Select **Connect** or **Connect for your team**, depending on your Claude workspace.

<Frame caption="Connect RNTOR to Claude">
  ![RNTOR connector details and Connect to Claude button](/images/mcp/claude/connect-rntor-to-claude.png)
</Frame>

### 3. Authorize your network

Sign in to RNTOR, choose the network you want Claude to access, and select **Allow**.

### 4. Start asking

Open a Claude conversation and ask for the RNTOR information you need.

<Frame caption="Ask Claude about unpaid invoices in RNTOR">
  ![A Claude conversation listing unpaid invoices from RNTOR](/images/mcp/claude/rntor-unpaid-invoices.png)
</Frame>

## What you can ask Claude

Ask Claude about:

- **Network and locations**
- **Members and companies**
- **Plans and subscriptions**
- **Resources and bookings**
- **Invoices and contracts**
- **Shop products and orders**
- **Benefits and applications**
- **Guides**

See every available tool and example on the [RNTOR MCP overview](https://docs.rntor.com/docs/mcp-server#supported-tools).

Claude can also:

- Combine several categories in one request, such as a member's subscription, recent invoices, and bookings.
- Pass findings to other connectors, such as drafting reminder emails for overdue invoices.

## Disconnect RNTOR from Claude

1. Open **Settings → Connectors** in Claude.
2. Find RNTOR in your connected services.
3. Select **Disconnect**.

You can reconnect at any time.
