We’ve created this start guide to help you set up your location and build on the basics. Explore how to save time and provide excellent service to members with RNTOR.

## Set up network

**Total time: 10 minutes**

You’ll need the following:

- Your business’s brand materials, including a square logo and brand colors.
- Access to your domain name’s control panel to set up a [custom domain](/admin/general/member-web-portal/custom-domain-for-your-member-web-portal).
- Your privacy policy and terms of use text.
- A description for the member web portal.

Steps to set up your network:

1. Log in as an [owner](/admin/administration/administrators/owner) to RNTOR.
2. On the left-side panel, click **(…)** next to your network name, then select **Network settings \> General**.

![Image](/images/getting-started-set-up-network-1.png)

3. Set up a [custom domain](/admin/general/member-web-portal/custom-domain-for-your-member-web-portal), an email footer, [customize the look of your member web portal](/admin/general/member-web-portal/customize-member-web-portal), and add your policies.

<Warning>
  Since you process personal data, applicable laws and app publishing platforms may require you to disclose your privacy practices and prohibit questionable content and behavior. Add a [privacy policy](/admin/general/privacy-policy) and [terms of use](/admin/general/terms-of-use), which appear in the member web portal and mobile app.
</Warning>

## Set up locations

**Total time: 15 minutes**

You’ll need the following:

- Basic location information such as name, address, description, contacts, and a photo or video.
- Location guides, for example parking, pets, and printing instructions.
- Your business’s billing and bank details.
- Your local sales or VAT tax rate.

Steps to set up the locations:

1. The owner needs to [add all locations](/admin/administration/locations).
2. As an [administrator](/admin/administration/administrators/administrators), select the location on the left-side panel, click **(…)** next to it, and choose **Location settings**.
3. Configure the general location settings, [billing](/admin/billing/set-up-billing), and enable the modules you need.
4. Click **(…)** next to the location, select **Integrations**, and connect integrations such as a [payment gateway](/admin/integrations/payments/overview).

![Image](/images/getting-started-set-up-locations-1.png)

## Set up resources

**Total time: 1 hour**

You’ll need the following:

- Cover photos for rooms, desks, offices, and any other services you provide.
- Prices for your resources.

Steps to set up the resources:

1. Go to **Resources** and select the necessary page.

![Image](/images/getting-started-set-up-resources-1.png)

- [Rooms](/admin/resource-management/resources/set-up-resources#rooms)
- [Desks](/admin/resource-management/resources/set-up-resources#desks)
- [Offices](/admin/resource-management/resources/set-up-resources#offices)
- [Parking](/admin/resource-management/resources/set-up-resources#parking)
- [Equipment](/admin/resource-management/resources/set-up-resources#equipment)

2. Create your resources and configure their availability, visibility, pricing, and booking or rental rules.

## Set up plans & contracts

**Total time: 30 minutes**

You’ll need the following:

- A list of recurring membership plans with titles, prices, and descriptions.
- Contract templates for individual agreements with your customers.

Steps to automate invoicing and subscriptions:

1. Go to **Billing \> Plans** and [create plans](/admin/billing/plans/set-up-plans).
2. [Add credit and day pass packages](/admin/billing/credits-and-day-passes/packages) and [discounts](/admin/billing/discounts/coupons-and-promo-codes) if necessary.
3. [Upload contract templates](/admin/billing/contracts/templates).

![Image](/images/getting-started-set-up-plans-and-contracts-1.png)

4. Click **Member web portal** on the left-side panel to check how your services appear to members.

## Invite members

**Total time: 5 minutes per account**

You’ll need a spreadsheet with members’ names, emails, and information about their subscriptions.

Steps to invite members:

1. [Create companies](/admin/customer-management/companies/set-up-companies).
2. [Invite members](/admin/customer-management/users/members) using a mass invitation file.
3. [Create subscriptions](/admin/billing/subscriptions/set-up-subscriptions).
4. Introduce what the new member experience can do via Feed, newsletters, and by [promoting the member web portal and mobile app](/guides/promotion/promote-member-web-portal-and-mobile-app) on social media and your marketing website.
