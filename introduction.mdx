RNTOR helps you deliver superior member service with automated operations, built-in AI, and a native mobile app, while making it easy for members to sign up, book, pay, and get support.

## Platform

RNTOR is designed as a dual-sided ecosystem: a command center for operators to manage the space, and a self-service portal for members to book your services.

- [**Admin portal**](/admin/overview) — the backend platform for managing your space. It connects members, administrators, data, and infrastructure, powering billing, bookings, access, community, and operations from one source of truth.
- [**Member portal**](/member/overview) — the branded frontend platform for members to use your services. Here, they browse and book inventory, pay for services, and connect with the broader community from any browser or supported mobile experience.
