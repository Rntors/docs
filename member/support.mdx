The **Support** page is designed to help users get answers to their questions, report issues, or share feedback. Users can search for answers in the guides or create a support ticket on this page.

To let users use the **Support** page, make sure to complete [the prerequisites](/admin/support/tickets).

## Search guides

If there are [guides](/admin/support/guides) created in the location and users are eligible to see them according to the guides' [**Visibility** settings](/admin/location/visibility), users can see the **Guides** section at the top of the **Support** page.

![Search guides on RNTOR coworking space management platform](/images/member-support-1.png)

This section displays the preview of the 4 most popular guides and includes a **Search guides** field where users can find an answer by typing keywords related to their question.

![Search guides on RNTOR coworking space management platform](/images/member-support-2.png)

This allows users to quickly resolve their questions without needing to contact administrators.

## Create support ticket

The **Tickets** section on the **Support** page includes the **Create ticket** button and a list of previously created support tickets by or for this user.

![Create support ticket on RNTOR coworking space management platform](/images/member-support-3.png)

If users cannot find an answer to their question in the guides or they want to report a problem or provide feedback, they can create a support ticket.

1. To create a ticket, users tap **Create ticket** button and fill in the ticket details:

![Create support ticket on RNTOR coworking space management platform](/images/member-support-4.png)

1. **Type** — indicates what the requester's question or issue is related to.
2. **User** — visible only to administrators and allows to select the user for whom they want to submit a ticket.
3. **Description** — any valuable details to describe the question or issue.
4. Press **Submit**.
5. After creating the support ticket, users can attach photos, videos, or files in the ticket comments if needed.

![Image](/images/member-support-5.png)

4. Administrators receive a notification about a new ticket and [manage it](/admin/support/tickets). Users get [notifications](/member/account/notifications) about the comments or updates to their ticket.
