## Create equipment booking

To book equipment, users go to **Locations** \> **Equipment** and select the following details:

![Image](/images/member-bookings-book-equipment-1.png)

- **Equipment** — what they want to book.
- **Date** — the necessary rental date.
- **Quantity/Items** — the number of units needed.
- **Time** — the desired time slot (if equipment is available for hourly booking).

<Info>
  Users and administrators can book any available current or future slot, but only administrators can log past bookings through the admin web portal.
</Info>

- **Repeat** — available only if the equipment is completely free. Choose one of the suggested recurrence patterns or select **Custom** to set your own recurrence pattern.
- **End repeat** — choose:
  - **On date** — to set a specific final booking date, or
  - **Never** — to set an open-ended recurrence.
  - For **Custom** recurrences, users can also choose **After number of occurrences** to schedule a specific number of recurring bookings.

<Info>
  Users can create recurring bookings only for completely free equipment. If the equipment booking is paid, administrators can create a [recurring booking](/admin/resource-management/resources/manage-bookings#create-a-recurring-booking) for the customer and charge them separately.
</Info>

![Image](/images/member-bookings-book-equipment-2.png)

<Tip>
  You can set up equipment for hourly or full-day rentals by [configuring Min time, Max time, and Availability increment.](/admin/resource-management/resources/set-up-resources#equipment)
</Tip>

In the booking form, users choose:

- **Booker** — the user for whom the booking is made. Booker selection is available only to [administrators](/admin/administration/administrators/administrators) or [company managers](/admin/customer-management/companies/company-members-and-managers) who can create bookings for their [company members](/admin/customer-management/companies/company-members-and-managers).

<Info>
  Company managers can make equipment bookings for other company members either with company credits or a company invoice.
</Info>

<Info>
  When you book for another user, you choose whether to notify them about the booking.
</Info>

- **Memo** — a private note visible only to the user and administrators.
- [**Promo code**](/admin/billing/discounts/coupons-and-promo-codes#create-promo-codes) or [**coupon**](/admin/billing/discounts/coupons-and-promo-codes) — allows a user to redeem a discount on the equipment booking if eligible.
- **Payment**. [Understand payment methods](/admin/billing/payments/payment-methods). The total price for a booking is calculated based on the [price tiers](/admin/resource-management/resources/set-up-pricing) added for this equipment, factoring in any applicable discount from the user's subscription or entered promo code.
- **Legal business name, Address,** and **Tax ID.** These fields appear depending on the [**Require billing details** setting.](/admin/customer-management/billing-details)

![Image](/images/member-bookings-book-equipment-3.png)

Once a booking is made, users can add it to their Google Calendar or Microsoft 365 Outlook.

![Image](/images/member-bookings-book-equipment-4.png)

On the member mobile app, the **Add to calendar** button opens the default calendar on the user's device.

<Tip>
  If you offer long-term rentals (weekly, monthly, etc.) of this equipment, you can [link the relevant plans](/admin/billing/plans/immediate-plan-sign-ups) to subscribe directly to it.
</Tip>

## View equipment bookings

- Users can see their equipment bookings on the **Account** \> **Bookings** page.

![Image](/images/member-bookings-book-equipment-5.png)

- Users can also find their bookings on the **Equipment** page \> **Bookings** tab.

![Image](/images/member-bookings-book-equipment-6.png)

- Upcoming bookings appear for them as a reminder on the banner on the **Locations** page.
- Users receive notifications-reminders before the booking starts and ends. [Understand users' notifications](/member/account/notifications)

## Manage equipment booking

To manage equipment booking, users follow these steps:

1. Open the booking \> press **Edit** to make changes. [Edit a booking](/admin/resource-management/resources/manage-bookings#edit-a-booking)
2. Press **Cancel** to cancel a booking.
3. Press **Add to calendar** to add the booking to your Google Calendar.

![Image](/images/member-bookings-book-equipment-7.png)

**FAQs**

<Accordion title="How is equipment ordered?">
  Equipment assigned to the customer appears first, followed by equipment with available items, and then the rest is sorted alphabetically by title and by creation date from oldest to newest.
</Accordion>
