---

Reporting and blocking users is important for maintaining a safe and respectful community within your space. By following these steps, users can help keep themselves and others protected from harmful or inappropriate behavior.

## Report a user

Users can report others to the administrator for any offensive behavior in the following way:

1. Open the other user's profile from **Chats**, **Feed**, or any other page.
2. Click the menu **(...)** in the top right corner and select **Report**.

![Image](/images/member-account-privacy-1.png)

3. Confirm your report by clicking **OK** in the dialogue window.

### How reporting a user works

- Administrators receive a [notification](/admin/administration/your-account/notifications/set-up-notifications) and can proceed with managing this report individually.
- The reported user isn't notified about any action against them.
- Reporting the user does not restrict this user's activity in any form until they are blocked, removed from **Customers,** or [added to the blacklist](/admin/general/users).

## Block your profile to a user

Users can block their profile to certain other users to hide their activity and personal information from them in the following way:

1. Open the other user's profile from **Chats**, **Feed**, or any other page.
2. Click the menu **(...)** in the top right corner and select `Block.`
3. Confirm clicking **OK** in the dialogue window.

### How blocking your profile to a user works

- The blocked user cannot see your name and personal details on the **Community** page, contact you in chat, or see your booking details.
- To the blocked member, your profile in member web portal or mobile apps will appear as **Unavailable user**.

<Info>
  Reporting and blocking features are available to users in member web portal or mobile app. Administrators, on the other hand, can [blacklist users](/admin/general/users) from the admin portal.
</Info>
