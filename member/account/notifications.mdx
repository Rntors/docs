User notifications inform users about updates, reminders, and actions related to their account. They help users stay engaged, up-to-date, and prompted to act when needed.

## How users receive and manage notifications

### Push notifications

Users receive all notifications as push notifications by default, except member invitations, confirmation emails, and emails to visitors.

How users allow push notifications:

- [From member mobile app](/admin/administration/your-account/notifications/set-up-notifications#push-notifications)
- [From member web portal](/admin/administration/your-account/notifications/set-up-notifications#push-notifications)

### Email notifications

There are mandatory and optional email notifications.

- Mandatory emails are always delivered and cannot be disabled.
- Users can manage the optional ones via their **Account** page **\> Notifications**.

![Image](/images/member-account-notifications-1.png)

If enabled, users receive them via both email and push notifications. If disabled, they receive them only as push notifications.

## Invoice-related user notifications

Invoice notifications ensure timely payments, confirm transactions, remind users of upcoming charges, and alert them to failed payments.

| Event | Notification | Sent as push | Sent via email |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Upcoming subscription auto-invoice or auto-pay (3 days before renewal) | <ul><li>If a customer has a default payment method: `This is a friendly reminder that your invoice will become ready for payment on [Date]. Your [payment method] will be charged at that time. If you’d prefer to use a different payment method, go to Membership > Billing, add a new one, and set it as default.`</li><li>If a customer doesn't have a default payment method: `This is a friendly reminder that your invoice will become ready for payment on [Date].`</li><li>Includes an option to open and preview the invoice and update their default payment method.</li></ul> | _X_ | Mandatory<br />✓ |
| Invoice issued | <ul><li>`Your invoice is ready for payment`.</li><li>Provides an option to open the invoice from the notification and pay, includes the invoice details, and a PDF attached.</li></ul> | ✓ | Mandatory<br />✓ |
| Invoice paid (auto-charged, charged by administraror, or paid manually by user) | <ul><li>​`Your invoice payment to [Location] is processing.` and<br />​`We have successfully processed payment to [Location], and you may find your receipt attached.`</li><li>Includes the invoice details and invoice and receipt PDFs attached.</li></ul> | _X_ | Mandatory<br />✓ |
| Invoice auto-charge failed | <ul><li>​`Invoice payment was unsuccessful. Please provide a valid payment method.`</li><li>Includes an option to open the invoice from the notification and pay with an alternative payment method.</li></ul> | ✓ | Mandatory<br />✓ |
| Invoice resent to user by an administrator | <ul><li>​`This is a friendly reminder that your invoice is ready for payment.`</li><li>Provides an option to open the invoice from the notification and pay, includes the invoice details, and a PDF attached.</li></ul> | _X_ | Mandatory<br />✓ |
| Automatic invoice payment retires and reminders (if enabled) | [<u>Understand payment retries and reminders</u>](/admin/billing/invoices/payment-retries-and-reminders) | _X_ | If set up by an administrator;<br />By default enabled <br />✓ |
| Invoice voided | <ul><li>​`Your invoice is voided.`</li><li>Provides an option to open the invoice from the notification, includes the invoice details, and a PDF attached.</li></ul> | ✓ | ✓ |
