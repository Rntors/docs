Connect your AI assistant to your RNTOR network with the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and get answers and information instantly by asking AI in plain language.

RNTOR MCP connects your RNTOR data directly to your AI assistant — like Claude or ChatGPT — using the [Model Context Protocol (MCP)](https://modelcontextprotocol.io), an open standard for linking AI assistants to other tools and platforms. Once connected, your AI assistant has access to your RNTOR network’s data, so you can ask about it right inside your AI chat and get answers instantly.

Instead of navigating the admin dashboard yourself, just ask in plain language and your AI assistant finds what you need. It can even use what it finds together with your other connected apps — for example, to draft a follow-up email.

## What you can do with RNTOR MCP

Once connected, your AI assistant can look up data from across your RNTOR network. Ask about any of the following in plain language, and it finds the answer for you:

- **Network & locations** — look up your network and the details of every location you operate.
- **Members & companies** — look up members and companies in your network, along with their full membership and contact details.
- **Plans & subscriptions** — look up member subscriptions and the membership plans you offer.
- **Resources & bookings** — look up resources and booking details.
- **Invoices & contracts** — look up invoices, contracts, and contract templates.
- **Shop & orders** — look up shop products and orders.
- **Benefits & applications** — look up the benefits and applications.
- **Guides** — look up your location guides.

## Connect RNTOR MCP

Before you connect, make sure you have an active RNTOR network. There are no API keys, hosting, or technical configuration to set up.

### Connect in Claude

Add RNTOR as a custom connector in Claude:

1. In Claude, open **Settings → Connectors** and choose to add a custom connector.
2. Enter **RNTOR** as the connector name and use the RNTOR MCP server URL:

```text
https://iwdewzswethsnvwvpind.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp
```

3. When prompted, sign in and authorize access to your RNTOR network.

[See the complete Claude setup guide](/mcp-server/claude).

### Connect in another AI assistant

For ChatGPT, or another AI assistant, add RNTOR as a custom connector:

1. In your AI client, open the connectors settings and choose to add a custom connector.
2. Enter the RNTOR server URL:

```text
https://iwdewzswethsnvwvpind.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp
```

3. When prompted, authorize access to your RNTOR network.

You can connect RNTOR to any MCP-compatible assistant.

Once connected, your AI assistant can access your RNTOR network’s data, and you can start asking. Just name what you’re looking for in plain language, and your AI assistant finds the right information for you.

## Supported tools

These are the tools your AI assistant can use to look up information across your RNTOR network. They work together, so your single request can span members, subscriptions, bookings, invoices, and more.

### Network & locations

| Tool | Description | Example prompts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `get_current_user` | See the signed-in RNTOR user and the account context available to them | “Which RNTOR account am I connected with?” |
| `list_merchants` | See the RNTOR networks available to the signed-in user | “What’s the name of our coworking network?” |
| `list_locations` | See all your locations, with their addresses, contact details, and other key information | “List our locations and their addresses” |
| `get_location` | Get full details about one location | “Show the address and contact details for our Downtown location” |

### Customers

| `get_customer` | Get full details about one member or company, including their contact, membership, and subscription details | “Pull up Jane Doe’s profile, subscription, and recent invoices” |

RNTOR’s tools are read-only — they retrieve and summarize information from your network but can’t change any data in your RNTOR account. Support for write actions may come in a future update. We’re continuously adding new data types and capabilities to the RNTOR MCP connector.

## Security

### Access

- You authorize the connection yourself — there are no API keys to manage in your AI client.
- You can disconnect RNTOR from your AI client at any time to end its access.
- Your AI assistant only reaches the network you authorize, based on your RNTOR role and permissions.

### Data

- RNTOR’s tools are read-only, so nothing in your account can be changed.
- Your AI assistant sees the same data your RNTOR account can access — nothing more.
- Nothing is shared beyond the AI client you connect, and any data you bring into it is handled under that client’s own privacy policy and terms.

If you have questions about RNTOR MCP, reach out to our team at [support@rntor.com](mailto:support@rntor.com).

## FAQs

### Is there a limit to how much I can ask?

RNTOR MCP is designed for normal conversational use. If a request is temporarily rate-limited with a `429` response, prompt your AI tool to reduce parallel operations, space out sequential requests, and try again after a brief pause.
