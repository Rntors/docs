Connect your AI assistant to your RNTOR network with the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and get answers instantly by asking in plain language.

RNTOR MCP connects your authorized RNTOR data directly to AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT using the [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io), an open standard for connecting AI assistants to external tools and platforms. Once connected, your AI assistant can access the RNTOR data permitted by your account and answer questions inside your conversation.

Instead of navigating the admin dashboard to find information, describe what you need in plain language. Your AI assistant can find the relevant RNTOR data and use it together with other connected apps, such as drafting a follow-up message from booking information.

## What you can do with RNTOR MCP

Once connected, your AI assistant can look up information from across the RNTOR networks you are allowed to access:

- **Account** — identify the connected RNTOR user and available account context.
- **Networks and locations** — list accessible networks and their locations.
- **Resources and bookings** — look up rooms, desks, offices, parking spaces, other resources, and recent bookings.

For example, you can ask:

- “Which RNTOR account am I connected with?”
- “List all live networks I can access.”
- “Show the locations in my network.”
- “List all meeting rooms at Lake View.”
- “What room bookings are scheduled this week?”

## Connect RNTOR MCP

Before you connect, make sure you have an active RNTOR account with access to at least one network. There are no API keys, hosting, or technical configuration to set up.

### Connect in Claude

Add RNTOR as a custom connector in Claude:

1. In Claude, open **Settings → Connectors** and choose to add a custom connector.
2. Enter the RNTOR MCP server URL shown below.
3. When prompted, sign in to RNTOR and authorize access to your network.

[See the complete Claude setup guide](/mcp-server/claude).

### Connect in another AI assistant

For ChatGPT or another MCP-compatible AI assistant, add RNTOR as a custom connector:

1. Open the connector settings in your AI assistant and choose to add a custom connector.
2. Enter the RNTOR MCP server URL:

```text
https://iwdewzswethsnvwvpind.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp
```

3. When prompted, sign in to RNTOR and authorize access to your network.

Once connected, enable the RNTOR connector in your conversation and ask for the information you need in plain language.

## Supported tools

These read-only tools work together to retrieve information from the RNTOR networks your account can access.

### Account

| Tool | Description | Example prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `get_current_user` | See the signed-in RNTOR user's identity and accessible account context. | “Which RNTOR account am I connected with?” |

### Networks and locations

| Tool | Description | Example prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `list_merchants` | List the active RNTOR networks the signed-in user can access, including live or test environment context. | “List all live networks I can access.” |
| `list_locations` | List locations inside an accessible network. | “Show the locations in my network.” |

### Resources and bookings

| Tool | Description | Example prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `list_resources` | List accessible resources and their resource types, optionally filtered by location or resource type. | “List all meeting rooms at Lake View.” |
| `list_recent_bookings` | List recent accessible bookings, optionally filtered by location, resource type, or date range. | “What room bookings are scheduled this week?” |

RNTOR MCP tools are read-only. They retrieve information but cannot create, update, or delete anything in your RNTOR account. Additional data types and capabilities may be added in future updates.

## Security

### Access

- You authorize the connection yourself through RNTOR sign-in, so there is no API key to manage in your AI client.
- You can disconnect RNTOR from your AI assistant at any time to end its access.
- Every request follows the signed-in user's RNTOR roles, permissions, network scope, and live or test environment boundaries.

### Data

- RNTOR MCP tools are read-only, so nothing in your RNTOR account can be changed.
- Your AI assistant only receives data your RNTOR account is already allowed to access.
- Data sent to the AI assistant is handled under that assistant's privacy policy and terms.

## FAQs

### Do I need an API key?

No. Sign in to RNTOR and authorize the connection when your AI assistant prompts you.

### Can RNTOR MCP change my account data?

No. The currently supported tools are read-only and cannot create, update, or delete RNTOR data.

<Note>
  If RNTOR adds, removes, or renames MCP tools, refresh the connector in your AI assistant to load the latest tool list.
</Note>
