Connect your AI assistant to RNTOR and get answers and operational information in everyday language.

RNTOR MCP connects your RNTOR data directly to compatible AI assistants through the open [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/). Once connected, you can ask questions about your workspace operations without switching between screens or building reports manually.

The connection is read-only: your assistant can retrieve and analyze the data you are authorized to access, but it cannot create, update, or delete records.

## What you can do with RNTOR MCP

- **Network and locations:** Review network details, locations, addresses, contact information, configuration, and status.
- **Customers:** Look up members and companies, including account and membership information.
- **Plans and subscriptions:** Compare membership and resource plans, and review subscription status and renewal dates.
- **Resources and bookings:** Explore rooms, desks, offices, parking spaces, equipment, and booking activity.
- **Invoices and contracts:** Review invoice records, balances, contracts, and contract templates.
- **Shop and orders:** Browse shop categories and products, and review customer orders.
- **Benefits and applications:** Explore member benefits, benefit categories, and submitted applications.
- **Guides:** Access published member guides and operational information.

## Connect RNTOR MCP

You need an active RNTOR account with access to at least one network. Each connection uses RNTOR OAuth, and you choose the network the assistant is allowed to access.

### Connect in Claude

1. Open [RNTOR in the Claude connector directory](https://claude.ai/directory/rntor).
2. Select **Connect**, sign in to RNTOR, and choose the network you want to authorize.
3. Start a conversation in Claude and ask a question about your workspace.

### Connect in ChatGPT

1. Open ChatGPT and go to **Settings → Plugins**, then find RNTOR.
2. Install the RNTOR plugin and select **Connect**.
3. Sign in to RNTOR, choose the network you want to authorize, and start asking questions.

### Connect in another AI assistant

1. Open the assistant's MCP or connector settings.
2. Add a remote MCP server using this URL: `https://mcp.rntor.com/mcp`
3. Complete the RNTOR sign-in and network authorization flow.

Once connected, the assistant can use the same read-only RNTOR tools shown below.

## Security

### Access

- Every user signs in through RNTOR OAuth.
- The user selects the RNTOR network to connect.
- The assistant can access only data the signed-in user is authorized to view.

### Data

- RNTOR MCP retrieves data only when a user asks the assistant a question.
- The connection does not create, update, or delete RNTOR records.
- Disconnecting RNTOR from the assistant removes that assistant's access.

## FAQs

### Is there a limit to how much I can ask?

RNTOR MCP is designed for normal conversational use. If a temporary request limit is reached, wait briefly and try again.

### Can I connect RNTOR to more than one AI assistant or account?

Yes. Each connection is authorized separately, so you can connect RNTOR to multiple supported assistants or accounts and disconnect any of them independently.
