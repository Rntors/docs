RNTOR MCP connects AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT to your live workspace operations through the open [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io).

Ask operational questions in plain language instead of switching between dashboards or building one-off reports. RNTOR uses the signed-in user’s existing permissions and the workspace network selected during connection.

## What you can do

RNTOR provides **35 read-only tools** across the operational areas below:

- **Networks and locations** — view the networks and locations you are authorized to access.
- **Members and companies** — find customer records and account status while protecting restricted contact data.
- **Plans and subscriptions** — compare membership and resource plans, active subscriptions, and renewal dates.
- **Resources and bookings** — explore rooms, desks, offices, parking spaces, equipment, and bookings.
- **Invoices and contracts** — review invoice status and balances, contracts, and contract templates.
- **Shop and orders** — inspect categories, products, and orders across locations.
- **Benefits and applications** — review member benefits, categories, and submitted applications.
- **Guides** — retrieve published member guides and operational information.

<Note>
  RNTOR MCP is read-only. It cannot create, edit, delete, approve, charge, refund, sign, or trigger workflows in your RNTOR account.
</Note>

## Connect RNTOR MCP

Use the canonical server URL for every new connection:

```text
https://mcp.rntor.com/mcp
```

There are no API keys to copy. RNTOR uses OAuth: sign in, select the network you want to authorize, review the read-only permissions, and choose **Allow**.

<Tip>
  If you can access multiple RNTOR networks, each connection is restricted to the network selected during authorization. Disconnect and reconnect to switch the authorized network.
</Tip>

## Supported tools

The tools are organized by the business tasks workspace teams handle every day.

### Identity, networks, and locations

| Tool | What it returns | Example prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `getCurrentUser` | The signed-in identity used by the connection | “Which RNTOR account is connected?” |

| `getNetwork` | Details for one authorized network | “Show the key details for our network.” |
| `getLocations` | Locations available in the selected network | “List our locations and their addresses.” |

## Access and privacy

- Sign in with your RNTOR account.
- Choose the workspace network you want to connect.
- The assistant can only view information available to your RNTOR account in that network.
- The connector is read-only and cannot change your RNTOR data.

## FAQs

### Can RNTOR MCP change my data?

No. The current 35-tool surface only retrieves data.

### Which network can the assistant access?

Only the network selected on the RNTOR OAuth consent screen, and only within the signed-in user’s RNTOR permissions.

### Can I connect a test network?

Yes. Choose the appropriate environment when the connection or request offers that option.

### What happens if I reach a rate limit?

Reduce parallel requests, allow a short pause, and retry. RNTOR returns a standard `429` response when temporary limits apply.

### How do I end access?

Disconnect RNTOR in your AI assistant. You can reconnect later and authorize a network again.
