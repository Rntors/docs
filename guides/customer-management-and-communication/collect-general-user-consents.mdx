title: "Collect general user consents"
sidebarTitle: "Collect general user consents"
description: "Collect mandatory user consent to your Terms of use during the member onboarding flow, requiring acceptance before completing any bookings or purchases"
---

If you require all your users to agree to the general rules in the space, [add the text of this agreement to the terms of use](/admin/general/terms-of-use).

When a new user logs in to the member web portal or mobile app for the first time, they accept your Terms of use during [the onboarding process](/member/onboarding) and cannot proceed further without completing this step. By pressing **Continue**, they confirm that they have carefully read and agreed to the terms of use.

![Image](/images/guides-collect-general-user-consents-1.png)

<Info>
  Users can review the agreement in the Terms of use at any time on their **Account** page in the member web portal and mobile app.
</Info>

![Image](/images/guides-collect-general-user-consents-2.png)

If, apart from the general agreement, you need customers to sign individual contracts for office rental or membership, please refer to our [dedicated articles](/admin/billing/contracts/set-up-contracts) on the Contracts feature.
