A trial day is a perfect way for potential customers to explore your space before committing to a membership or booking. It gives them a chance to explore the atmosphere, test the amenities, and connect with the community. Whether they're considering coworking memberships, private offices, or event rentals, a trial day helps them make a confident, informed decision.

Below, we’ve outlined a few effective ways to offer trial days in RNTOR.

## Offer a free trial desk day with a promo code

A great way to introduce potential customers to your space is by offering a promo code for their first booking. This allows them to experience your coworking space risk-free and encourages future engagement.

To organize it, follow these steps:

1. [Create a coupon](/admin/billing/discounts/coupons-and-promo-codes) for the desk with the necessary discount (e.g., 100% for a free trial).
2. [Create a promo code](/admin/billing/discounts/coupons-and-promo-codes#create-promo-codes) to distribute to your potential customers. Ensure to enable the **Limited by first purchase** property.
3. Add the promo code to your website, newsletters, and social media to drive bookings through your member web portal or mobile apps.
4. Customers can easily [redeem the promo code](/admin/billing/discounts/coupons-and-promo-codes#create-promo-codes) during checkout.

![Organize trial days on RNTOR's coworking space management platform](/images/guides-organize-trial-days-1.png)

Offering a trial lowers the barrier for first-time users, helping them experience the value of your coworking space without commitment.

## Private office for a day

Another way to provide trial access is by offering an office for daily bookings. This approach allows businesses to try your coworking space in a structured way while generating revenue—even when the office isn’t leased to a company yet.

To set it up, follow these steps:

1. [Add an office](/admin/resource-management/resources/set-up-resources#desks) to the **Desks** page and set a daily rate.
2. Once added, users can [book](/member/bookings/book-a-desk) it on a daily basis in your member web portal or mobile apps.

![Image](/images/guides-organize-trial-days-2.png)

3. Promote this offer by highlighting this option on your website, newsletter, and social media to attract members looking for a flexible workspace.

It also helps your new customers experience the benefits of working from your space, increasing the likelihood of long-term office rental.

## Office for a long-time rental

If your space offers private offices, you can [list](/admin/resource-management/resources/set-up-resources#offices) them on the **Offices** page to allow potential renters apply for an office while also arranging a trial day. This solution can greatly help you with collecting the contacts of users who are interested in the private suites.

To organize it, follow these steps:

1. [Add your private suites](/admin/resource-management/resources/set-up-resources#offices) to the **Offices** page.
2. Once added, users interested in a long-term office rental will [apply](/admin/resource-management/resources/rental-applications-or-immediate-sign-ups) for them.

![Image](/images/guides-organize-trial-days-3.png)

The administrator [receives a support ticket](/admin/resource-management/resources/rental-applications-or-immediate-sign-ups) and can coordinate the visit date directly within the ticket. If the user or company decides to rent the office, the administrator simply [assigns](/admin/resource-management/resources/manage-resource-rentals-with-assignments) the private suite to them.
